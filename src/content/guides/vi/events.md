# Last War: Survival — Events Database

## VS Duel (Alliance Duel)
- **Format:** 6-day weekly (Mon-Sat), matched rival alliance
- **Daily Themes:**

| Day | Theme | Points | Key Actions |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Radar Training | 1 | Radar tasks, Secret Tasks, stamina |
| 2 | Base Expansion | 2 | Construction speedups, building upgrades |
| 3 | Age of Science | 2 | Research speedups, Valor Badges |
| 4 | Train Heroes | 2 | Hero EXP, recruit tickets, skill medals |
| 5 | Total Mobilization | 2 | Troop training, promotions |
| 6 | Enemy Buster | 4 | PvP kills, hospital healing, legendary trucks |

- **Win:** First to 7/13 points
- **Strategy:** Hoard & Burst — never consume speedups/EXP outside matching theme day
- **Reward:** Valor Badges

## SvS (Server vs Server)
- **Prep Phase:** Sun-Fri accumulate points
- **Invasion:** Saturday — higher prep = Invader, can teleport to defender warzone
- **Capital Clash:** Control central Capital + Defense Turrets, points per second of occupation
- **Reward:** Supreme President title + server-wide buff authority

## Desert Storm
- **Format:** Weekly 20v20 instanced battlefield
- **Troop Safety:** 100% safe — NO permanent troop death
- **Objectives:** Nuclear Silo (highest pts), Oil Refineries, Science Hub, Field Hospitals
- **Reward:** Honor Points (for Honor Store UR Gear Blueprints)

## Winter Storm
- **Format:** 5v5, 10 min max
- **Win:** First to 30,000 Battlefield Points
- **Objectives:** Nuclear Silo (100 pts/s), Oil Refineries (30 pts/s), Missile Centers (10 pts/s + debuff)
- **Troop Safety:** Zero permanent losses
- **Reward:** 10,000-16,500 Honor Points per cycle

## Enemy Buster Day (VS Day 6)
- **5x Multiplier:** Kills from rival alliance = 5x points
- **Tactics:**
  - Keep 24h shield active, drop only for coordinated strikes
  - Uncheck all squads from wall defense when offline → 0 troop loss if breached
  - Dispatch 4x Legendary Trade Trucks + Legendary Secret Tasks for free points
  - Heal in small batches (1-2h) for alliance help instant completions
  - Scout before attacking — never hit empty bases

## Ammo Bonanza
- **Mechanics:** Shoot targets using Ammo items
- **CRITICAL:** NEVER spend Ammo incrementally. Hoard 600+ Ammo across multiple events → spend all in one event for highest milestone
- **Best Rewards:** Mythic Blueprints, Upgrade Ore, EW Shards
