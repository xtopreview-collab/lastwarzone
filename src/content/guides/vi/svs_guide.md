# Server vs Server (SvS) Guide

Alliance Â· 2026-04-26

## What Is SvS?

Server vs Server (SvS) is the largest competitive event in Last War: Survival. Two servers are matched and fight for **6-7 days** in a points-based war. Every player on your server contributes â kills, captures, gathering, and event tasks all count toward the server's total. The winning server takes the largest rewards in the game.

SvS Wins on Coordination, Not Individual Power

**The strongest individual player on a disorganised server loses to a coordinated mid-tier player on a well-led server.** SvS rewards the alliance that follows orders, fills rallies, defends together, and hits high-value targets. Solo attempts waste troops against organised defenders. The single most important SvS skill is "do what the call says, on time."

## How SvS Matchmaking Works

Servers are matched against opponents of similar age and total power. The match is announced typically **5-7 days before SvS opens**, giving both sides preparation time. Once matched:

* Both servers can scout the other's map
* Cross-server chat becomes available (optional, often used for diplomacy)
* Migration windows freeze â players cannot leave a server during an active SvS cycle
* Internal server fighting should pause completely

## Pre-SvS Preparation Timeline

The preparation week is more important than the SvS days themselves. Servers that arrive prepared dominate. Servers that scramble in the final 24 hours lose.

### 1 Week Before â Diplomacy and Planning

* **Server-wide NAP enforcement** â all alliances on your server stop fighting each other. R5s coordinate through Discord or external chat
* **Identify server leadership** â usually the top 1-3 alliances run strategy. Smaller alliances follow their lead
* **Scout the enemy server** â identify high-value targets (top players, key alliances, major hives)
* **Tactics Cards / Coffee buffs** prepared for the active season â see [Tactics Cards Guide](/guides/season-4/tactics-cards-guide/) and [Caffeine Institute Guide](/guides/season-5/caffeine-guide/)

### 3-4 Days Before â Resource Stockpile

* **Shields stockpiled** â multi-day shields for everyone. SvS without shield = troop graveyard
* **Speedups banked** â healing, training, and construction. SvS will burn through them
* **Hospitals upgraded and emptied** â capacity for the troop losses you will take
* **Train replacement troops** â fill the queue Mon-Thu so casualties replace fast
* **Coordinate rally leaders** â top accounts lead rallies; mid accounts fill them

### 1-2 Days Before â Final Setup

* **Identify your role**: rally leader, rally filler, garrison defender, gatherer, scout
* **Pre-position teleports** if using bubble strategy â high-power players move toward border
* **Apply long shield** if you will sleep through any part of SvS
* **Charge devices and confirm sleep schedule** â knowing when your alliance is most active matters

### SvS Day 0 (the day before opening) â Quiet Day

* **No rallies, no captures, no aggression** â both sides are usually quiet to lock in setup
* **Final shield checks** for everyone
* **Final rally leader confirmations** in alliance chat / Discord

## SvS Day Structure

SvS typically runs 6 days with rotating themes. The exact schedule varies by version but the pattern is consistent:

Treat the Order as Indicative

**The day themes below are the usual pattern, not a published schedule.** The game names its Duel themes Base Expansion, Age of Science, Hero Initiative, Total Mobilization and Enemy Buster, but does not publish which weekday each falls on â and it can differ. Read the in-game schedule at the start of the week rather than planning from a fixed table.

DayTypical Theme

Day 1Building / Construction â points for upgrading buildings

Day 2Research â points for completing tech research

Day 3Hero / Gear â points for hero upgrades and gear levels

Day 4Training / Mobilisation â points for training troops (waterfall day)

Day 5Gathering / Resources â points for gathering on enemy territory

Day 6**Kill Day** â the decisive day. Massive point multipliers for kills

Day 6 Decides SvS

**Kill Day point multipliers are so large that 4-5x more points are scored on Day 6 than any other single day.** Servers can lose Days 1-5 and still win SvS by dominating Day 6. Conversely, servers that rest on a Day 1-5 lead get caught and passed if they take Day 6 lightly. Treat Days 1-5 as preparation â Day 6 is where the war is actually won.

## Point Sources by Day

### Days 1-5: Personal Activity Points

Every personal activity that matches the day's theme produces SvS points. The exact values shift between game versions but the relative weights hold:

* **Building upgrades** (Day 1) â high points for HQ pushes, especially ones queued and timed for the day
* **Research completion** (Day 2) â long research finishes count
* **Hero stat upgrades / Exclusive Weapons** (Day 3) â banked hero items spent today
* **Troop training** (Day 4) â T11 waterfall training is the best points-per-speedup day
* **Gathering on enemy territory** (Day 5) â sending marches to enemy resource tiles. Risky but high-value

### Day 6: Kill Points

Kill points are server-pooled. Every troop killed by your server scores. Common kill sources:

* **Rally targets** â large enemy rallies on weaker enemy bases. The biggest single kill events
* **Defending against rallies** â kills from defensive holds count for your server too
* **Solo attacks on shielded-down enemies** â opportunistic when you can find them
* **Reinforcement battles** â sending troops to defend an alliance member who is attacked

## SvS Day 6 â Hour-by-Hour

The single most important day. A well-run Day 6 plan looks like this:

* **00:00-04:00** â quiet hours for most servers. Apply shields, set defensive garrisons. Rallies typically pause
* **04:00-08:00** â early-morning probing. Some servers use this window for surprise rallies
* **08:00-12:00** â the morning push. Major rallies begin. Rally leaders coordinate timing
* **12:00-16:00** â mid-day peak. Most servers rally heavily here
* **16:00-20:00** â the decisive window. Most kills happen here. Maximum activity required
* **20:00-23:00** â late-day push. Last opportunities for kill points before the day closes
* **23:00-23:59** â final-minute kills. Some servers save big rallies for the closing window

Activate Coordination Tools Before Day 6

SvS Day 6 cannot be coordinated through in-game chat alone â too slow, too fragmented. **Discord, WhatsApp, or LINE is non-negotiable.** Voice chat is even better for the peak windows. Servers that try to coordinate purely through in-game chat lose to servers that pre-built external coordination habits during pre-season.

## Common SvS Mistakes

1. **Solo attacking organised defenders** â wastes troops with no point return. Always rally
2. **Not shielding when offline** â every unshielded base is a kill target. Even mid-power players
3. **Burning speedups Days 1-5 outside the matching theme** â Day 1 building speedups score, Day 2 research speedups score. Day 3 building speedups score nothing
4. **Skipping the prep week** â you cannot prepare for SvS in the final 24 hours
5. **Internal server fighting during SvS** â breaking the server NAP wastes troops you need against the enemy
6. **Rallying without confirming the leader's call** â premature rallies waste time. Wait for the formal "GO"
7. **Not reinforcing hive members under attack** â one ally lost weakens the whole hive's defence
8. **Trying to solo-rally Day 6** â full alliance rallies always outscore solo attempts
9. **Logging off during the 16:00-20:00 peak window** â the most important hours of the entire SvS week
10. **Treating Days 1-5 as the war** â Day 6 multipliers mean the war is on Day 6. Don't burn out early

## Cross-Server Diplomacy

SvS is competitive but some servers negotiate truces or specific terms:

* **Surrender SvS** â a weaker server may negotiate with the stronger one to lose gracefully and minimise troop losses. Both sides usually accept
* **Even-fight SvS** â equally matched servers sometimes agree to cap kill activity to specific windows so both sides can participate productively
* **Specific target embargos** â agreements not to attack designated players (often during real-life events like holidays)

These deals are negotiated at the R5 / server-leader level. Individual players follow whatever their server's leadership communicates.

## Post-SvS Recovery

After Day 6 closes:

* **Heal all wounded troops** â hospitals will be full
* **Rebuild lost troops** â waterfall training to recover army size
* **Replenish speedup stockpile** â for the next event cycle
* **Server diplomacy reset** â internal NAPs that were extended for SvS may need renegotiation
* **Reward claim** â claim every SvS milestone reward. Rewards include UR Hero Shards, Skill Medals, gear materials, and gems
