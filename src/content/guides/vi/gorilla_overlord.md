## What the Gorilla Overlord Is

The **Gorilla Overlord** is the first of the game's two **Overlords** (the second is the **Skybreaker**). An Overlord is a special unit that **joins one of your combat squads** and is strengthened on its own track â a **Bond Rating**, a **Promotion** (title/star) system, and its own **Skills** â separate from your regular heroes.

You don't summon it. You **rescue and heal it** through a short radar questline, build its Bond Rating with team training, and once the bond is high enough it can be **added to a squad** and fight alongside your heroes.

When Do You Get It?

It arrives around the **end of Season 2 / start of Season 3**. It's **questline-gated, not a fixed date** â it unlocks when you complete its rescue radar task (*Search Mission: 1*), so the exact timing varies by server. Treat "late S2" as your cue to start preparing. **Confirm the exact trigger in your client.**

## How You Unlock It (the flow)

1. **Rescue / heal it.** A radar questline appears â you send troops to gather "treatment supplies" and run a series of recovery search missions to restore the Overlord to strength. *These recovery supply methods stop once it's fully healed*, so run them while they're available.
2. **Build Bond Rating.** Do **team training** to raise the bond between the Overlord and your heroes. When the Bond Rating hits the required threshold, the Overlord **can join a squad in battle** â and this also **unlocks its Promotion and Skills systems**.
3. **Promote + level skills.** From there you raise its **Promotion title/stars** (which unlock higher skill stars) and level its **skills** â both on their own currencies (below).

Each Overlord sits in **one squad at a time**. Promotion raises its battlefield title (10 levels per title), which increases skill strength.

The "Armed Truck" IS the Overlord Expedition

The green **Armed Truck** that parks by your base is the entry to the **Overlord Expedition** â a **combat** stage-clear mode (with **Auto Battle** once you've cleared enough stages) that also pays **hourly idle rewards**. It's not a passive loot truck â it's one of your main sources of Overlord materials (Training Guidebooks, Overlord Skill Badges, Supply Chests). Push its stages for bigger hourly rewards.

## What to Save

Start banking these **before** the Overlord unlocks so it's immediately useful, and keep feeding them after:

| **Overlord Bond Badges** | Raising Bond Rating (the squad-join gate) | Team training, VS Day 5, events |

**Key sources to work every day:** the **exclusive supply radar tasks** (appear ~3Ã/day, up to 10), the **Overlord Expedition** idle rewards (auto-settle hourly, cap ~8 hours â collect before it caps), and **VS Day 5 "Total Mobilisation"**, which scores Bond Badges and Universal Overlord Shards (see the [Alliance Duel VS guide](/guides/general/vs-guide/)).

When to Start Saving

From **mid-to-late Season 2**, once the rescue questline is in sight: **stop spending Universal Overlord Shards and Bond Badges on anything else**, and bank Skill Badges and Training Guidebooks. The same logic as a UR-hero window (see [What to Save & When](/guides/general/what-to-save-and-when-guide/)) â prepare a season ahead so the Overlord lands strong instead of sitting at square one.

Half the cost is in the last ten levels

Getting an Overlord to level 50 costs **2,900** shards. The final ten levels cost **1,800 more** â more than everything from level 0 to 40 combined. And since there are two Overlords, maxing both is **9,400** shards.

That is the argument for taking one Overlord a long way rather than both partway, and for treating levels 50-60 as a genuine end-game project rather than the last stretch of a climb. Level 40 to 50 is where most accounts should be aiming first.

## The Second Overlord: Skybreaker (the Hawk)

There are **two Overlords in total** â the Gorilla is first, and the **Skybreaker** (a hawk, also called the **Hawk Overlord** â Meritt's companion from the desert season) is the **second, unlocked later**, around **Season 3**, on its own rescue questline. No third Overlord has appeared so far.

It works **exactly like the Gorilla** â rescue â build Bond Rating â joins a squad â Promotion + Skills â with one difference that matters for saving:

* It promotes with its **own Skybreaker shards** (separate from Gorilla shards), **but**
* it **shares** the **Universal Overlord Shards, Overlord Skill Badges and Bond Badges** with the Gorilla.

Don't Dump Everything Into the Gorilla

Because Skybreaker draws from the **same Universal shards and badges**, keep a **Universal-shard and Bond/Skill-Badge reserve** rather than pouring 100% into the Gorilla. When Skybreaker's questline arrives you'll want those Universals ready â the same "start a season ahead" habit applies. Its exact costs are **confirm-in-game** too.
