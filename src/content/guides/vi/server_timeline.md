# Server Timeline & Week-by-Week Roadmap

## How a Server Progresses

A Last War: Survival server unrolls features in a fixed order. Some unlocks are tied to **your HQ level**, some are tied to **server age** (days since the server opened), and a few are tied to **alliance state**. The exact day numbers vary by player activity and spending, but the order is consistent. This guide is a roadmap so you know what is coming, what to prepare for, and what unlocks gate the next stage.

Two Clocks Run in Parallel

**Your HQ level is your personal clock. The server age is everyone's clock.** Personal-clock features unlock the moment you hit the HQ requirement. Server-clock features open for everyone at the same time regardless of how strong any individual is. Understanding which clock gates a feature tells you whether to push HQ to unlock it or just wait â pushing HQ does nothing for server-clock events like Alliance Duel VS opening or the season starting.

Your climb is gated by what you can beat

Bosses, sieges and events decide how fast you move. Enter the heroes you own and make sure the squad you bring to them is the strongest five you have.

## Server Clock â Days Since Launch

These open for everyone at the same time, regardless of HQ level. The exact days vary slightly by server but the order is consistent.

### Week 1 (Days 1-7) â The Tutorial Window

* **Newbie Pack and 7-day login rewards** â extremely high value, do not miss
* **First Recharge bonuses** â if you intend to spend at all, the first $0.99 pack gives the best gem-per-dollar ratio in the game
* **Build Speedup events** â early server events shower speedups; bank them for HQ pushes
* **Goal: HQ 10-12 by end of Week 1** for an average pace player

### Week 2 (Days 8-14) â Drone Center Opens

* **Drone Center** unlocks around here â **HQ 15** and **Campaign Stage 38** are both cited as the gate; most active players clear both in week 2
* **Sky Predator** event begins â daily zombie boss with strong rewards
* **Hero Trial** opens â daily PvE rotation worth running every day
* **Wall of Honor** rally tracking goes live for those at HQ 18+
* **Goal: HQ 16-18, T7 troops, Drone Center building started**

### Week 3 (Days 15-21) â Marshal's Guard and Zombie Siege

* **Marshal's Guard** event window â top alliance fights for the title with major buffs
* **Zombie Siege** opens for alliance defence coordination
* **Cross-Server Hero Trial** rotates in
* **Alliance Tech** starts mattering â donate daily for the buffs
* **Goal: HQ 20, T8 troops, first Drone Lv5+**

### Week 4 (Days 22-28) â Alliance Duel VS Opens

* **Alliance Duel VS** â the flagship 6-day event most servers see in week 4
  + Day 1: Radar | Day 2: Building | Day 3: Research | Day 4: Heroes | Day 5: Mobilisation | Day 6: Combat
  + See the [Alliance Duel VS Guide](/guides/general/vs-guide/) for full mechanics
* **First Cross-Server Wars** depending on server age and matching
* **Hot Deals** rotate every few hours â the [Hot Deals Guide](/guides/general/hot-deals-guide/) covers what is worth grabbing
* **Goal: HQ 22-24, Drone Lv10+, full T8 squad**

### Weeks 5-9 (Days 29-63) â Mid-Game Grind

* **Capitol Conquest** starts gating major alliance ambitions
* **Cross-Server Wall of Honor** opens for top-server coordination
* **HQ 25-30 push** â this is where most accounts plateau if they did not bank correctly
* **Exclusive Weapons** become a meaningful power source from HQ 25
* **Goal: HQ 27-30, T9 troops, Profession System started**

### ~Day 70 â Pre-Season Ends, Season 1 Begins

The biggest single day on the calendar. The pre-season map closes, players are migrated to a Season 1 server, and the **Crimson Plague** mechanics open. From here, the server will rotate through seasons:

* **[Pre-Season 1 Timeline](/guides/pre-season-1/timeline-guide/)** â the foundation runway, Tank meta, week-by-week to HQ 30
* **[Season 1: The Crimson Plague](/guides/season-1/timeline-guide/)** â Exclusive Weapons, T10 troops, updated squad meta
* **[Season 2: Polar World](/guides/season-2/timeline-guide/)** â temperature management, Rare Soil ranking
* **[Season 3: Golden Realm](/guides/season-3/timeline-guide/)** â Curse Resistance, Desert Protectors, Goldust Conquest
* **[Season 4: Evernight Isle](/guides/season-4/timeline-guide/)** â Lighthouse, Tactics Cards, Copper War, T11 troops
* **[Season 5: Wild West](/guides/season-5/timeline-guide/)** â Bank Strongholds, CrystalGold economy
* **[Season 6: Lost Rainforest](/guides/season-6/timeline-guide/)** â faction warfare, Altars, City Destruction, Hero Awakening

Each season runs roughly 60-90 days and ends with a transition to the next. Your account, heroes, and HQ level carry forward; the map and seasonal mechanics reset. Click into the per-season timeline above for the week-by-week plan for whatever season your server is currently on.

Pre-Season Is Your Setup Phase

Pre-season exists to get you to Season 1 with a strong account. **Do not stockpile heavily expecting pre-season to last forever.** Use your speedups and resources to push HQ â the migration carries account state, but no in-game event is worth letting your HQ stall to bank for. Players who hit Season 1 at HQ 25+ have a permanent advantage over those who arrive at HQ 18.

## Arms Race Runs Continuously

[Arms Race](/guides/general/arms-race-guide/) runs in 4-hour slots, six per day, every day. The phase rotation (Radar / Construction / Tech / Heroes / Mobilization) is independent of the weekly cycle and runs from server day one â start engaging with it the moment you can to build the badge habit.

## Common Timeline Mistakes

1. **Stalling HQ to "save resources"** â you save resources to push HQ, not the other way around. A stalled HQ is wasted time
2. **Skipping early events because you "are not ready"** â server events have generous reward floors. Participating at any level beats not participating
3. **Joining an alliance late** â alliance help, alliance tech, and alliance events are too valuable to miss for any reason. Join on day one
4. **Spending speedups on whatever timer is currently running** â speedups are most valuable on Day 5 of VS (4x training multiplier). Save for VS Day 5 from week 4 onward
5. **Treating pre-season like the main game** â pre-season is your runway. Use it to set up, not to settle
6. **Not preparing for Drone Center at HQ 15** â Drone parts come from limited sources. Players who hit HQ 15 with parts already saved jump levels immediately
