## How HQ Progression Works

Your Headquarters (HQ) level gates everything in the game. Higher HQ means higher building levels, better troops, stronger heroes (hero level cap increases by +5 per HQ level), and access to more systems.

Before each HQ upgrade, you must meet specific building requirements. Always check what is needed for the next HQ level and work toward those prerequisites first.

**The single biggest rule: never let your builder sit idle.** Always have something upgrading.

Your climb is gated by what you can beat

Bosses, sieges and events decide how fast you move. Enter the heroes you own and make sure the squad you bring to them is the strongest five you have.

**Key takeaways:**

* **Tech Center is required at every level from HQ 8 onward** â always one level behind your target HQ, so start it early. It's the biggest bottleneck in the game.
* **Costs escalate sharply after HQ 20** â save your resource chests for this phase.
* **Build times are brutal from HQ 25+** â base times run into weeks, then months (HQ 35 â 149 days base). Construction-speed buffs, alliance help and speedups are essential.

## HQ 1-10: Sprint Phase

**Goal:** Blast through this as fast as possible. Resources are plentiful and timers are short.

### Priorities

1. **Upgrade HQ non-stop** â use speedups freely here, they are cheap at this stage
2. **Follow the tutorial** â it gives excellent rewards
3. **Tech Center** â keep it matching your HQ. It is the biggest bottleneck throughout the game
4. **Barracks** â keep within 1-2 levels of HQ
5. **Join an alliance on day one**

### What NOT to Do

* Do not upgrade resource production buildings beyond what is required for HQ prerequisites
* Do not upgrade the Material Workshop
* Do not spend gems on anything â save for VIP
* Do not worry about squad composition yet â use whatever heroes you have

## HQ 11-20: Foundation Phase

**Goal:** Build your first proper squad. Get into a rhythm of daily progression.

This is where the game slows down. Upgrades take hours instead of minutes. Get used to planning ahead.

### Building Priority

1. **HQ prerequisites** â always work toward whatever buildings are needed for the next HQ upgrade
2. **Tech Center** â must match HQ level. This unlocks troop tiers and research
3. **Hospital** â keep capacity high. Troops that exceed hospital capacity after a battle die permanently
4. **Barracks and Drill Grounds** â keep within 1-2 levels of HQ
5. **Walls** â required at multiple HQ tiers

### Research (Tech Center)

Your Tech Center unlocks research trees. Priority order:

1. **Troop upgrades** â higher troop tiers are dramatically stronger than lower ones
2. **Combat stats** for your primary squad type
3. **Construction speed** and **research speed** bonuses
4. **March-related research** later

### Squad Building

By HQ 15 you should be building toward a mono-type squad. In pre-season, **Tank is the dominant meta**:

* Tank heroes are the easiest UR heroes to get early
* The recommended early squad is **Murphy, Williams, Kimberly, Stetmann, Marshall**
* Focus all hero investment on this squad
* See the Tank Squad Guide for full details

### The Drone

After Stage 38 you unlock the Drone Center. Start upgrading it immediately â it provides global stat bonuses to your entire squad.

## HQ 21-25: Growth Phase

**Goal:** Max out your primary squad. Start accumulating gold gear materials. Prepare for the push to HQ 30.

### Building Priority

1. **HQ prerequisites** â always first
2. **Tech Center** â always matching HQ
3. **Hospital** â capacity should cover your full army
4. **Military buildings** matching your primary squad type

### Resource Crunch

Around HQ 25 you will notice resources getting tight. Building costs jump significantly.

* **Do not waste resources on unnecessary building upgrades** â only upgrade what is needed for HQ or your squad
* **Save resource chests** â do not open them until you need to spend immediately
* **Participate in every event** â events are now your primary resource source

### Gold Gear

Around HQ 22-25 you can start working toward gold (legendary) gear for your primary squad heroes.

* **Do not craft randomly** â follow the gear craft order in the Gear Guide
* **Focus on your main DPS hero first** (Kimberly for Tank squads)
* **Buy Legendary Gear Blueprints from the Honor Shop** â this is the single best use of Honor Points

### Planning Ahead

Check the requirements for HQ 26-30. The biggest bottleneck is typically the Tech Center and specific building levels. Start those upgrades early so you are not blocked.

## HQ 25-30: Late Pre-Season

**Goal:** Reach HQ 30. Build gold gear for your primary squad. Prepare for Season 1.

### Building Priority

1. **HQ to 30** â this is the finish line for pre-season
2. **Tech Center maxed** â unlocks T10 troops at level 30, which are a massive power jump
3. **All prerequisite buildings** â clear the path to HQ 30
4. **Hospital** â max capacity

### T10 Troops

When your Tech Center hits level 30, you unlock Tier 10 troops. These are dramatically stronger than lower-tier troops and are one of the biggest power spikes in the game. Getting here should be a high priority.

### Oil (After Season 2 â Not Yet)

Oil is **not** part of pre-season or Season 1. It arrives with the **"Age of Oil"** off-season **after Season 2**, which is when the HQ cap finally lifts past 30 (toward 35) and oil becomes the primary resource bottleneck. Through pre-season, Season 1 and Season 2 you run on Food / Iron / Coin â don't plan around oil until then.

### Squad Status at HQ 30

By HQ 30 your primary squad should be:

* All 5 heroes well-levelled with skills invested
* Working toward full gold gear (see Gear Guide for craft order)
* T10 troops unlocked and training
* Drone at a decent level

You may be starting to think about a second squad (Aircraft) for the mid-game meta transition â but do not start investing until your primary squad is in a strong position.

## General Tips for All Levels

* **Never let your builder idle** â this is the single biggest factor in progression speed
* **Never let research idle** â same principle
* **Save gems for VIP** â VIP 6 gives you a second builder queue, which effectively doubles your building speed
* **Do not open resource chests early** â resources in your base can be stolen. Items in your inventory cannot
* **Plan for VS (Building Day)** â save your bigger upgrades for the Arms Race building day for extra event points
* **Use the "Next 2 HQ levels" rule** â before starting a long upgrade, check the requirements for your next two HQ levels and start the slowest prerequisite first
