## How Hero Leveling Works

Your hero level is capped by your HQ level. **Each HQ level adds +5 to the hero level cap.** This means your HQ is always the bottleneck â push HQ first, then level heroes to the new cap.

The Golden Rule

**Always max your main DPS hero to the level cap before touching anyone else.** A Kimberly *at cap* with four teammates 20 levels short will outperform five heroes all 10 levels short, every single time. Concentrated investment creates power spikes â scattered investment creates mediocrity.

## Step-by-Step Leveling Process

When your HQ upgrades and the hero level cap increases by 5, follow this process:

1. **Level Hero #1 to the new cap** â do not touch anyone else until this is done
2. **Level Hero #2 to the new cap** â only after Hero #1 is maxed
3. **Level Hero #3 to the new cap**
4. **Level Hero #4 to the new cap**
5. **Level Hero #5 to the new cap**
6. **Push HQ to the next level** â then repeat from step 1

Why This Order Matters

**Hero stats scale non-linearly.** The last five levels before your cap are worth more than five levels in the middle of the climb. Maxing your DPS hero first means your squad's damage output is always as high as possible. A carry at the level cap hits dramatically harder than one that is 10-15 levels behind â those missing levels represent thousands of attack power that multiplies with gear, skills, and star upgrades.

## Common Leveling Mistakes

1. **Spreading EXP evenly across all 5 heroes** â this is the most common mistake. Five heroes 10 levels short of cap lose to a squad with a carry *at* cap and four supports 15 short
2. **Leveling heroes you do not use** â every EXP item spent on a benched hero is wasted. Only level your active squad
3. **Leveling your second squad before your first is maxed** â one maxed squad beats two half-leveled ones
4. **Hoarding EXP items indefinitely** â EXP items do not gain value over time. Use them on your priority hero as soon as the cap increases. The exception is saving for VS Thursday (Day 4) for Alliance Duel points
5. **Forgetting to collect idle rewards** â idle EXP caps out. Collect at least twice daily or you lose free EXP
6. **Leveling tanks before DPS** â your tank's job is to survive, and they can do that at lower levels. Your DPS's job is to kill, and they need every point of attack power

## Skill Leveling Priority

Hero level and skill level are separate investments. While leveling heroes in the order above, also follow this skill priority:

1. **Main DPS Tactics skill to Level 40** â Kimberly's Barrage Strike, DVA's Steel Barrage, Tesla's Lightning Chain
2. **Main DPS passive skills** â these multiply the Tactics skill damage
3. **Main tank defensive skills** â Murphy's front-row damage reduction, Carlie's energy damage reduction
4. **Support buff skills** â Marshall's critical rate buff, Morrison's defence shred
5. **All remaining skills to Level 30** â bring everyone to a baseline
6. **Push all remaining skills to Level 40** â same priority order as hero leveling
