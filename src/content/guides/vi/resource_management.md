**Key insight:** Most of your resources should come from **events and activities**, not from passive production buildings. This is why resource production buildings are low priority for upgrades.

## The Golden Rule: Spend Before You Log Off

Resources sitting in your base can be stolen by attackers. Resources in your inventory (as chests/items) cannot.

**Before you close the game:**

1. Start a building upgrade
2. Start a research project
3. Queue troop training
4. If you still have excess, shield up

> **Never open resource chests until the moment you need to spend them.** A resource chest in your inventory is safe. Resources in your stockpile are a target.

## Where Resources Actually Come From

New players often think resource production buildings are important. They are not â at least not early on.

### Primary Sources (Focus Here)

* **Events** â alliance mobilisation, Arms Race, and other events reward massive resource packs
* **Daily activities** â completing daily tasks gives steady resource income
* **Campaign stages** â progressing through PvE content drops resources
* **Zombie hunts** â killing zombies on the world map
* **Gathering tiles** â sending marches to resource tiles on the map

### Secondary Sources (Do Not Over-Invest)

* **Resource production buildings** â keep them low level. They produce resources passively but the output is small compared to events
* **Alliance donations** â contribute to alliance research for long-term buffs
* **Trading** â when available

## Resource Protection

### Shields

* Shields prevent all attacks on your base for a set duration
* Use 8-hour shields overnight
* Use 24-hour shields when you know you will be away
* **Never drop your shield during a kill event if you have resources**

### Alliance Protection

* Stay near your alliance hive â members can reinforce your base
* Coordinate shield times with your alliance
* Call for help immediately if attacked while online

### Resource Chests vs Stockpile

Think of resource chests as a savings account and your stockpile as a current account:

* Keep your stockpile low (spend it on builds, research, training)
* Keep your savings high (hold resource chests in inventory)
* Only open chests when you are about to spend immediately

## Spending Priority

When you have resources, spend them in this order:

1. **HQ upgrade** â if prerequisites are met
2. **Tech Center upgrade** â the most important building after HQ
3. **Other HQ prerequisites** â whatever is blocking your next HQ level
4. **Research** â keep research running at all times
5. **Troop training** â keep queues running
6. **Other buildings** â only if nothing else needs resources

## Common Resource Mistakes

1. **Opening all resource chests at once** â you are painting a target on yourself
2. **Upgrading resource production buildings too high** â poor return on investment early
3. **Not participating in events** â free resources left on the table
4. **Hoarding resources without a shield** â either spend them or protect them
5. **Upgrading the Material Workshop** â costs a lot, gives very little. One of the most common traps for new players
6. **Spending resources on non-essential buildings** â only upgrade what is needed for HQ or your squad
