# Last War: Survival — Seasons Overview

## Season 1: The Crimson Plague
- **Theme:** Global viral outbreak
- **Core Mechanics:** Virus Resistance, Protein Farms, Virus Research Institute, Territory Expansion, Builder Alliance Mode
- **Meta:** EW unlocked at HQ25+, T10 troops via Special Forces tech, Mason SSR→UR promotion, Aircraft meta begins (DVA, Carlie, Morrison, Schuyler, Lucius)
- **Post-Season:** Queen's Revenge PvE event

## Season 2: Polar World (Polar Storm)
- **Theme:** Arctic survival
- **Core Mechanics:** Temperature System, High-Heat Furnace, -20°C lockout, Coal & Titanium Alloy resources, Rare Soil ranking
- **Meta:** Aircraft dominance (Carlie EW), Missile emergence (Tesla, McGregor, Fiona, Swift)
- **Post-Season:** Age of Oil unlocked → HQ 31-35 pathway

## Season 3: Golden Realm
- **Theme:** Ancient Egypt / Cursed desert
- **Core Mechanics:** Curse Resistance, Curse Research Lab, Sacred Water, Desert Protectors (troop recycling), Mithril, Goldust Conquest (Wed/Sat PvP)
- **Meta:** Scarlett SSR→UR promotion (Tank boost), Marshall EW, Mithril scarcity forces troop specialization

## Season 4: Evernight Isle
- **Theme:** Japanese dark archipelago
- **Core Mechanics:**
  - **Lighthouse & Electricity:** Fuel generators, maintain power grids, darkness = debuffs
  - **Tactical Cards:** Profession Hall — 2 Core + 4 Battle + 4 Resource cards (10 total). Core Cards are PERMANENT after S4
  - **Blood Night:** 3x daily (02:30, 10:30, 18:30 server time), 30 min each, buffed zombies + Oni Generals
  - **Copper War:** Bi-weekly faction war for copper reserves + city control
- **Meta:** T11 troops via Armament Institute (requires HQ35 + Barracks Lv.35)

## Season 5: Wild West
- **Theme:** Frontier economy
- **Core Mechanics:** CrystalGold banking, Caffeine Institute, Coffee Factories, Fortuna Trade Train
- **Meta:** Type Mastery, Tactical Weapons, T11 Armament Institute focus
