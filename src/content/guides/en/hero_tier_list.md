# Last War Hero Tier List — Season 3-6 Meta Rankings

> Updated September 2026 · Covers Season 3 through Season 6 meta

Choosing the right heroes is the single biggest decision in Last War. A well-built squad of five synergistic heroes will crush a random collection of "good" heroes every time. This guide ranks every hero, explains *why* they're ranked there, and gives you a clear investment path.

|---|---|---|---|

## Recommended Squad Compositions

### 🟢 Tank Squad (F2P / Low-Spender — Build This First)

```
FRONTLINE                BACKLINE
┌─────────────┐          ┌─────────────┐
│ [1] Williams │          │ [3] Kimberly │ ← Main Carry
│ [2] Murphy   │          │ [4] Marshall │ ← Buffer
└─────────────┘          │ [5] Stetmann │ ← Co-DPS
                          └─────────────┘
```

**Why this works:** Williams and Murphy form the tankiest frontline in the game, buying time for Kimberly to cycle her AoE rockets. Marshall multiplies everyone's crit damage. Stetmann adds burst on the backline.

**Budget alternative:** Swap Stetmann for UR Mason in early seasons. Mason is easier to 5-star and deals solid PvE damage.

**Formation bonus:** 5 Tank heroes = +20% HP, ATK, and DEF. This single bonus is worth more than any "better" hero from another type.

### 🔵 Aircraft Squad (High-Investment PvP Dominator)

```
FRONTLINE                BACKLINE
┌─────────────┐          ┌─────────────┐
│ [1] Lucius   │          │ [3] DVA      │ ← Main Carry
│ [2] Carlie   │          │ [4] Morrison │ ← Def Shredder
└─────────────┘          │ [5] Schuyler │ ← CC/Stun
                          └─────────────┘
```

**Why this works:** Lucius and Carlie completely neutralize Tank energy damage. DVA's lasers pierce through frontline to snipe backline carries. Morrison shreds armor so DVA hits harder. Schuyler stuns enemy Kimberly before she can cast.

**When to build:** Season 3+ when you have the resources for a second maxed squad. Aircraft hard-counters the 85%+ Tank user base.

### 🔴 Missile Squad (Whale Anti-Aircraft)

```
FRONTLINE                BACKLINE
┌─────────────┐          ┌─────────────┐
│ [1] McGregor │          │ [3] Tesla    │ ← Main Carry
│ [2] Adam     │          │ [4] Fiona    │ ← Anti-Air
└─────────────┘          │ [5] Swift    │ ← Executioner
                          └─────────────┘
```

**Why this works:** Tesla's chain lightning obliterates Aircraft squads with +20% type advantage. Fiona adds another +25% anti-air bonus. Swift finishes off weakened targets. McGregor's taunt protects the backline.

**When to build:** Season 4-5+, specifically to counter high-spending Aircraft players in cross-server wars.

## The Rock-Paper-Scissors Meta

Understanding the type cycle is critical for PvP:

```
     🟢 TANK
    ╱        ╲
   ╱ +20% dmg ╲
  ╱     bonus   ╲
🔴 MISSILE ←───── 🔵 AIRCRAFT
         +20% dmg
          bonus
```

- **Tank beats Missile** (+20% damage bonus)
- **Missile beats Aircraft** (+20% damage bonus)
- **Aircraft beats Tank** (+20% damage bonus)

### How the Meta Evolves Per Season

| Season | Dominant Meta | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-Season – S2 | 90% Tank squads | Easiest to build, Murphy/Kimberly accessible |
| S3 – S4 | High spenders pivot to Aircraft | Aircraft hard-counters the dominant Tank base |
| S4 – S5 | Whales build Missile | Missile hard-counters Aircraft spenders |
| S5+ | True Rock-Paper-Scissors | All three types active in Capitol Wars |

## Hero Investment Priority (F2P Path)

1. **Kimberly to 5-star + EW Level 20** — Non-negotiable. This is the single biggest power spike in the game. The +7.5% squad-wide stat bonus at EW 20 alone is worth more than starring up two other heroes.

2. **Murphy to 5-star** — Your frontline anchor. If Murphy dies in the first 8 seconds, your Kimberly never gets to cast her ult.

3. **Williams to 5-star** — Paired with Murphy, makes your frontline nearly unkillable.

4. **Marshall to 5-star** — His squad-wide crit/ATK buff is a direct multiplier on Kimberly's damage.

5. **Stetmann to 5-star + EW** — Co-DPS. His EW (Season 5) brings him back to parity with Kimberly for damage.

> ⚠️ **Golden Rule:** Never split resources between types. A full 5-star Tank squad beats a rainbow squad of 3-star "better" heroes every single time.

## TL;DR

- **Build full Tank first** — Williams, Murphy, Kimberly, Marshall, Stetmann/Mason
- **Kimberly is queen** — 5-star + EW20 before anything else
- **Never mix types** — pure squad +20% bonus > individual hero quality
- **Aircraft is your second squad** — build it in Season 3-4 to counter Tanks
- **Missile is endgame** — only for whales countering Aircraft whales
