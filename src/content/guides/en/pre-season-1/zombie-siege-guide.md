# Zombie Siege (Pre-Season 1)

Alliance · 2026-03-11

## Zombie Siege in Pre-Season

Zombie Siege follows the same 20-wave structure during pre-season, but your alliance is weaker, less coordinated, and more vulnerable. The key difference is that your margin for error is much smaller — wrong difficulty selection or poor reinforcement coordination can result in mass eliminations far earlier than in a developed alliance.

For the full event mechanics, see the [Zombie Siege Guide](/guides/general/zombie-siege-guide/).

Start Low, Learn Fast

**In pre-season, pick a difficulty lower than you think you need.** Clearing all 20 waves at a low difficulty gives better rewards than failing at wave 12 on a higher one. You can always increase difficulty next time once you know your alliance's real strength.

## What Is Different in Pre-Season

### Weaker Individual Defences

* Heroes are lower level with fewer skill upgrades
* Troops are lower tier with less HP and damage
* Gold gear is rare — most members have purple gear or below
* Defence formations are not optimised yet

### Less Alliance Coordination

* Members may not understand the event mechanics
* Communication habits are still forming
* Reinforcement coordination may be disorganised
* Some members will forget to set their defence squad

### Bigger Power Gaps

* The gap between your strongest and weakest members is often largest in pre-season
* Your top 5 players may survive easily while your bottom 15 get knocked out by wave 8 (each member gets 2 failed defences before elimination)
* This makes reinforcement strategy critical — without it, you lose most of your alliance early

## Pre-Season Difficulty Selection

Alliance StrengthRecommended Difficulty

Most members below HQ 15Lowest available difficulty. Focus on learning the event, not pushing rewards

Mixed HQ 15-20Low-medium difficulty. Your strong players can carry but weak players will need reinforcements

Most members HQ 20+Medium difficulty. Your alliance should clear all 20 waves with proper coordination

Strong pre-season allianceMedium-high. Push the difficulty gradually as you learn your alliance's limits

**Rule of thumb:** If more than 5 members get eliminated before wave 15, your difficulty is too high.

## Pre-Season Reinforcement Strategy

Reinforcements are even more critical in pre-season because the power gaps between members are larger.

### Before the Event

1. **Identify your weakest 5-10 members** — these are the players who will need help
2. **Assign 2-3 strong players as reinforcement providers** — they need to know who they are responsible for
3. **Brief weak members:** "Set your defence squad. Accept all reinforcements. Stay online"

### During the Event

Wave RangeReinforcement Action

Waves 1-5No reinforcements needed. Let everyone defend solo — early reinforcements steal personal points from the player being reinforced

Waves 6-8Start reinforcing your weakest 2-3 members who have already failed one defence. Send Tank squads for durability

Waves 9-12Reinforce anyone who reported taking heavy damage. Spread reinforcements across at-risk bases

Waves 13-20All available reinforcements go to the weakest surviving members. Focus on keeping as many alive as possible

Reinforce Early

**Do not wait until a base is about to fall.** In pre-season, weaker members can go from "fine" to "eliminated" in a single wave. Send reinforcements by wave 6-8 to the weakest members, not wave 12 when it is already too late.

### Tank Players as Reinforcement Providers

In pre-season, most of your alliance runs Tank squads. This is actually an advantage for Zombie Siege:

* Tank squads are the best at garrison defence — they absorb the most damage
* Send your Tank heroes to reinforce weak members
* Even a mid-strength Tank garrison can keep a weak base alive through several extra waves

## Pre-Season Preparation Checklist

Send this to your alliance before every Zombie Siege:

1. **Set your defence squad on your wall** — your strongest formation assigned to base defence
2. **Recall ALL troops** — gathering marches, rally troops, everything comes home
3. **Heal all wounded troops** — maximum strength before wave 1
4. **Stop all other activities** — no gathering, no rallies, no drone missions
5. **Stay online** — if you can, be available for the full event
6. **If you are weak, ask for help now** — do not wait until you are about to be eliminated

## What to Do If You Are Eliminated

If your base falls during the event:

* Your reinforcement troops at other bases **return to you** — this frees up reinforcement capacity
* You cannot re-enter the event for that round
* Learn from it — what wave did you fall on? What could you do differently?
* **Help your alliance from chat** — call out who needs reinforcements, coordinate for those still fighting

## Building Toward Later Seasons

Zombie Siege becomes easier as your alliance grows:

* **Higher hero levels and skills** mean better individual defence
* **Better gear** increases survivability substantially
* **More squad diversity** gives more reinforcement options
* **Better coordination** comes from practising the event repeatedly in pre-season
* **Higher Alliance Gift levels** provide passive bonuses that help everyone

The experience you gain now — coordinating reinforcements, choosing difficulty, communicating during the event — makes your alliance stronger for every future Zombie Siege.

## Common Pre-Season Zombie Siege Mistakes

1. **Difficulty too high** — the most common pre-season mistake. Start low and increase gradually
2. **Members forget to set defence squad** — remind them every single time. This never stops being an issue
3. **No reinforcements sent** — strong players survive solo while weak members get eliminated early
4. **Reinforcements sent too late** — waiting until wave 12 to help someone struggling since wave 6
5. **Troops out on gathering marches** — members lose because half their army is on a resource tile
6. **No advance notice** — members log in to find the event already running and they are unprepared
7. **Strong players not helping** — individual survival means less than alliance-wide wave completion
