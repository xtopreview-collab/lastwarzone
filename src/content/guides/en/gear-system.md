# The Ultimate Gear System & Exclusive Weapons Guide

In **Last War: Survival Game**, your squad’s power is defined as much by their equipment as their levels and stars. The gear system, culminating in the ultra-powerful **Mythic Gear** and **Exclusive Weapons**, is where true endgame strength lies. This comprehensive guide covers everything from the basics of crafting to the strategic nuances of Mythic passive skills and optimal upgrade priorities.

---

## 1. The Gear System Basics: Rarity and Slots

Before diving into advanced crafting, it is crucial to understand the foundational structure of gear in the game.

### Rarity Hierarchy

Gear progresses through several rarity tiers. While lower tiers serve as stepping stones, your ultimate goal is building and perfecting **Legendary** and **Mythic** equipment.

| Tier / Color | In-Game Naming Convention | Max Gear Level | Function / Lifecycle Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **White / Grey** | Basic / Tutorial Equipment | Lv 5–10 | Early tutorial baseline; rapidly dismantled. |
| **Green** | Common (M1 / M2 Series) | Lv 10 | Early-game placeholder; dismantle for refined iron. |
| **Blue** | Rare (M3 Series) | Lv 15 | Temporary bridge; dismantle once Purple gear arrives. |
| **Purple** | Epic (M4 / M4-A Series) | Lv 30 | Mid-game transition gear; do not heavily upgrade past Lv 20. |
| **Orange / Gold** | **Legendary (M5 / M5-A Series)** | **Lv 40** | **The foundational end-game gear.** Craftable in Gear Factory; eligible for Star Promotion. |
| **Red** | **Mythic (M5-A 5-Star Promoted)** | **Lv 40 (+5)** | **Ultimate pinnacle tier.** Promoted from 4-star Legendary using 10 Mythic Red Blueprints. Unlocks unique passive skills. |

### The Four Gear Slots

Every hero has four equipment slots divided into offensive and defensive halves.

![Hero Gear Layout](/images/gear/hero-gear-layout.png)

* **Offensive Slots (Left Side)**
  * **Railgun / Heavy Cannon (Gun):** Boosts Attack. Essential for primary damage dealers (Kimberly, DVA, Tesla, etc.) as it directly scales skill damage.
  * **Data Chip (Tactical Chip):** Provides Attack and critical hit mechanics (chance/damage). Great synergy with heroes that naturally crit.
* **Defensive Slots (Right Side)**
  * **Reactive Armor (Armor):** Grants high flat Physical Defense and Base HP. Protects against physical/auto-attack damage.
  * **Tactical Radar (Radar):** Grants Energy Defense and Base HP. Currently the most crucial defensive item, mitigating deadly energy burst attacks from top-tier carries.

## 2. Crafting & Material Economy

The **Gear Factory** (unlocked at HQ Level 9) is where you craft your equipment. The most important rule of the Gear Factory: **Stop upgrading it at Level 20.** Past Level 20, the resource costs heavily outweigh the minor production speed benefits.

![Gear Factory Crafting](/images/gear/gear-factory-craft.webp)

### The 4-to-1 Conversion Rule

Materials follow a strict 4-to-1 synthesis ratio:
* 4 Screws = 1 Refined Iron Module
* 4 Refined Iron Modules = 1 Superalloy (16 Screws)
* 4 Superalloys = 1 Synthetic Resin (64 Screws)
* 4 Synthetic Resins = 1 Dielectric Ceramic (256 Screws)

### Legendary Gear Crafting Costs

Crafting **Legendary (Gold M5-A)** gear requires **750 Dielectric Ceramic** per piece. This is equivalent to an astonishing **192,000 Screws**. Since your base workshops cannot generate this alone, you must actively farm gear chests from Secret Tasks, Truck Plunder, Alliance Duels, and the VIP Shop.

## 3. Enhancement and Star Promotion

![Legendary M5-A Gear Set](/images/gear/m5a-full-set.webp)

### Leveling (Levels 1 to 40)
Upgrading gear requires **Upgrade Ore** and **Gold Coins**. Maxing a piece from 0 to 40 costs roughly 200,000 Upgrade Ore. 
* **Pro Tip:** Gear receives significant base stat multipliers at Levels 10, 20, 30, and 40. Always level in increments of 10!

### Star Promotion (Stars 1 to 5)
Once Legendary gear reaches Level 40, you can promote it using Legendary Blueprints. (Unlocks at Gear Factory Level 20).

* **1 Star:** 5 Legendary Blueprints
* **2 Stars:** +10 Legendary Blueprints (15 total)
* **3 Stars:** +15 Legendary Blueprints (30 total)
* **4 Stars:** +20 Legendary Blueprints (50 total)
* **5 Stars (Mythic):** **10 Mythic (Red) Blueprints**

## 4. The Mythic (Red) Tier

Promoting gear to 5 Stars transforms it into Mythic Gear, granting a devastating passive ability. 

![Mythic Promotion](/images/gear/gear-mythic-promotion-red.jpg)

### Mythic Passive Abilities

| Gear Piece | Mythic Passive Effect | Competitive Rating |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tactical Radar** | -30% Critical Damage Received | **SSS Tier** (Must-Have First for PvP) |
| **Reactive Armor** | -15% Critical Hit Chance Received | **S Tier** (Frontline Staple) |
| **Railgun / Cannon**| +10% Counter Troop Damage Bonus | **A Tier** (High DPS Scaling) |
| **Data Chip** | Bonus Counter Multiplier / Crit | **B Tier** (Lowest Priority) |

### The Great Debate: DPS Gun vs. Frontline Radar
* **PvE Focus (Campaign, World Boss):** Rush your carry’s (e.g., Kimberly) Gun to Mythic first for raw damage.
* **PvP Focus (Apex Arena, Alliance Duels):** Rush your frontline’s (Murphy, Williams) Radar to Mythic. A Mythic Radar cuts incoming critical damage by 30%. In endgame PvP, if your frontline melts in 6 seconds to an enemy Kimberly, your DPS won't have time to cast skills.

*Community Consensus:* Leave the Data Chip at 2-Star or 4-Star. Never waste 10 Mythic Blueprints on a Data Chip early on.

## 5. Exclusive Weapons (EW) Deep Dive

Exclusive Weapons are unique armaments for UR Heroes at 5 Stars. 

### Activation & Shard Costs

You need exactly **50 Hero-Specific Named Shards** to unlock the weapon. Universal Shards *cannot* be used for the initial unlock.

![Exclusive Weapons Upgrade Guide](/images/gear/exclusive-weapons-upgrade-guide.webp)

| Level Bracket | Shards per Level | Cumulative Total | Unlocked Milestones & Effects |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Unlock (0 -> 1)** | 50 (Named Only) | **50** | **Base EW Mechanic Activation** |
| **Lv 1 -> 5** | 20 Shards | 130 | Base Stat Growth (ATK, HP, DEF) |
| **Lv 5 -> 10** | 40 Shards | 330 | **Tactics Skill Upgrade 1** |
| **Lv 10 -> 15** | 60 Shards | 630 | Enhanced Base Stat Growth |
| **Lv 15 -> 20** | 100 Shards | **1,130** | **Meta Sweet Spot: +7.5% Squad Buff & Awakening Ready** |
| **Lv 20 -> 25** | 150 Shards | 1,880 | High-Level Stat Growth |
| **Lv 25 -> 30** | 200 Shards | **2,880** | **Max Tactics Skill Upgrade & Max Stats** |

### The "Meta Sweet Spot" (Level 20)
Reaching Level 20 is essential. It provides a massive **+7.5% squad-wide troop stat boost** and is a prerequisite for Hero Awakening (Season 6+). 
* **Efficiency Rule:** It is much better to bring two heroes to Level 20 than to push one hero from 20 to 30.

### Getting EW Shards
* **The Black Market:** During the post-season Black Market celebration, purchase Exclusive Weapon Shard Choice Chests (10 shards for 1,000 Cash). This is the primary F2P acquisition route.
* **Universal Shards:** Earned through Alliance Duels and Apex Arena. Use these only to push unlocked weapons from Level 1 to 20.

## 6. Dismantling & Gear Swapping

* **Gear Swapping is FREE:** You can swap complete gear sets between heroes instantly with 0 cost. Shift your best gear to specialized PvE heroes for World Bosses, and back to PvP defenders afterward.
* **The Dismantling Trap:** Dismantling gear refunds 100% of Upgrade Ore, Blueprints, and Ceramics, but **0% of Gold Coins**. Since upgrading costs hundreds of millions of gold, dismantling high-tier gear is a permanent gold sink. Avoid it whenever possible!

## 7. Optimal Gear Upgrade Priorities

**The Golden Rule:** *Never split blueprints.* Focus entirely on your primary carry until their key pieces are finished. Concentrated power beats distributed weakness.

### Standard Tank Squad Progression
1. **Kimberly Railgun:** Lv 40 -> 4-Star -> Mythic (5-Star).
2. **Kimberly Data Chip:** Lv 40 -> 2-Star / 4-Star.
3. **Murphy Tactical Radar:** Lv 40 -> 4-Star -> Mythic (Crucial for frontline survival).
4. **Williams Tactical Radar:** Lv 40 -> 4-Star -> Mythic.
5. **Murphy & Williams Reactive Armor:** Lv 40 -> 4-Star -> Mythic.
6. **Stetmann Railgun:** Lv 40 -> 4-Star -> Mythic.
7. **Kimberly Radar/Armor:** Lv 40 -> Mythic.

### Aircraft Squad Progression (DVA Carry)
1. **DVA Railgun:** Lv 40 -> 4-Star -> Mythic.
2. **Lucius Tactical Radar:** Lv 40 -> 4-Star -> Mythic (The ultimate anti-energy wall).
3. **Carlie Tactical Radar & Armor:** Lv 40 -> 4-Star -> Mythic.
4. **DVA Data Chip:** Lv 40 -> 4-Star.
5. **Morrison Railgun:** Lv 40 -> 4-Star -> Mythic.

### Missile Squad Progression (Tesla/Fiona Carry)
1. **Tesla/Fiona Railgun:** Lv 40 -> 4-Star -> Mythic.
2. **McGregor Reactive Armor & Radar:** Lv 40 -> 4-Star -> Mythic.
3. **Adam Tactical Radar:** Lv 40 -> 4-Star -> Mythic.
4. **Swift Railgun:** Lv 40 -> 4-Star -> Mythic.

## Summary

Mastering the gear system in Last War: Survival Game is a marathon. Conserve your screws, never waste honor points on anything but Legendary/Mythic Blueprints, and always push your primary damage dealer and frontline tanks to their Mythic powers first. Stop Exclusive Weapons at Level 20 until your entire core team is fully buffed. Good luck out there, Commanders!
