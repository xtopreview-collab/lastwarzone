# Missile Vehicle Squad Guide (Season 3)

Heroes & Squads · 2026-03-24

## Missiles in Season 3: The Faction Warfare Counter

Missile squads remain the essential Aircraft counter in Season 3. With Goldust Conquest running twice weekly and the Faction Duel looming in Week 7, your alliance needs Missile players more than ever. Aircraft dominance in Goldust Conquest offence means Missile defenders are critical for protecting your faction's buildings and Goldust.

Season 3 Role

**In Goldust Conquest, your Missile squad is your faction's shield against enemy Aircraft attacks.** While Aircraft squads crack buildings on offence, Missile squads hold the line on defence. A garrison with Tesla and Fiona will destroy incoming Aircraft rallies. Every faction needs Missile players — if your faction is short on them, you are giving the enemy free buildings every round.
