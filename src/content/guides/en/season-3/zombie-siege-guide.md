# Zombie Siege (Season 3)

Alliance · 2026-03-24

## Zombie Siege in Season 3

Zombie Siege continues its regular schedule in Season 3. Your alliance has the strongest squads yet — Scarlett UR, Marshall with Exclusive Weapon, multiple weapon-equipped heroes, and higher star-promoted gear. The key Season 3 addition is Desert Protectors, which serve as powerful reinforcement troops for weaker bases.

For the full event mechanics, see the [Zombie Siege Guide](/guides/general/zombie-siege-guide/).

CR Does Not Affect Zombie Siege

**Curse Resistance has no effect on Zombie Siege.** Zombies are not Season 3 PvE content — they are a persistent event that uses standard combat stats. Your Virus Resistance, hero power, gear, and troop quality are what matter here. Do not confuse CR-gated content with Zombie Siege.
