# Desert Bazaar Guide (Season 3)

Season Mechanics · 2026-03-24

## The Trading Post You Cannot Ignore

The Desert Bazaar is Season 3's event store, and Sandworm Scales are the only currency it takes. You earn scales by killing sandworms and spend them on whatever the merchant is stocking — with a hard cap on each item that resets every time the store refreshes.

Sandworm Scales have no use outside the Bazaar, and they do not survive the season. Every scale sitting in your inventory when Season 3 closes is simply deleted, so the question is never whether to spend them, only on what.

The Overview Said It Best

**Do not let Sandworm Scales pile up in your inventory.** The Season 3 overview warns about this for a reason. Scales have exactly one purpose — being traded for Sacred Water at the Desert Bazaar. If you are hoarding scales "for later," you are making a mistake. Sacred Water production is time-gated through the Blessing Fountain, and the Bazaar is your way to supplement that income. Convert scales as you earn them.
