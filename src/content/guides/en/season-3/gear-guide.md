# Gear Guide (Season 3)

Gear · 2026-03-24

## Gear in Season 3

By Season 3, your primary squad should have all 20 gold gear pieces at Level 40 with significant star promotion progress. Many competitive players will have 1-2 Mythic pieces. The gear focus in Season 3 is on **gearing Scarlett** (for Tank players integrating her), continuing **star promotion toward Mythic**, and managing gear across squads for Goldust Conquest demands.

The Season 3 Gear Reality

**If your primary squad is not fully gold-geared by Season 3, you are significantly behind.** Catch up immediately using the craft orders from the [Pre-Season 1 Gear Guide](/guides/pre-season-1/gear-guide/). Season 3 gear progression assumes a complete gold set with star promotion underway.
