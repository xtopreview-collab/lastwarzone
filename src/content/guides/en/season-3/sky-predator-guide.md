# Sky Predator (Season 3)

Alliance · 2026-03-24

## Sky Battlefront in Season 3

By Season 3, your alliance has the deepest roster of Exclusive Weapons yet. DVA, Kimberly, Murphy, Carlie, and now Marshall all have weapons. Scarlett's UR promotion adds another durable front-line option. Combined, these upgrades push your Sky Predator damage to levels that can overwhelm airship shield phases entirely.

For the full event mechanics, see the [Sky Predator Guide](/guides/general/sky-predator-guide/).

Season 3 Weapon Density

**With five heroes carrying Exclusive Weapons, your rally compositions are dramatically stronger than in previous seasons.** Marshall's weapon alone adds a team-wide damage multiplier to every rally he joins. Rallies led by DVA or Kimberly with Marshall buffing deal damage that was not achievable in Season 2. Push your shield-break strategy accordingly.
