# Hero Guide (Season 3)

Heroes & Squads · 2026-03-24

## What Changes in Season 3

Season 3 brings several hero-level changes that shift the tier list:

1. **Scarlett upgrades from SSR to UR** — the headline change for Tank squads, making her a legitimate A-tier defensive hero
2. **Marshall receives his Exclusive Weapon** — elevates him to the strongest support hero in the game
3. **Desert Protectors** change the cost/benefit of combat — troop losses create substantially stronger elite troops
4. **Goldust Conquest demand** — heroes are evaluated not just on raw power but on their contribution to faction PvP twice per week
5. **All previous Exclusive Weapons carry forward** — Kimberly, DVA, Murphy, and Carlie's weapons are now standard equipment

For the core hero mechanics (rarities, skills, star upgrades, Tactics cards), see the [Pre-Season 1 Hero Guide](/guides/pre-season-1/hero-guide/). This guide focuses on what changes in Season 3.
