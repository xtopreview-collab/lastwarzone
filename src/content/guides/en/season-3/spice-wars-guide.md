# Goldust Conquest Strategy Guide

Strategy · 2026-03-24

## What Goldust Conquest Is

Goldust Conquest is Season 3's headline PvP event. Two factions fight for Goldust — the ranking currency that determines your final Season 3 standing. Every other system in the season (Curse Resistance, Desert Protectors, resource production) exists to feed your performance here.

If you are not participating in Goldust Conquest, you are not playing Season 3.

Goldust Is Everything

**Your total accumulated Goldust determines your Season 3 ranking.** Not territory held, not power score, not kill count. Goldust. Every decision you make — which buildings to upgrade, where to spend resources, how to allocate Desert Protectors — should be evaluated against one question: does this help me earn or protect Goldust?
