# Chip Lab Guide (Season 3)

Buildings · 2026-03-24

## Chip Lab in Season 3

By Season 3, your primary squad should have a complete legendary chip set (4 chips) with significant star progression. Many players will also have a second squad's Interference chip crafted. The Chip Lab focus in Season 3 is on **matching chip loadouts to Goldust Conquest demands**, managing Combat Boost, and continuing star ascension.

For the full Chip Lab mechanics, see the [Pre-Season 1 Chip Lab Guide](/guides/pre-season-1/chip-lab-guide/).

Season 3 Chip Progress

**The target for Season 3 is all 4 legendary chips at 3-star or higher for your primary squad, with a second Interference chip at 2-star for your secondary squad.** If you have been farming consistently since pre-season, this should be achievable. If you are behind, prioritise completing your primary set before investing in a second squad.
