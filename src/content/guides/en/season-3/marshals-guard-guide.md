# Marshal's Guard (Season 3)

Alliance · 2026-03-24

## Marshal's Guard in Season 3

Marshal's Guard continues every 2-3 days in Season 3. Your alliance has the strongest squads ever — multiple Exclusive Weapons, Scarlett UR, Marshall with weapon, and T10 troops. Combined with Desert Protectors as an additional combat resource, you can push significantly higher difficulty than in Season 2.

For the full event mechanics, see the [Marshal's Guard Guide](/guides/general/marshals-guard-guide/).

Push Higher Again

**If you were clearing Level 9-11 in Season 2, target Level 12-14+ in Season 3.** Marshall's Exclusive Weapon boosts your entire rally's damage output. Scarlett UR adds front-line durability. Desert Protectors can supplement your rally force. Every difficulty level increase means better rewards — do not coast on last season's settings.
