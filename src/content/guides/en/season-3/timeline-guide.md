# Season 3 Timeline & Week-by-Week Roadmap

Season Overview · 2026-04-25

## How Season 3 Plays Out

Season 3 is **faction warfare season with a hard endpoint**. Two factions — Scarlet Legion and Golden Tribe — battle through twice-weekly Goldust Conquest, culminating in the Week 7 Faction Duel that decides the entire season. Every week of Curse Resistance research, Desert Protector accumulation, and Goldust farming feeds into that final battle. Season 3 runs roughly **7-8 weeks** with the Duel as the climax.

The Season 3 Chain of Causation

**CR → PvE damage → Mithril and Sacred Water → Building upgrades → Goldust Conquest dominance → Goldust → Final ranking → Faction Duel preparation.** Every system feeds the next. Skip CR and the chain breaks at step one. Skip Goldust Conquest and the chain breaks at step five. Treat Season 3 as a single connected loop, not seven independent systems.
