# Alliance Train (Season 3)

Alliance · 2026-03-24

## Alliance Train in Season 3

The Alliance Train continues as a daily routine in Season 3. The fundamentals have not changed — drive the train, guard it, claim rewards. What changes is the resource context. Season 3 introduces Mithril, Sacred Water, and Goldust as critical resources, and the train rewards need to be evaluated against these new demands.

For the full mechanics breakdown, see the [Alliance Train Guide](/guides/general/alliance-train-guide/).

Season 3 Train Value

**The train remains one of the most consistent daily resource sources in the game.** By Season 3, your alliance should be running it on autopilot — fixed time, rotating drivers, Gold Express every day. The rewards compound. An alliance that has been running the train since pre-season has accumulated months of free resources that new alliances cannot match.
