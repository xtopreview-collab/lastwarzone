# Aircraft Squad Guide (Season 3)

Heroes & Squads · 2026-03-24

## Aircraft in Season 3: Full Weapon Arsenal

Aircraft squads enter Season 3 with their most complete toolkit yet. Carlie's Exclusive Weapon (introduced in Season 2) is now standard, DVA's Energy Master is at high levels, and Murphy's Mitigation Master continues to make the Murphy Exception the default formation. With Goldust Conquest demanding strong PvP squads twice per week, Aircraft remains the premier offensive force.

Season 3 Position

**Aircraft is still the PvP king, and Goldust Conquest is a PvP event.** Your Aircraft squad is your primary offensive weapon for destroying enemy buildings and stealing Goldust. The combination of DVA's burst, Murphy's survivability, and now Carlie's weapon (available for standard formation players) gives you options for both offence and garrison cracking.
