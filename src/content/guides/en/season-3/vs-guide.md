# Alliance Duel VS (Season 3)

Alliance · 2026-03-24

## VS in Season 3

Alliance Duel VS continues its 6-day cycle in Season 3, but now competes for your attention with Goldust Conquest (Wednesday and Saturday). The dual PvP commitments require careful resource management. You cannot afford to go all-in on VS if it leaves you unprepared for Goldust Conquest, and vice versa.

For the full day-by-day mechanics, see the [Alliance Duel VS Guide](/guides/general/vs-guide/).

The Dual Commitment

**Season 3 demands strong PvP performance in both VS and Goldust Conquest.** VS runs weekly. Goldust Conquest runs Wednesday and Saturday. Wednesday VS coincides with Goldust Conquest, meaning you need troops, heroes, and resources for both on the same day. Poor planning here costs you in one or both events.
