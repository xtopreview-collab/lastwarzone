# F2P Season 3 Guide — Curse Resistance and Goldust Conquest on a Budget

Free 2 Play · 2026-06-21

## What Changes for F2P in Season 3

Season 3 (Golden Realm) moves the war into an ancient-Egypt desert, and it swaps Season 2's cold for a new gate: **Curse Resistance.** Just as virus resistance defined Season 1, Curse Resistance decides whether your hits land or fizzle against the season's monsters. For a free-to-play player that's actually encouraging — it's earned by **grinding, not spending**, so a patient F2P account stays PvE-relevant all season.

The season's identity is **Goldust Conquest** (the PvP), **Desert Protectors** (disposable seasonal troops), and a steady Curse Resistance climb. None of the three requires a wallet to participate in.

Level Your Sacred Water Fountains Evenly

**Curse Resistance comes from the Curse Research Lab, fed by Sacred Water — and the cardinal F2P rule is to upgrade your Sacred Water Fountains *evenly*, never maxing one before the others.** Output gains are flat per level while costs climb, so spreading your upgrades keeps your resistance rising fastest for the least resource. Fall behind on Curse Resistance and your damage collapses against the season's Pharaoh-zombies and elites — this is the one system F2P cannot skip.
