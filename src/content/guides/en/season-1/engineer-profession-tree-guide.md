# Engineer Profession Tree — Full Lv 1 to Lv 40 Build (Season 1)

Profession · 2026-08-11

## Build Summary

The **Engineer** profession is the fastest path to T10 in Season 1 — it compresses the time to the HQ 30 / Tech Center 30 / Barracks 30 building gates and the Special Forces research that unlocks Unit X. This guide gives the corrected Season 1 tree, a levelling order, and an at‑cap setup.

PropertyDetail

Season 1 cap**Profession Level 40 — 45 skill points** total (about one per level plus ~5 bonus points; the tree shows rows up to Lv 50, but Lv 45/50 are NOT accessible in S1)

Skill tiers9 tiers: Lv 1, 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40

Permanent skillsLeft + middle columns — carry into every future season

Seasonal skillsRight column (shared with Warlord) — **refunded at season end**, so spend them freely while levelling

Extra pointsUp to ~10 more via **Skill Point** consumables; a **Profession Skill Reset Book** fully respecs

Confidence note

The **Lv 40 cap, the 9 tiers, and the skill names below are confirmed** from the in-game screens and multiple guides. Exact effect percentages and the precise point split are **recommendations** — verify the live numbers in your own client. (Skill names vary slightly by translation.)
