# Missile Vehicle Squad Guide (Season 1)

Heroes & Squads · 2026-03-12

## Missiles in Season 1: The Essential Counter

As Aircraft becomes the dominant meta in Season 1, Missile squads become **more important than ever**. Every alliance needs Missile players to counter the wave of DVA-led Aircraft squads. If you started building Missiles in pre-season, Season 1 is where your investment pays off.

The Counter-Pick Meta

**In Season 1, Missile squads go from "nice to have" to "alliance essential."** Without Missile players, your alliance has no answer to enemy Aircraft squads in Alliance Wars and SvS. The combat triangle demands coverage.
