# Aircraft Squad Guide (Season 1)

Heroes & Squads · 2026-03-12

## Aircraft in Season 1: The Dominant Meta

In Season 1, Aircraft squads transition from a secondary build to the **dominant meta**. If you started collecting Aircraft heroes during pre-season, now is the time to commit. DVA-led Aircraft squads are the strongest PvP composition in Season 1.

The Shift

**Aircraft is now the primary squad for competitive players.** Tank squads are still strong for PvE and rallies, but Aircraft wins the open PvP meta. If you have not started building Aircraft yet, Season 1 is the time.
