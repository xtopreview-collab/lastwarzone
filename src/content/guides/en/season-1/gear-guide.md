# Gear Guide (Season 1)

Gear · 2026-03-12

## Gear in Season 1

If you followed the [Pre-Season 1 Gear Guide](/guides/pre-season-1/gear-guide/), your primary squad should have most or all of its 20 gold gear pieces crafted. Season 1 shifts the gear focus from **crafting** to **star promotion and Mythic progression**.

The Season 1 Gear Shift

**Pre-season was about crafting gold gear. Season 1 is about making that gold gear legendary-starred and eventually Mythic.** Each star is a bigger jump than the one before it, and the fifth is the biggest of all — it is also what turns Legendary gear Mythic. This is where your gear investment goes in Season 1.
