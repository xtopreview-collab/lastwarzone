# Marshal's Guard (Season 1)

Alliance · 2026-03-12

## Marshal's Guard in Season 1

Marshal's Guard continues to run every 2-3 days in Season 1. With gold gear, Exclusive Weapons, and T10 troops, your alliance can push significantly higher difficulty levels and earn much better rewards.

For the full event mechanics, see the [Marshal's Guard Guide](/guides/general/marshals-guard-guide/).

Push Higher

**If your alliance was clearing Level 5-7 in pre-season, you should be targeting Level 8-10+ in Season 1.** Gold gear, Exclusive Weapons, and T10 troops make a dramatic difference. Every difficulty level increase improves rewards.
