# Warlord Profession Tree — Full Lv 1 to Lv 40 Build (Season 1)

Profession · 2026-08-11

## Build Summary

The **Warlord** (in-game: **War Leader**) profession is the combat counterpart to Engineer — rally damage, reinforcement walls, troop capacity and battlefield buffs. This guide gives the corrected Season 1 tree, a levelling order, an at‑cap setup, and a dedicated **Desert Storm / PvP / rally** build.

PropertyDetail

Season 1 cap**Profession Level 40 — 45 skill points** total (about one per level plus ~5 bonus points; the tree shows rows up to Lv 50, but Lv 45/50 are NOT accessible in S1)

Skill tiers9 tiers: Lv 1, 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40

Permanent skillsLeft + middle columns — carry into every future season

Seasonal skillsRight column (shared with Engineer) — **refunded at season end**

Extra pointsUp to ~10 more via **Skill Point** consumables; a **Profession Skill Reset Book** fully respecs

Confidence note

The **Lv 40 cap, the 9 tiers, and the skill names below are confirmed** against the in-game profession screens. Exact effect percentages and the precise point split are **recommendations** — verify in your client. (The real War Leader garrison/reinforce skills are **Tactical Garrison, Garrison Boost, Garrison Haste, Rally Guard** and **Hold the Base** — not "Fortify Defense", "Siege Boost", "Tactical Reinforce" or "Reinforce Strengthen", which are not real War Leader skills; Siege Mastery is an *Engineer* skill.)
