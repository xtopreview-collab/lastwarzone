# Sky Predator (Season 1)

Alliance · 2026-03-13

## Sky Battlefront in Season 1

By Season 1, your alliance has Exclusive Weapons, gold gear, and more coordination experience. This means your rallies hit harder, your shield-break timing can be more aggressive, and your overall damage output is significantly higher than pre-season.

For the full event mechanics, see the [Sky Predator Guide](/guides/general/sky-predator-guide/).

Season 1 Changes Everything

**Exclusive Weapons transform your rally damage.** A rally led by DVA with Energy Master or Kimberly with Rocket Shadow deals dramatically more damage than the same heroes without their weapons. If your alliance has even 2-3 rally leaders with Exclusive Weapons, you can push for clean shield-breaks consistently.
