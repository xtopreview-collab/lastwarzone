# Alliance Train (Season 1)

Alliance · 2026-03-12

## Alliance Train in Season 1

By Season 1, your alliance should have the Alliance Train running as a daily routine. The fundamentals do not change, but your alliance is now stronger — stronger guards, more consistent Gold Express, and better rewards.

For the full mechanics breakdown, see the [Alliance Train Guide](/guides/general/alliance-train-guide/).

Season 1 Train Rewards

**Season 1 train rewards are better than pre-season.** Higher Alliance Gift levels, more participants, and Gold Express consistency mean every daily train generates meaningful resources. If you built the habit in pre-season, you are already reaping compounding returns.
