# Season 1 HQ Guide

Progression · 2026-03-12

## What Changes in Season 1

Season 1 begins once your server completes the pre-season countdown. If you followed the [HQ Progression Guide](/guides/general/hq-progression/) and reached HQ 30, you are in a strong position. Season 1 introduces several new systems and shifts the meta significantly.

The Big Picture

**Season 1 is about deepening your account, not rushing to the next HQ level.** HQ 30 remains the cap for the **whole** of Season 1 — it does not rise until the Age of Oil, after Season 2. The focus shifts from vertical progression (HQ levels) to horizontal progression — stronger squads, better gear, more systems unlocked, and preparing your second squad.
