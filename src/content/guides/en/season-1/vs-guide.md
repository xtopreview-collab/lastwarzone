# Alliance Duel VS (Season 1)

Alliance · 2026-03-12

## VS in Season 1

Alliance Duel VS follows the same 6-day structure in Season 1, but the stakes are higher. Your alliance has stronger squads, more resources to save, and better rewards at stake. The meta shift to Aircraft also changes Day 6 combat significantly.

For the full day-by-day mechanics, see the [Alliance Duel VS Guide](/guides/general/vs-guide/).

Season 1 VS Matters More

**Season 1 VS rewards are significantly better than pre-season.** The investment in saving resources and coordinating your alliance pays off with more impactful returns. Every week you skip or underperform is a bigger opportunity cost than in pre-season.
