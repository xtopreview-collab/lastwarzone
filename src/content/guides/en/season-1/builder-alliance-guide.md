# Builder Alliance Guide (Season 1)

Season Mechanics · 2026-05-16

## What Is Builder Alliance?

Builder Alliance is an **alternative alliance mode** that opens in **Week 4 of Season 1**. Switching to it means **abandoning the City Race and Alliance Influence ranking** in favour of **facility construction, donation-driven progression, and stable resource rewards**.

It is designed for small, mid-tier, casual, or off-timezone alliances that cannot realistically compete for city-war rankings — instead of grinding rewards they will never win, they switch tracks entirely and earn steady growth-focused rewards.

Irreversible — Decide Carefully

**Once an alliance switches to Builder Alliance, it cannot revert for the rest of the season.** You lose access to city wars, influence-points ranking, the top decoration rewards (God of Judgment), and Season Merit Medals. Make the decision deliberately with full R4/R5 buy-in, not in a moment of frustration after a bad fight.
