# Gear Guide (Season 4)

Gear · 2026-03-24

## Gear in Season 4

By Season 4, your primary squad should have a complete gold gear set at Level 40 with multiple pieces at 2-star or higher. Many competitive players are pushing toward 4-star and Mythic gear. The gear system itself does not change — what changes is how gear interacts with two new systems: the **Armament Institute** and **Tactics Cards**.

For full gear mechanics, see the [Pre-Season 1 Gear Guide](/guides/pre-season-1/gear-guide/).

Season 4 Gear Focus

**Season 4 gear strategy has two priorities: continue star promotion toward Mythic, and start Armament Institute research.** The Armament Institute adds a new layer of stat progression that complements your existing gear. Tactics Cards interact with gear by providing additive stat bonuses that compound with your gear's base stats. Together, these systems make every gear upgrade more valuable than before.
