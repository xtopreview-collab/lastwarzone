# Lucky Cats Guide (Season 4)

Season Events · 2026-03-24

## Free Rewards on the World Map

Finding One Is Only Half of It

**Discovering a Lucky Cat does not give you anything — you have to dispatch electricians to charge it, and the rewards can only be claimed once it is fully charged.** A cat found and left uncharged pays nothing at all. Budget the electricians before you go looking.

### The Two Kinds, and How Each Is Found

TypeHow It Appears

Large Lucky CatHidden on the map, in a position that **differs in every warzone**. Use **Flares or Base Lights** to illuminate the night for a chance to discover one

Mini Lucky CatTriggered by **illuminating the Path to Battle** at night — and capped at **one Mini per day**

Extra Large catsOnce the warzone hits its stage target for defeating **Oni Generals**, **additional Large Lucky Cats** spawn — so the PvE push and the cat hunt feed each other

ChargingDispatch **electricians** to charge a discovered cat. Rewards unlock only at full charge

Lucky Cats (Maneki-neko) are a daily world map mechanic in Season 4 that provides free resources and items. They are not a competitive system and they will not make or break your season. But free rewards are free rewards, and over the course of a full season, the accumulation is meaningful.

The mechanic fits the Japanese theme of Sakura Isle — ceramic cat statues scattered across the map, waving their paw and offering gifts to whoever finds them first.

Low Effort, Steady Returns

**Lucky Cats require almost no effort.** You explore the world map, find a statue, claim the reward, and move on. The time investment is minimal — a few minutes per day. The rewards include resources, speedups, Armament Materials, and occasionally Tactics Card packs. None of these are game-changing individually, but skipping them daily for an entire season means leaving a significant total on the table.
