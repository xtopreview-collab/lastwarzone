# Missile Vehicle Squad Guide (Season 4)

Heroes & Squads · 2026-03-24

## Missiles in Season 4

Missile squads continue their rise as the essential Aircraft counter. Tesla and Fiona are the established core, and by Season 4, most serious Missile players have both heroes at high stars with strong gear. There are no new UR promotions or Exclusive Weapons for Missile heroes in Season 4 — Fiona's weapon arrives in Season 5. The power gains come from T11 troops and Tactics Cards, both of which benefit Missile squads significantly.

Season 4 Missiles

**Missile squads are the counter-punch of Copper War.** When enemy Aircraft rallies attack your alliance's cities, your Missile squads are the answer. With T11 Assault Raiders and optimised Tactics Cards, Tesla's Lightning Chain and Fiona's Atomic Blast hit harder than ever. Every alliance needs Missile players in Season 4 — the Aircraft threat does not go away.
