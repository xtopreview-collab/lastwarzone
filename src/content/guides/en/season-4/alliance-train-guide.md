# Alliance Train (Season 4)

Alliance · 2026-03-24

## Alliance Train in Season 4

The Alliance Train continues daily in Season 4. The fundamentals are unchanged, but Evernight Isle introduces unique considerations: darkness affects train routes, Blood Night windows can disrupt schedules, and Copper War competes for alliance attention.

For the full mechanics breakdown, see the [Alliance Train Guide](/guides/general/alliance-train-guide/).

Do Not Skip the Train

**Season 4 has more demands on your alliance's time and attention than any previous season.** It is tempting to skip the train when Copper War is active and Blood Night requires response. Do not. The daily compounding rewards from consistent Gold Express train runs are significant, and they are resources you need for everything else this season demands. Maintain the habit.
