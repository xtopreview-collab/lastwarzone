# Aircraft Squad Guide (Season 4)

Heroes & Squads · 2026-03-24

## Aircraft in Season 4

Aircraft squads remain the dominant PvP force entering Season 4. The Murphy Exception is fully standard, DVA's Energy Master is at Level 30 across competitive accounts, and Carlie's Exclusive Weapon from Season 2 provides reliable front-line durability. No new weapons or UR promotions arrive in Season 4 — the power shifts come from T11 troops, Tactics Cards, and the unique Evernight Isle mechanics.

Season 4 Aircraft

**Aircraft squads are the spearhead of Copper War offence.** When your alliance needs to take a copper-producing city, Aircraft rallies with Assault Raiders and optimised Tactics Cards are the tip of the spear. Your burst damage breaks garrisons, and your type advantage against the Tank defenders holding those cities makes Aircraft the most valuable offensive squad in Season 4.
