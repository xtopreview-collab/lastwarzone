# Chip Lab Guide (Season 4)

Buildings · 2026-03-24

## Chip Lab in Season 4

By Season 4, your primary squad should have a complete legendary chip set (4 chips) at 2-star or higher. Your secondary squad likely has its own Interference chip. The Chip Lab itself is a mature system at this point — Season 4 does not change how chips work. What changes is how chips interact with Tactics Cards and T11 troops, and how darkness affects your material farming routine.

For full Chip Lab mechanics, see the [Pre-Season 1 Chip Lab Guide](/guides/pre-season-1/chip-lab-guide/).

Season 4 Chip Targets

**The Season 4 target is pushing your primary squad's chip set toward 4-5 star ascension while maintaining a functional secondary squad chip set at 2-star.** Chip bonuses compound with Tactics Cards and T11 troop stats — every chip star level is worth more now than it was in previous seasons because the base stats being multiplied are higher.
