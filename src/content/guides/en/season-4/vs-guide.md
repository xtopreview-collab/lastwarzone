# Alliance Duel VS (Season 4)

Alliance · 2026-03-24

## VS in Season 4

Alliance Duel VS follows the same 6-day structure in Season 4, but the environment has changed dramatically. Darkness penalties reduce resource generation, Blood Night disrupts daily schedules, and the Copper War competes for the same resources and attention you need for VS. Your alliance must balance VS preparation against Generator fuel farming, Lighthouse maintenance, and city defence.

For the full day-by-day mechanics, see the [Alliance Duel VS Guide](/guides/general/vs-guide/).

Season 4 VS Tension

**The biggest VS challenge in Season 4 is not the event itself — it is resource competition.** Copper War demands troops, speedups, and attention. Generator fuel farming demands marches and time. Blood Night disrupts schedules three times daily. VS requires discipline to save resources when everything else in Season 4 is trying to consume them. Alliances that maintain VS discipline while managing Season 4 mechanics will pull ahead.
