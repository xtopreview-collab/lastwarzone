# Murphy — Unyielding Warrior

**UR** · Tank · Defense · Physical damage

Why Murphy matters

First free UR hero most players receive. Excellent tank with Exclusive Weapon that enables the 'Murphy Exception' in Aircraft squads. Skill priority: Ironclad Barrier > Stand Firm > Cannon Fire.
