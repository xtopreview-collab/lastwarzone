# Tesla — Magnetic Expert

**UR** · Missile · Attack · Energy damage

Why Tesla matters

Premier missile DPS with Inductive Current stacking mechanic. High base stats make him a strong end-game carry. One of the first to receive Awakening in Season 6.

## How to invest in Tesla

* Level and gear Tesla per the [Hero Leveling guide](/guides/general/hero-leveling-guide/).
