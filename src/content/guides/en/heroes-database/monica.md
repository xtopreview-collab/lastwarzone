# Monica — Resource Farmer

**SSR** · Tank · Support · Physical damage

Why Monica matters

Resource farming specialist. Treasure Hunter raises the Coins, Food and Iron gained after your squad defeats Ironhead, Glutton, Miser and Doom Elites. Exclusively valuable for World Map farming. Not competitive in PvP.
