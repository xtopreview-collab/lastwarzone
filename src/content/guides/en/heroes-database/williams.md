# Williams — Storm Hunter

**UR** · Tank · Defense · Physical damage

Why Williams matters

Best defensive tank in the game. His damage reduction is self-only; Iron Will buffs Defense on two front-row heroes and reaches the squad only at 3 stars. Still the backbone of any formation.
