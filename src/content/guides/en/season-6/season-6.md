# F2P Season 6 Guide — Free Awakening and Surviving Permanent City Loss

Free 2 Play · 2026-06-21

## What Changes for F2P in Season 6

Season 6 (Lost Rainforest) — fought across the Shadow Rainforest map — is, in two ways, the most F2P-relevant season yet. The good: **Hero Awakening is unusually friendly to free players this season**, with no paywall on the shards. The bad: **City Destruction is permanent**, which punishes careless free accounts harder than anyone. Play it right and an F2P account can come away with a genuinely awakened hero; play it carelessly and you can lose a city for good.

The season splits your warzone into two factions — **Deepwood vs Wetland** — locked for the whole season, with a neutral Great River clan holding the centre.

Awakening Is Free This Season — Use It

**Season 6 has no Awakening Battle Pass, and the Awakening Shards are earnable for free** — chiefly from the Hero Awakening Trial and from Global Expedition (the main free shard source). For an F2P account that already has a hero at 5★ with their Exclusive Weapon at Level 20+, this is a realistic shot at *unlocking* Awakening (the named-shard threshold) on a hero like Kimberly. Chasing a full 5-star Awakening (which needs many times more shards) is whale territory — unlock the first tier and stop there.

Note too that the season's Exclusive Weapon shards for its featured heroes appear in the **regular Tavern recruitment**, so **save your Hero Recruitment Tickets** for them rather than spending them earlier.
