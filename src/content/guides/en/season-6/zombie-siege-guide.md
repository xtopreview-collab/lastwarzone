# Zombie Siege Guide (Season 6)

Alliance · 2026-04-25

Season 6 Context

The core mechanics in this guide are largely unchanged from prior seasons. Season 6 (Lost Rainforest) layers **faction warfare** and the new progression systems on top — see the [Season 6 Overview](/guides/season-6/overview/), [Factions Guide](/guides/season-6/factions-guide/), [Altars Guide](/guides/season-6/altars-guide/), and [City Destruction Guide](/guides/season-6/city-destruction-guide/) for what's new. Hero Awakening for Kimberly (W1), DVA (W4), and Tesla (W7) is covered in the [Hero Awakening Guide](/guides/general/hero-awakening-guide/).

## Zombie Siege in Season 6

Season 6 Zombie Siege features the strongest defensive squads in the game's history. T11 troops, the full Exclusive Weapon lineup, and faction-buff VR bonuses combine to make your alliance dramatically more capable. If your alliance is not pushing the highest available difficulty, you are leaving significant rewards on the table.

For the full event mechanics, see the [Zombie Siege Guide](/guides/general/zombie-siege-guide/).

Push Maximum Difficulty

**Season 6 gives you the strongest defensive tools ever.** Murphy with Mitigation Master, T11 troops, faction-buff VR bonuses, Scarlett UR, and Hero Awakening for Kimberly/DVA/Tesla mean your garrison defence is at its peak. If you cleared high difficulty in Season 4, push 2-3 levels higher in Season 6.
