# Alliance Squad Coordination (Season 6)

Alliance · 2026-04-25

Season 6 Context

The core mechanics in this guide are largely unchanged from prior seasons. Season 6 (Lost Rainforest) layers **faction warfare** and the new progression systems on top — see the [Season 6 Overview](/guides/season-6/overview/), [Factions Guide](/guides/season-6/factions-guide/), [Altars Guide](/guides/season-6/altars-guide/), and [City Destruction Guide](/guides/season-6/city-destruction-guide/) for what's new. Hero Awakening for Kimberly (W1), DVA (W4), and Tesla (W7) is covered in the [Hero Awakening Guide](/guides/general/hero-awakening-guide/).

## Why Coordination Is Everything in Season 6

Season 6 demands more alliance coordination than any previous season. **Altar capture and holding** in the central Great River region requires faction-wide rotation. **City Destruction** attacks need stacked rallies from multiple alliances simultaneously. **Faction Pacts** between alliances determine reinforcement chains. And the **War Merit economy** rewards daily participation over sporadic effort.

Season 6 Coordination Reality

**An uncoordinated alliance will lose Altars, fail City Destruction attempts, and fall hopelessly behind in War Merit rankings.** Unlike previous seasons where solo play could compete, Season 6's mechanics (faction-wide alliance skills via Altars, permanent City Destruction, faction-vs-faction tracking) make coordination non-negotiable. Season 6 is won by alliances that coordinate daily, not just during events.
