# Season 6 Timeline & Week-by-Week Roadmap

Season Overview · 2026-04-25

## How Season 6 Plays Out

Season 6 is **faction warfare season with a Hero Awakening unlock every three weeks**. Three Awakening unlocks (Kimberly W1, DVA W4, Tesla W7) drive the progression rhythm. Three Global Expedition windows (W2, W4, W6) deliver named Awakening Shards. The Braz SSR→UR window opens around Week 3. City Destruction is permanent. Altars rotate alliance skills. Season 6 runs roughly **8-10 weeks** with the heaviest week-by-week content of any season to date.

The Three Disciplines of Season 6

**Faction coordination, Awakening progression, and War Merit economy.** Every week feeds all three. Skip faction coordination and you lose Altars and City Destruction. Skip Awakening and your carries fall behind by Week 6. Skip War Merit and you cannot keep up in the seasonal shop. The three reinforce each other — they are not parallel tracks.
