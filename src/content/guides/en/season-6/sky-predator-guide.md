# Sky Predator Guide (Season 6)

Alliance · 2026-04-25

Season 6 Context

The core mechanics in this guide are largely unchanged from prior seasons. Season 6 (Lost Rainforest) layers **faction warfare** and the new progression systems on top — see the [Season 6 Overview](/guides/season-6/overview/), [Factions Guide](/guides/season-6/factions-guide/), [Altars Guide](/guides/season-6/altars-guide/), and [City Destruction Guide](/guides/season-6/city-destruction-guide/) for what's new. Hero Awakening for Kimberly (W1), DVA (W4), and Tesla (W7) is covered in the [Hero Awakening Guide](/guides/general/hero-awakening-guide/).

## Sky Predator in Season 6

Season 6 Sky Predator benefits from the full Exclusive Weapon roster from S1-S5, T11 troops, **Hero Awakening (Kimberly W1, DVA W4, Tesla W7)**, and Fungus Institute faction buffs that can be timed for maximum rally damage. Your alliance has never been stronger for airship battles.

For the full event mechanics, see the [Sky Predator Guide](/guides/general/sky-predator-guide/).

Season 6 Sky Power

**If your alliance has been timing Fungus Institute faction buffs for City Destruction attacks, apply the same discipline to Sky Predator.** Activate the Skill Damage faction buff before rallies and use the class-specific buff that matches your squad type. The stat difference between buffed and unbuffed rallies is the difference between clean shield-breaks and failed attempts.
