# War Merit Guide

Season Mechanics · 2026-04-24

## What Is War Merit?

War Merit is a **new rare currency introduced in Season 6**. Unlike resources (Food, Iron, Coins, Oil) or standard season currencies, War Merit is earned specifically through **faction warfare contributions** — participating in Altar captures, City Destruction attacks, defensive repels, and similar faction activities.

War Merit is spent in the **War Merit Shop** on season-exclusive rewards.

War Merit Rewards Players Who Fight

**War Merit is a direct reward for being an active, contributing faction member.** Players who sit in their bases farming resources without participating in Altars, City Destruction, or cross-faction fights earn significantly less War Merit. This is the deliberate design — the rewards favour faction participation over passive accumulation.
