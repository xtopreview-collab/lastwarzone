# City Destruction Guide

Season Mechanics · 2026-04-24

## What Is City Destruction?

City Destruction is the **headline strategic mechanic of Season 6**, and it works more simply than it sounds: **you capture a City in an enemy faction's Warzone and it is automatically destroyed.** There is no separate demolition step — capture *is* destruction. Destroyed Cities **cannot be rebuilt**, their territory stops providing adjacency, and the ground itself passes to your faction.

Permanent Means Permanent

**This is not a capture mechanic.** When you capture a city, it becomes yours and the enemy can take it back. When you **destroy** a city, it is gone from the map for the season. The enemy faction permanently loses its influence points, its resource production, and its positional value. Season 6 is the first Last War season where you can inflict irreversible damage on the enemy.
