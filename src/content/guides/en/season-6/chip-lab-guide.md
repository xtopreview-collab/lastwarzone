# Chip Lab Guide (Season 6)

Buildings · 2026-04-25

Season 6 Context

The core mechanics in this guide are largely unchanged from prior seasons. Season 6 (Lost Rainforest) layers **faction warfare** and the new progression systems on top — see the [Season 6 Overview](/guides/season-6/overview/), [Factions Guide](/guides/season-6/factions-guide/), [Altars Guide](/guides/season-6/altars-guide/), and [City Destruction Guide](/guides/season-6/city-destruction-guide/) for what's new. Hero Awakening for Kimberly (W1), DVA (W4), and Tesla (W7) is covered in the [Hero Awakening Guide](/guides/general/hero-awakening-guide/).

## Chip Lab in Season 6

By Season 6, your Chip Lab should be a mature system. You should have a complete legendary chip set (4 chips) for your primary squad at 3-star or higher, with a second squad's Interference chip crafted. The fundamentals have not changed, but the Fungus Institute and the Season 6 economy create new strategic considerations.

For the full chip mechanics, see the [Pre-Season 1 Chip Lab Guide](/guides/pre-season-1/chip-lab-guide/).

Season 6 Chip Considerations

**The Chip Lab itself has not changed, but everything around it has.** Fungus Institute faction buffs interact with your chip bonuses to create higher total combat stats than ever. The War Merit economy means your resource allocation is tighter — every War Merit spent on chip materials is War Merit not spent on EW Lv20+ progression or Awakening Shards (which gate Season 6's biggest power increase).
