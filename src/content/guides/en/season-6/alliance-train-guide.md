# Alliance Train Guide (Season 6)

Alliance · 2026-04-25

Season 6 Context

The core mechanics in this guide are largely unchanged from prior seasons. Season 6 (Lost Rainforest) layers **faction warfare** and the new progression systems on top — see the [Season 6 Overview](/guides/season-6/overview/), [Factions Guide](/guides/season-6/factions-guide/), [Altars Guide](/guides/season-6/altars-guide/), and [City Destruction Guide](/guides/season-6/city-destruction-guide/) for what's new. ## Alliance Train in Season 6

Season 6 introduces the **Faction supply chain** as a major map mechanic, but the regular **Alliance Train** still runs as a daily alliance event. These are two separate systems and it is important not to confuse them.

For the full Alliance Train mechanics, see the [Alliance Train Guide](/guides/general/alliance-train-guide/).

Alliance Train vs Faction supply chain — They Are Different

**The Alliance Train is the daily alliance event you have been running since pre-season.** You board wagons, set guards, complete deliveries, and earn Alliance Gift rewards. The Faction supply chain is a Season 6 map mechanic where you send faction supply runs across map routes that can be intercepted by the enemy faction. Both run simultaneously. Both are important. Do not skip one thinking it is the other.
