# Hero Guide (Season 6)

Heroes & Squads · 2026-04-25

## What Changes in Season 6

Season 6 brings the biggest hero-progression mechanic since the original Exclusive Weapon system. The headline changes:

1. **Hero Awakening** unlocks for **Kimberly (Week 1)**, **DVA (Week 4)**, and **Tesla (Week 7)** — the new fourth-skill system that replaces Exclusive Weapons as the season's hero progression mechanic
2. **Braz SSR→UR Promotion** — Season 6's seasonal SSR→UR hero (Week 3-7 promotion window)
3. **No new Exclusive Weapons this season** — the slot is taken by Hero Awakening instead
4. **Faction warfare reshapes squad selection** — Altar capture and City Destruction reward different traits than Season 5's Bank Stronghold loop

For the core hero mechanics (rarities, skills, star upgrades, Tactics cards), see the [Pre-Season 1 Hero Guide](/guides/pre-season-1/hero-guide/). For Awakening specifics, see the [Hero Awakening Guide](/guides/general/hero-awakening-guide/). For Braz, see the [SSR to UR Upgrade Guide](/guides/general/ssr-ur-upgrade-guide/).

The Awakening Window

**Awakening prerequisites — 5★ hero + Exclusive Weapon Lv20+ + 50 named Awakening Shards — make pre-season preparation critical.** The EW Lv20+ requirement is the silent killer: players who pushed Kimberly's EW to ~Level 18 in Season 5 thinking it was "good enough" cannot Awaken her in Week 1 of Season 6 without finishing the EW first. If your carry's EW is below 20, that is the #1 priority before anything else this season.
