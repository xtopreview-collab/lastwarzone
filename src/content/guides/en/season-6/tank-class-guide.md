# Tank Squad Guide (Season 6)

Heroes & Squads · 2026-04-25

## Tanks in Season 6: Awakening Era

Tank squads enter Season 6 with their biggest single power upgrade ever: **Kimberly's Awakening (Priceless Resolve)** unlocks in **Week 1**. Everything else in the squad builds around making Kimberly's new skill devastating.

The other components carry over from previous seasons: Murphy, Williams, Marshall, and Stetmann all have Exclusive Weapons by now. Scarlett has been UR since Season 3. T11 troops are baseline from Season 4. What's new in S6 is a power tier above the rest of the squad — and a faction-warfare context that rewards durable, versatile formations.

Season 6 Reality

**Tanks are dangerous again thanks to Kimberly Awakening.** A 5-star Kimberly with EW Lv20+ and Awakening unlocked outputs roughly 3,500% of Attack in a single skill cast at maximum stacks. Tanks are now the strongest pick for **garrison defence under City Destruction pressure**, rally leadership, Altar capture rallies, and counter-picking Missile opponents. The Awakening prerequisite (EW Lv20+) is the season's biggest priority for Tank players.
