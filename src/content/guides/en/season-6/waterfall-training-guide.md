# Waterfall Training (Season 6)

Alliance · 2026-04-25

Season 6 Context

The core mechanics in this guide are largely unchanged from prior seasons. Season 6 (Lost Rainforest) layers **faction warfare** and the new progression systems on top — see the [Season 6 Overview](/guides/season-6/overview/), [Factions Guide](/guides/season-6/factions-guide/), [Altars Guide](/guides/season-6/altars-guide/), and [City Destruction Guide](/guides/season-6/city-destruction-guide/) for what's new. Hero Awakening for Kimberly (W1), DVA (W4), and Tesla (W7) is covered in the [Hero Awakening Guide](/guides/general/hero-awakening-guide/).

## Waterfall Training in Season 6

By Season 6, waterfall training is a fully mature system. T11 troops are standard from Season 4, your barracks should be well-staggered, and Alliance Duel research multipliers are at their highest. The waterfall chain extends through T11, generating the most points per cycle in the game's history.

For the full explanation of how waterfall training works, see the [Waterfall Training Guide](/guides/general/waterfall-training-guide/).

Season 6 Waterfall Status

**Waterfall training is no longer optional — it is the expected standard.** Every competitive alliance uses waterfall chains for VS Day 5 and Arms Race. The point difference between waterfall and direct training at T11 is enormous. If you are not running waterfall, you will lose Day 5 every single week. The system is mature and the setup should already be in place from previous seasons.
