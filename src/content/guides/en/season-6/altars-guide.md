# Altars Guide: Central Region Capture

Season Mechanics · 2026-04-24

## What Are Altars?

One Claim Worth Checking In-Game

**The specific alliance skills named below could not be confirmed as Altar unlocks.** The Warlord Missile in particular is documented in-game as a Rare Soil War skill from Season 2, with its own launch rules — see the [Rare Soil War guide](/guides/season-2/rare-soil-war-guide/). The Altars themselves, their central-region placement and their importance to the season are not in doubt; which skills they grant is what needs confirming on your own server.

Altars are **capturable structures** scattered through the Great River Clan (central) region of the Season 6 map. When your alliance captures an Altar, it unlocks **faction-wide alliance skills** — buffs and abilities that every alliance in your faction can use.

The Season 6 Dividing Line

**Faction Altar control is the single biggest predictor of who wins Season 6.** Alliance skills like Warlord Missile and Fortify can turn losing matchups into wins. A faction that lets the enemy control all the Altars fights the entire season without these tools. If your alliance is not pushing Altars, you are playing with one hand tied behind your back.
