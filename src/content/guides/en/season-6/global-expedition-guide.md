# Global Expedition Guide

Season Mechanics · 2026-04-24

## What Is Global Expedition?

Global Expedition is a **new PvE game mode** introduced in Season 6. It is the **primary free source of named Awakening Shards** — the resource required to Awaken Kimberly, DVA, and Tesla this season.

Not to Be Confused with Warzone Expedition

Season 6 has **two completely different "Expedition" features** running on similar schedules — and the community frequently confuses them:

* **Global Expedition** (this guide) — **PvE auto-battle**, three rounds opening Weeks 2 / 4 / 6, each lasting ~14 days. Rewards named Awakening Shards. Solo activity, no PvP.
* **[Warzone Expedition](/guides/season-6/warzone-expedition-guide/)** — **PvP cross-warzone** mode starting Week 3. Outpost construction in enemy warzones, fortress and city captures, daily caps of 2 city declarations + 2 fortress captures. Different rewards, different time commitment, different alliance coordination requirements.

If you're looking for the PvP outpost-and-fortress mode, that's a separate feature — this guide covers the PvE Awakening-shard side only.

Global Expedition sits alongside the paid Battle Pass as your Awakening shard source:

SourceWhat You Get

Battle Pass (paid)70 named Awakening Shards per hero — enough to unlock the Awakening plus one tier toward the first Awakening Star

Global Expedition (free)Free Awakening Shards via PvE clears. The main source for players who don't buy the Battle Pass

Free Doesn't Mean Optional

**If you want your Awakening shards as a free-to-play player, Global Expedition is not optional.** Awakening requires **50 named shards per hero**. You must clear every available Global Expedition stage in Weeks 2, 4, and 6 to accumulate enough free shards for even one Awakening. Miss a week and you fall behind.
