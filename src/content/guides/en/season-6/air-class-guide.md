# Aircraft Squad Guide (Season 6)

Heroes & Squads · 2026-04-25

## Aircraft in Season 6: DVA Awakening

Aircraft squads enter Season 6 awaiting their signature upgrade: **DVA's Awakening unlocks in Week 4**. Until that point, Aircraft players are running their Season 5 baseline. From Week 4, the squad's burst damage steps up to a new tier — provided you've prepared the Awakening prerequisites.

Aircraft has dominated PvP since Season 1 and continues that role in S6 with the Murphy Exception, T11 troops, and Morrison's existing defence shred. The faction-warfare context (Altars, City Destruction) makes burst-damage rallies more decisive than ever — Aircraft is the offensive type of choice for delete-the-back-row plays.

Season 6 Aircraft

**Aircraft remains the PvP king, with DVA Awakening Week 4 raising her ceiling further.** The Awakening prerequisite (DVA's Energy Master at Level 20+) is the main thing to verify before Week 4 — without it, you cannot Awaken her on schedule. Missile squads (with Fiona's EW and Venom UR) remain a genuine counter, so type-matching remains critical for offensive rallies and City Destruction attacks.
