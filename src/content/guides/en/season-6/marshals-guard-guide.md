# Marshal's Guard Guide (Season 6)

Alliance · 2026-04-25

Season 6 Context

The core mechanics in this guide are largely unchanged from prior seasons. Season 6 (Lost Rainforest) layers **faction warfare** and the new progression systems on top — see the [Season 6 Overview](/guides/season-6/overview/), [Factions Guide](/guides/season-6/factions-guide/), [Altars Guide](/guides/season-6/altars-guide/), and [City Destruction Guide](/guides/season-6/city-destruction-guide/) for what's new. Hero Awakening for Kimberly (W1), DVA (W4), and Tesla (W7) is covered in the [Hero Awakening Guide](/guides/general/hero-awakening-guide/).

## Marshal's Guard in Season 6

Marshal's Guard in Season 6 features the highest damage output in the game's history. T11 troops, the full Exclusive Weapon roster from prior seasons, **Hero Awakening for Kimberly (W1), DVA (W4), and Tesla (W7)**, and Fungus Institute faction buffs combine to create rallies that would have been unimaginable in pre-season. If your alliance is not pushing the highest available difficulty, you are leaving rewards on the table.

For the full event mechanics, see the [Marshal's Guard Guide](/guides/general/marshals-guard-guide/).

Push Maximum Difficulty

**If your alliance was clearing Level 11-12 in Season 4, you should be targeting Level 13-15+ in Season 6.** The Awakening unlocks for Kimberly, DVA, and Tesla, plus T11 troops and Fungus Institute faction buffs, make your rallies dramatically stronger. Every difficulty level increase improves rewards.
