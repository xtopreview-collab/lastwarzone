# Factions Guide: Deepwood vs Wetland

Season Mechanics · 2026-04-24

## The Three Clans of Season 6

Season 6 introduces a three-clan world, but only two of them are playable:

ClanRole

Deepwood ClanPlayable faction — controls 4 warzones (forest and highland territory)

Wetland ClanPlayable faction — controls 4 warzones (lowland and river territory)

Great River ClanNeutral peacekeepers — NOT playable. Controls the central region and its Altars

The two playable factions — Deepwood and Wetland — are **mechanically symmetric**. Buildings, research, hero availability, and faction rewards are the same for both. The choice between them is almost entirely a **social/alliance** choice, not a power one.
