# Gear Guide (Season 6)

Gear · 2026-04-25

## Gear in Season 6

By Season 6, your primary squad should have full Mythic or near-Mythic gear on your carry heroes. The gear system itself has not changed since prior seasons, but **Hero Awakening reshapes the investment trade-off**: every shard or material spent on gear competes with EW Lv20+ progression and Awakening Shard accumulation. Knowing when gear is the right investment vs when to redirect to Awakening prerequisites is the core S6 gear question.

Awakening Changes the Maths

**If your carry's Exclusive Weapon is below Level 20, push the EW first — even at the expense of gear progress.** Awakening requires EW Lv20+ as a hard prerequisite. A 5-star Mythic-geared Kimberly without EW Lv20+ cannot Awaken in Week 1; her gear sits idle while she waits for a level you should have finished. Gear and weapons are multiplicative, but the gating sequence in S6 is EW first, gear second, Awakening shards third.
