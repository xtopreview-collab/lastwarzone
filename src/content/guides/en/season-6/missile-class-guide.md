# Missile Vehicle Squad Guide (Season 6)

Heroes & Squads · 2026-04-25

## Missiles in Season 6: Tesla Awakening

Season 6 brings the third Awakening to Missile squads: **Tesla's Awakening unlocks in Week 7**. Until then, Missile players run on the Season 5 baseline (Fiona's EW, Venom UR, Tesla's Inductive Current EW). After Week 7, Tesla becomes the squad's signature carry with even higher energy-damage output.

The Missile squad enters S6 already strong: Fiona's Season 5 EW transformed her into a top-tier carry, Venom's S5 UR promotion is established, T11 troops are baseline, and McGregor's taunt remains the foundation of Missile defence. Season 6 layers Tesla Awakening on top of all that.

Tesla Awakening Is the Season's Endpoint

**Missile players have the longest runway in S6 — Tesla awakens Week 7.** Use the time before then to push Tesla's Exclusive Weapon (Inductive Current) to Level 20+ if it's not there yet, accumulate Tesla shards from War Merit Shop and Global Expedition, and gear up for the moment Tesla's Awakening goes live. Missile is also a strong defensive squad in S6 — Adam's Counter Defense and Fiona's Radiation DoT shine in long City Destruction defences.
