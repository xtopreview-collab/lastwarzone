# Rare Soil War Guide (Season 2)

Season Events · 2026-09-07

## What Is Rare Soil War?

Rare Soil War is the **headline PvP event of Season 2**. From Week 4 through Week 7, alliances declare war on each other's **Alliance Furnaces** in scheduled war windows. Winning steals a percentage of the defender's accumulated Rare Soil — which directly impacts season-end rewards.

It is fundamentally an attack-vs-defence game on a single building (the Furnace) inside a 40-minute window, with tiered rank-group matchmaking and a one-shot Warlord-only nuclear option.

This Is the Season 2 PvP Centrepiece

**If your alliance is competitive at all, Rare Soil War is the main event of Season 2.** The Rare Soil transfers in Weeks 6-7 are 30% per successful war — losing one war late in the season can wipe out weeks of farming. Defensive preparation is as important as offensive coordination. Alliances that ignore Rare Soil War will find their season-end totals far behind alliances that engaged.
