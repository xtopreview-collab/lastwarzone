# Tech Center Research — Season 2 (Polar World) + The Age of Oil

Research Priority · 2026-06-04

## Season 2 in One Sentence

Season 2 — **Polar World** — is a 48-day frozen map where **temperature management** (furnaces + **Coal**) is survival-critical, **Titanium Alloy** is the new material, and alliances are ranked by **total Rare Soil accumulated**. And it ends with the single biggest progression jump since T10: **The Age of Oil**, which finally lifts your HQ past 30.

The Age of Oil belongs here — not Season 1

Oil, Oil Wells and the Age of Oil tech tree are introduced at the **end of Season 2** (the "Age of Oil" / Season 2 – Celebration phase). It needs **Tech Center 30** plus part of the **Special Forces** research, and its final research lifts your HQ cap from 30 to **35**. Core-tree node names are confirmed from our data; the temperature/Coal seasonal research is confirmed at the mechanic level (confirm exact node names in-game). New to the series? Start with [Pre-Season](/guides/tech-center/pre-season/) and [Season 1](/guides/tech-center/season-1/).

## Season 2 Priority Order

### 1. Seasonal: temperature & Coal research *(survival-critical)*

The cold degrades your base if you ignore it. Prioritise the season's **furnace / warmth / Coal-efficiency research** the moment it opens — it keeps production, training and gathering running. Treat it as a gate: nothing else matters if your base is freezing.

### 2. Lean economy toward Rare Soil accumulation

Because S2 ranks by **total Rare Soil accumulated** (not territory held), sustained output wins. Finish any **Economy** nodes you skipped — the **Output** lines and the **More Farmlands / Iron Mines / Gold Mines** capacity unlocks. Remember you still have **only two research labs** until the Age of Oil arrives, so keep both busy.

### 3. Core combat: Type Mastery + Tactical Weapon + Hero

Keep converting to squad power: deepen your one **Tank / Missile / Aircraft Mastery** (Synergy → Damage → March Size → direct stats → Ultimate Defense, tier I then II), push **Tactical Weapon** (**Support: Hero**, **Enhancement: Drone**, **Limit Break: Skill Star I/II**), and continue your squad's **Hero** line.

### 4. End of Season 2 — THE AGE OF OIL *(the big payoff)*

When Polar World ends, the server enters the **Age of Oil**: **oil** becomes a new resource and your HQ can finally pass 30. Open the **Age of Oil** tech tree and work it in order — **rush Fast Builder V + the 3rd Tech Center first**:

1. **Unlock Oil Well** — opens the oil economy
2. **Food Output V · Iron Output V · Coin Output V**
3. **Oil Output I**
4. **Fast Builder V · Research Enhancement V** ← top-tier construction & research speed
5. **3rd Tech Center** ← a **third research queue** (huge throughput jump)
6. **Focused Training V · Rapid Field Dressing V**
7. **Oil Output II**
8. **Infirmary Expansion V · Emergency Capacity I**
9. **More Oil Wells**
10. **Training Cost I · Healing Cost II** — cheaper troops and heals
11. **Parts Workshop** ← a source of **Drone Parts** (the drone bottleneck)
12. **Oil Output III**
13. **Base Expansion** ← lifts your HQ cap past 30 (toward **HQ 35**)

**Why:** this single phase hands you elite build/research speed, a **third lab** that accelerates everything afterward, oil income, cheaper training/healing, a **Drone Parts** source, and the **HQ 31–35** runway. It's the biggest jump since unlocking T10.

### 5. Cheap defensive picks

Round out **Defense Fortifications** (**Extra Hospitals → Hold the Line / Counter Defense / Solid Defense**) — sustain matters in a long attrition season.
