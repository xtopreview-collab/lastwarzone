# Aircraft Squad Guide (Season 2)

Heroes & Squads · 2026-03-24

## Aircraft in Season 2: Still the King

Aircraft squads entered Season 1 as the dominant meta and Season 2 only widens the gap. Carlie's Exclusive Weapon is the marquee addition — it boosts her HP, Defence, and Attack, making the Aircraft front line significantly tougher. Combined with DVA's Energy Master and Murphy's Mitigation Master, the Murphy Exception formation is now the undisputed best PvP squad in the game.

Carlie's Exclusive Weapon

**Carlie's Exclusive Weapon provides approximately 10% increase to HP, Defence, and Attack per level.** This is a well-rounded power boost that makes Carlie viable as a front-row tank even in the Murphy Exception formation for players who lack Murphy investment. For standard Aircraft players, it closes the gap between the standard formation and the Murphy Exception, giving both variants a significant power spike.
