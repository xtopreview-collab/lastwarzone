# Chip Lab Guide (Season 2)

Buildings · 2026-03-24

## Chip Lab in Season 2

By Season 2, you should have a complete legendary chip set (4 chips) for your primary squad and be working on ascension and Combat Boost. The Chip Lab itself does not gain new features in Season 2 — Levels 31-35 require Age of Oil, which is a post-Season 2 unlock. Your focus is on maximising what you already have.

For the full Chip Lab mechanics, see the [Pre-Season 1 Chip Lab Guide](/guides/pre-season-1/chip-lab-guide/).

Season 2 Chip Lab Status

**Your Chip Lab should still be at Level 20.** Levels 21-30 are not worth upgrading in Season 2 — the resources are better spent on furnace upgrades and other buildings. Levels 31-35 require Age of Oil research that is not available until after Season 2. Do not waste resources upgrading the Chip Lab during this season.
