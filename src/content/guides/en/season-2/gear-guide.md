# Gear Guide (Season 2)

Gear · 2026-03-24

## Gear in Season 2

By Season 2, your primary squad should have all 20 gold gear pieces crafted and many of them at 2-star or higher. The gear system itself has not changed, but Season 2 introduces a new tension: **furnace upgrades compete with gear upgrades for your time and resources**. Managing both is the key challenge.

For the full gear mechanics (crafting, levelling, materials), see the [Pre-Season 1 Gear Guide](/guides/pre-season-1/gear-guide/). For Season 1 star promotion priorities, see the [Season 1 Gear Guide](/guides/season-1/gear-guide/).

The Season 2 Gear Reality

**Gear progression continues but is no longer your only upgrade priority.** Your High-Heat Furnace provides temperature (survival) AND virus resistance (combat power). War Buildings at Furnace Level 30 provide up to +15% damage for your squad type. These compete with gear for your attention and resources. The right approach is to advance both — not to neglect one for the other.
