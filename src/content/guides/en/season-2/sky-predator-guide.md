# Sky Predator (Season 2)

Alliance · 2026-03-24

## Sky Battlefront in Season 2

By Season 2, your alliance has two full seasons of progression behind it. Exclusive Weapons are approaching Level 30 across most squad members. Carlie's new Exclusive Weapon adds durability to Aircraft rally teams. War Buildings at Furnace Level 30 add +15% damage. Your rallies hit harder, survive longer, and your coordination should be sharp.

For the full event mechanics, see the [Sky Predator Guide](/guides/general/sky-predator-guide/).

Season 2 Power Spike

**Season 2 is the first season where most competitive alliances have fully maxed Exclusive Weapons AND War Building bonuses.** This creates a massive damage increase over Season 1. If your alliance could clean shield-break in Season 1, you can now push for faster clears and higher damage thresholds. If you struggled with shield-breaks before, the extra damage may push you over the threshold.
