# Alliance Train (Season 2)

Alliance · 2026-03-24

## Alliance Train in Season 2

The Alliance Train continues as a daily routine in Season 2. The mechanics are unchanged, but Season 2's resource economy shifts what wagons you prioritise and how the train fits into your daily schedule alongside furnace management, coal gathering, and Beast Crisis preparation.

For the full mechanics breakdown, see the [Alliance Train Guide](/guides/general/alliance-train-guide/).

Season 2 Train Priority

**The Alliance Train is still a daily non-negotiable, but wagon priorities have shifted.** Coal and Titanium Alloy are now critical resources that may appear in wagon selections. These Season 2 resources change which wagons you grab. The train's daily rewards compound — skipping days during Season 2 because "there is too much else to do" is a mistake.
