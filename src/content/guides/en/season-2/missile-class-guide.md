# Missile Vehicle Squad Guide (Season 2)

Heroes & Squads · 2026-03-24

## Missiles in Season 2: More Essential Than Ever

The Aircraft meta continues to dominate in Season 2, and Carlie's Exclusive Weapon makes Aircraft even stronger. That means Missile squads are more important than ever. Every alliance needs Missile players to break through Aircraft-garrisoned cities and defend against Aircraft rallies. If your alliance lacks Missile coverage, you will lose every engagement against Aircraft-heavy opponents.

Season 2 Missile Reality

**Carlie's Exclusive Weapon makes Aircraft tougher to fight, but the combat triangle still holds.** Missiles still counter Aircraft. Tesla's energy damage and Fiona's Atomic Blast still shred DVA-led squads. Your role has not changed — if anything, the demand for Missile players in your alliance has increased because there are more Aircraft squads to counter.
