# Hero Guide (Season 2)

Heroes & Squads · 2026-03-24

## What Changes in Season 2

Season 2 brings one major hero change and shifts the context in which heroes operate:

1. **Carlie gets her Exclusive Weapon** — she rises in the tier list with a well-rounded HP, Defence, and Attack boost
2. **No new UR promotions** — the hero roster is the same as Season 1
3. **All Season 1 heroes and weapons carry forward** — Mason remains UR, all Exclusive Weapons remain active
4. **Season 2 content priorities shift** — Beast Crisis defence, city garrison, and Rare Soil capture determine which heroes matter most

For core hero mechanics (rarities, skills, star upgrades, Tactics cards), see the [Pre-Season 1 Hero Guide](/guides/pre-season-1/hero-guide/). For Season 1 tier changes, see the [Season 1 Hero Guide](/guides/season-1/hero-guide/).
