# Zombie Siege (Season 2)

Alliance · 2026-03-24

## Zombie Siege in Season 2

Two full seasons of progression mean your alliance can push Zombie Siege to its highest difficulty levels. Gold gear at 2-4 stars, Exclusive Weapons at Level 25-30, and War Building damage bonuses make your defences dramatically stronger. Cold weather does not affect Zombie Siege directly — the event runs in its own instance — but Season 2's resource demands affect your overall readiness.

For the full event mechanics, see the [Zombie Siege Guide](/guides/general/zombie-siege-guide/).

Maximum Difficulty

**If your alliance was clearing Level 8-10 in Season 1, push to the maximum available difficulty in Season 2.** Exclusive Weapons at Level 30, star-promoted gear, and War Buildings create a power level that trivialises lower difficulties. If you are clearing all 20 waves with 10+ minutes left, you are wasting potential rewards. Push higher.
