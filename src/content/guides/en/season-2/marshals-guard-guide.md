# Marshal's Guard (Season 2)

Alliance · 2026-03-24

## Marshal's Guard in Season 2

Marshal's Guard continues every 2-3 days in Season 2. Your alliance is stronger than ever — two full seasons of gear progression, maxed Exclusive Weapons, and Carlie's new weapon give you significantly more damage output. Temperature management adds a minor logistics concern but does not change the event mechanics.

For the full event mechanics, see the [Marshal's Guard Guide](/guides/general/marshals-guard-guide/).

Push Even Higher

**If your alliance was clearing Level 9-11 in Season 1, you should be targeting Level 11-13+ in Season 2.** Two seasons of progression mean every rally hits harder. Exclusive Weapons at Level 30, 2-4 star gold gear, and War Building damage bonuses create a massive power increase over your Season 1 performance. The rewards at higher difficulty levels are substantially better.
