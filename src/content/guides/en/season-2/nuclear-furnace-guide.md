# Reactivate the Nuclear Furnace (Season 2) — Glacieradon & Warzone Ranking

Season Events · 2026-09-07

## What This Event Is

The **Nuclear Furnace** sits at the polar centre of the Season 2 map. In the season's story it is the heat distribution hub — all warmth radiates from it — which is exactly why Emperor Boreas froze it and plunged the region into ice. The city furnaces you have been running until now are temporary stand-ins.

**Reactivate the Nuclear Furnace** is the event where the warzone takes it back. You capture the Furnace, then march troops into it to build **activation progress**. When that progress fills, the Furnace switches on and heats the entire map.

Two things stand between you and that: the progress bar is large, and **four Glacieradons** are walking toward the Furnace knocking chunks out of it.

This Is a Warzone Event, Not an Alliance One

**Warzones are ranked against each other by how fast they activate the Furnace, and the faster warzones earn a higher Furnace temperature.** That temperature is a standing benefit for everybody in your warzone for the rest of the season — it is not a one-off chest. A warzone that squabbles internally and activates slowly is colder than its neighbours until Season 2 ends.
