# Polar Dishes Guide (Season 2)

Season Events · 2026-08-17

## Meet Snowflake

Polar Dishes is Season 2's **daily cooking activity**. You look after **Snowflake**, a stray **Siberian Husky** that led an avalanche rescue team to survivors (it loves rolling in the snow — hence the name). Feed it each day and it digs treasures out of the snow for you. The presentation is whimsical, but the rewards are scarce Season 2 resources — so treat it as a daily.

Don't just cook — MATCH the taste

Polar Dishes runs daily from roughly **Week 2 to the end of Season 2**. The key isn't cooking *anything* — it's cooking what **Snowflake wants that day**. The game is explicit: *"if the food matches the dog's taste, the rewards will be better,"* and *"the better the match, the higher the quality of the chest."* A matched cook gives a much better chest than a mismatch (which just gets you a "please follow the recipe"). One matched cook a day is one of the best time-for-reward activities in S2.
