# Season 2 Timeline & Week-by-Week Roadmap

Season Overview · 2026-04-25

## How Season 2 Plays Out

Season 2 is **survival season**. Temperature gates everything: drop below -20°C and you lose the ability to rally, teleport, gather, and heal at full speed. The whole season is structured around managing heat sources while accumulating Rare Soil — the ranking resource that rewards consistency over burst. Season 2 runs roughly **8-10 weeks**.

The Two Clocks of Season 2

**Furnace progression is your survival clock. Rare Soil accumulation is your ranking clock.** Both run from Day 1. Falling behind on the Furnace gets you locked out of combat during Cold Waves; falling behind on Rare Soil leaves you out of contention for end-of-season rewards. There is no catching up on either — you are either ahead of schedule or losing.
