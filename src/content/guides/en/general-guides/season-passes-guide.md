# Season Passes Guide — Every Purchasable Pass in Last War

Economy · 2026-05-03

## What Counts as a "Season Pass"?

In Last War: Survival, "pass" is a category that covers every recurring or season-bound product with the **Free / Advanced / Premium tier structure**, plus a handful of subscription-style products like the Super Monthly Pass. They are different from one-off bundle packs (Growth Fund, EW shards, hero packs) — passes deliver rewards over time as you complete tasks, not all at once.

This guide is the **catalogue**. For deep strategy on the seasonal Battle Pass specifically, see the [Battle Pass Guide](/guides/general/battle-pass-guide/).

The 2026 Monetisation Update Changed What's Available

**Several passes were removed or reworked in a monetisation update earlier this year.** The Awakening Battle Pass is gone (Awakening Shards are now F2P-only via Hero Awakening Trial + Global Expedition). Some Custom Weekly Pass appearances have been pulled from rotation. Always confirm a pass is currently available in your in-game shop before planning spend around it — outdated guides (including older versions of this site's content) may still describe removed passes.

## Seasonal Battle Pass

The headline pass for each season. One Seasonal Battle Pass per season, lasting the full season.

* **Free track**: Modest rewards at every tier — gems, speedups, basic resources, some hero shards
* **Advanced (paid)**: 500 Gold Bricks (~$4.99 equivalent). Unlocks the mid-tier reward path
* **Premium (paid)**: 2,000 Gold Bricks (~$19.99 equivalent). Unlocks the top reward path on top of Advanced
* **Season-specific currency**: Each season's BP gives a season-themed currency (e.g. Rainforest Mushrooms in Season 6) usable in the season store

**How to buy**: Tap the Battle Pass icon (top of main map) → choose Advanced or Premium → confirm with Gold Bricks. Buying early in the season is mandatory — purchase retroactively unlocks all rewards from tiers you've already passed, but you lose out on bonus task points if you delay.

For full strategy on tier targets, premium-vs-free ROI, and late-season buying maths, see the [Battle Pass Guide](/guides/general/battle-pass-guide/).

## Hero Growth Battle Pass

A separate, shorter pass that recurs in the Hot Deals during specific weeks of the season (typically **Weeks 2 and 6**).

* **Tiers**: Free / Advanced (500 GB) / Premium (2,000 GB) — same structure as Seasonal BP
* **Reward focus**: Hero progression — Universal Hero Shards or Exclusive Weapon Shards for the featured hero
* **Tasks**: Daily and target tasks similar to the Seasonal BP, but on a compressed timeline
* **Best for**: Players actively building the featured hero. Skip if the featured hero isn't on your active squad

**How to buy**: Hot Deals icon → look for the Hero Growth Battle Pass card → Advanced or Premium.

Match Hero Growth BP to Your Active Carry

**Hero Growth Battle Pass shards are hero-specific.** Buying it for a hero you don't intend to invest in wastes the entire premium fee. Check which hero is featured before purchasing — if it's not your main rally lead or VS Day 5 carry, the value drops sharply. Same logic applies to Exclusive Weapon shard rewards.

## Special Training Pass

A 7-day pass focused on **gear power** — gear upgrade materials, ceramic, blueprints, training-related items.

* **Tiers**: Free / Advanced (500 GB) / Premium (2,000 GB)
* **Reward focus**: Gear progression materials — most useful during HQ pushes when you're upgrading multiple gear pieces in parallel
* **Best for**: Mid-game players (HQ 18-30) actively upgrading gear stars

**How to buy**: Hot Deals icon → Special Training Pass card. Recurring — appears every few weeks.

## Drone Training Pass

A 7-day pass focused on **drone progression** — Drone Cogs, drone parts, and drone-related materials. Same Free / Advanced (500 GB) / Premium (2,000 GB) structure.

**Best for**: Players actively progressing their drone tier (especially during pre-season and post-merge windows when drone power matters most). Skip if your drone is parked at a comfortable tier.

**How to buy**: Hot Deals → Drone Training Pass when in rotation.

## Custom Weekly Pass

A 7-day pass with **player-chosen daily rewards** from a menu of options.

* **Premium tier**: 500 GB. Includes 500 Diamonds, gold currency, 500 VIP Points, 1 Alliance Gift, 50 Energy Badges as instant rewards. Choose **2 daily rewards** from the 8-option menu
* **Elite tier**: 10,000 GB (~$99 equivalent — heavy spender territory). On top of Premium, adds 1,000 more diamonds, 10,000 VIP Points, 1 more Alliance Gift, 100 more Energy Badges. Choose **4 daily rewards**
* **Daily reward menu**: 2 Hero Weapon Shards, 10 Drone Cogs, 40 Dielectric Ceramics, 2 UR Resource Choice Chests, 6 SSR Hero EXP Items, 100 Stamina, 100×5-min Speed-Ups, 1,000 Upgrade Ore — each option choosable once per pass

Custom Weekly Pass Status — Verify In-Game

**The Custom Weekly Pass was reportedly pulled from rotation as part of the 2026 monetisation update, but some sources still list it as active in Hot Deals.** If you don't see it in your in-game Hot Deals tab, it's currently paused for your region/server. The Premium tier (500 GB) was traditionally one of the better value passes; the Elite tier at 10,000 GB is whale-tier and almost never the right call.

## Super Monthly Pass

A 30-day subscription-style pack — the **only pass permanently available in the shop** (not tied to events or seasons).

* **Cost**: ~$24.99 / 2,500 Gold Bricks
* **Instant rewards**: 2,500 Gems + 300 VIP Points
* **30-day perks**: Full VIP privileges at your current VIP level, extra marching squad, Garage 4 unlock, +10% gathering speed, -5% damage from map monsters, exclusive avatar frame, chat translation
* **30-day daily payout (cumulative)**: ~5,500 gems, 3,000+ stamina, 60 Hero Recruitment Tickets, 150+ hours universal speedups, 375+ ceramic upgrade materials, plus super alloy and resource drops

**How to buy**: Shop icon → Subscriptions or Monthly Pass card → confirm purchase.

Super Monthly Pass Is the Best Subscription Value in the Game

**The 30-day cumulative payout exceeds 8,000% return on the upfront cost.** The extra marching squad alone is a meaningful PvE/gathering boost; the daily gem and speedup drip turns spendable cash into permanent compounding progression. If you spend at all on the game, the Super Monthly Pass is widely considered the highest-ROI single subscription. Only skip it if you're committed to F2P or already past the spending tier where its daily rewards matter (HQ 35+, mature account).

## Event Battle Passes

Limited-time Battle Passes tied to real-world events: New Year, Easter, Halloween, anniversaries, etc.

* **Same Free / Advanced / Premium tier structure** as the Seasonal BP
* **Event-specific currency** that funnels into the event store (New Year Tokens, Easter Eggs, etc.)
* **Duration**: Typically 7-14 days, matching the event window
* **Value**: Highly variable — read the rewards carefully. Event passes that include heroes shards or exclusive cosmetics tend to be A-tier; pure-currency event passes are often skippable

**How to buy**: Event hub icon (appears on main map during the event) → Battle Pass tab.

## Awakening Battle Pass — Removed

The Awakening Battle Pass historically delivered **70 named Awakening Shards** for a season's featured Awakening hero. It was removed as part of the 2026 monetisation update. Awakening Shards are now obtained exclusively through:

1. **Hero Awakening Trial** — up to 10 shards per hero, F2P
2. **Global Expedition** (Weeks 2, 4, 6 of an Awakening season) — the primary shard source, F2P

If a guide on this site or elsewhere still mentions the Awakening Battle Pass, treat that section as out of date.

## In-Game Currency: Gold Bricks

Every paid pass tier is priced in **Gold Bricks** — Last War's premium currency. You buy Gold Bricks with real money via:

* **Top-up** in the in-game shop (multiple denominations, larger packs give bonus bricks)
* **Third-party top-up sites** can offer 15-30% discounts on Gold Bricks — verify your server supports these before purchasing

## Pass Priority Order — Where to Spend First

For accounts that spend at all, the order of operations:

1. **Super Monthly Pass** — best dollar-for-dollar pass in the game. Renew every 30 days as long as you're playing actively
2. **Seasonal Battle Pass Premium** — buy in Week 1 of every season. Two-thousand Gold Bricks for a full season of compounding rewards is consistently strong value
3. **Hero Growth Battle Pass** — only when the featured hero is on your active squad. Premium tier (2,000 GB) preferred over Advanced (500 GB) for the EW shards
4. **Special Training Pass** — during HQ push windows when you're upgrading multiple gear pieces. Skip when your gear is stable
5. **Drone Training Pass** — only if you're actively pushing drone tier
6. **Event Battle Passes** — case-by-case. Read the rewards, skip the pure-cosmetic ones
7. **Custom Weekly Premium** — when in rotation and the daily menu lines up with your needs (Hero Weapon Shards + Drone Cogs is the usual high-value combo)
8. **Custom Weekly Elite** — almost never. Whale-tier only

**For F2P**: Free track on every pass that runs. The Free track on the Seasonal BP alone delivers significant cumulative gems and speedups across a season. Free track tasks also stack with daily routines you're already running.

## How to Buy Any Pass — Step-by-Step

1. **Open Last War: Survival**
2. **Find the pass**:
   * Seasonal BP → top of main map (Battle Pass icon)
   * Hero Growth / Special Training / Drone Training / Custom Weekly → **Hot Deals** icon (typically right side of map)
   * Super Monthly Pass → **Shop** → Subscriptions tab
   * Event Battle Pass → **Event hub** icon during the event window
3. **Tap the tier** you want (Advanced or Premium)
4. **Confirm payment** in Gold Bricks (top up first if needed)
5. **The pass activates immediately** — past task progress retroactively unlocks rewards

If you don't see a pass listed here in your in-game shop, it may not be in current rotation for your server, or it may have been removed in a recent update.

## Common Pass Mistakes

1. **Buying Hero Growth BP for a hero you're not building** — shards are hero-specific; matching to your active carry is non-negotiable
2. **Delaying Seasonal BP purchase past Week 2** — you miss the bonus task points that buyers get retroactively, reducing total tier reach
3. **Buying Custom Weekly Elite at 10,000 GB** — the marginal extra value over Premium rarely justifies the 20× cost step. Whale-territory only
4. **Skipping the Super Monthly Pass while spending on individual gem packs** — the Monthly Pass delivers more gems over 30 days than two $9.99 generic gem packs combined
5. **Buying the Special Training Pass when your gear is stable** — it's optimised for active gear-upgrade windows, not idle accounts
6. **Confusing the Seasonal BP with the Hero Growth BP** — they run in parallel. Premium-buying both during a season is a real cost; pick one if budget is tight
7. **Buying event passes for the rewards without checking the event store conversion rate** — sometimes the event currency converts poorly back into useful items
8. **Looking for the Awakening Battle Pass** — it was removed. All Awakening Shards now come from Hero Awakening Trial + Global Expedition (free)
