# Arms Race Guide

Events · 2026-04-25

## What Is Arms Race?

Arms Race is the **daily individual competition event** that runs continuously in Last War: Survival. It is the second-most important event after Alliance Duel VS, and it runs **every day** — meaning what you do in your day-to-day base development directly competes against ~12-15 other players at your level for daily ranking rewards.

Arms Race Is Always On

Unlike Alliance Duel VS (six days a week with a Sunday recovery), **Arms Race runs 24/7 in 4-hour phases**. Every troop you train, every research you complete, every drone hit you take in combat is scoring you Arms Race points whether you realise it or not. Players who time their actions to match the active phase score 2-3x more than players who don't.

## Daily Structure

Arms Race resets at **00:00:00 server time** every day. The day is split into **six 4-hour slots** that cycle through five thematic phases (one phase repeats):

The event rewards timing, not spending. Hoard the matching resource while its phase is closed, then release the whole stockpile the moment it opens.

## The Five Phases

Each 4-hour slot is one of these five phase types. Activities scoring during the active phase count for **multiplied points**:

### 1. Hero Advancement

Score points by:

* Levelling heroes (XP gains count, including Hero Trial XP)
* Recruiting new heroes via summons
* Star-progressing heroes (each star milestone)
* Upgrading hero skills

**Strategy**: stockpile hero EXP books and skill medals during off-phase; use them during this phase. Pull on summon banners during this phase if you're going to pull anyway.

### 2. City Building

Score points by:

* Completing building upgrades (the moment a build finishes counts, not when you start)
* Power gained from new buildings

**Strategy**: time your large building completions for this phase. Use construction speed-ups to **finish** during the phase — start the build before it begins, then speed up to land the completion inside the phase. This is the highest-value use of speed-ups in the game.

### 3. Unit Progression

Score points by:

* Training new troops
* **Promoting troops up tiers** (this is huge — see [Waterfall Training Guide](/guides/general/waterfall-training-guide/))
* Power from upgraded units

**Strategy**: this is the phase where waterfall training pays off. Save your upgrade chain for Unit Progression phases. A staggered barracks setup can earn 3-4x more points by promoting up the chain during this phase than by training fresh troops.

### 4. Tech Research

Score points by:

* Completing research nodes in the Tech Center
* Power gained from new tech

**Strategy**: time your big research completions for this phase. Long research nodes (multi-day) should always finish during a Tech Research phase, not before. Use research speed-ups to land the completion inside the window. Critical for the Special Forces T10 unlock chain — see the [T10 Troops Guide](/guides/general/t10-troops-guide/).

### 5. Drone Boost

Score points by:

* Using drone combat data (10 points per use)
* Spending stamina in PvE combat (zombies, monsters, sky predator, rampage bosses)

**Strategy**: hold stamina and drone data for this phase. **Never burn stamina outside the Drone Boost window unless you have a specific reason** — you are leaving Arms Race points on the table. Boss event timing should align with this phase wherever possible.

## How Scoring Works

Each phase has its own **Phase Chest progression**:

Phase ChestReward

First Phase Chest1 Arms Race Badge

Second Phase Chest2 Arms Race Badges

Third Phase Chest3 Arms Race Badges

So **6 badges per phase × 6 slots = 36 badges per day** if you maximise every chest in every phase. Realistically, hitting all three chests in every phase requires having activities prepared across all five phase types — most players hit fewer than 30/day.

### Daily Goal Chests

In addition to per-phase chests, the daily badge total unlocks **Daily Goal Chests** with bigger rewards:

* **Bronze Daily Chest** — unlocks at the lower badge threshold
* **Silver Daily Chest** — middle threshold
* **Gold Daily Chest** — typically unlocks at **18 badges total per day** for the highest rewards

Daily Goal Chests are where the **speed-ups, hero fragments, and EW materials** come from. Hitting Gold every day is what separates serious Arms Race players from casual ones.

## Daily Ranking

Every day at 00:00 server time, **12-15 Commanders of similar Main Base levels are randomly matched** for that day's competition. You compete against this group on **total daily points**.

Ranking rewards are sent via in-game mail at the end of the day. Higher placement = more rewards (extra badges, extra speed-ups, hero fragments, sometimes Awakening shards or EW materials).

**This is where the slot-swap matters** (see below) — squeezing ranking by scoring slightly higher than your matched opponents has compounding rewards.

## The Slot Swap Feature

Once per day, you can **swap your current phase slot with a different time block**. The mechanics:

* **One swap per day** — no repeats
* **No extra rewards from swapping** — the daily total is still 36 badges max
* **Blocked when unclaimed rewards exist** in the current block, or when **less than 5 minutes remain** in the active phase

### What slot swap is for

The point of the swap is not to score more — it is to **complete two different phase types during a single time period**. For example:

* You are mid-day in a Hero Advancement phase, but Unit Progression is the next slot. You have just promoted a big batch of troops via waterfall, but Hero Advancement points are easy and you've already burned through your hero levelling stockpile
* Swap the current slot to Unit Progression — now your troop promotions count for the multiplier

Use cases:

* You miss a phase you were prepared for (oversleep the City Building window) — swap a less-prepared slot to it
* A specific phase aligns with an event happening right now (Zombie Invasion landing in a Drone Boost window your server doesn't currently have)
* You want to stack two strong phases back-to-back when you have a big completion ready

Don't burn the swap on optimization. Save it for when you genuinely need to rebalance.

## Optimisation Tips

### General Pattern

The day's pattern most serious players use:

1. **Check the day's slot order** at 00:00 server time (or first time you log in)
2. **Plan completions to land inside phase windows** — not just any time during the day
3. **Save speed-ups for completions**, not for starting tasks
4. **Hold stamina and drone data** until Drone Boost windows
5. **Hit at least the second Phase Chest** in every phase you can — that's 3 badges per phase × 6 = 18 badges, the typical Gold Daily Chest threshold
6. **Use slot swap reactively, not proactively** — only when something disrupts your plan

### Phase-Specific Stockpiles

Maintain a baseline stockpile for each phase type so you always have ammo:

### Coordinate with Alliance Duel VS

Arms Race phases align with VS days but aren't identical:

Alliance Duel runs a different **theme** each day. The themes the game names are **Base Expansion**, **Age of Science**, **Hero Initiative**, **Total Mobilization** and **Enemy Buster** — pair each with the Arms Race phase that scores the same actions:

* **Base Expansion** — land big construction completions; they score for both
* **Age of Science** — finish Tech Research here for double-dip points
* **Hero Initiative** — hero levelling and skill upgrades count twice over
* **Total Mobilization** — waterfall promotions score for both this and Arms Race Unit Progression

Check the Order on Your Own Server

**Which theme lands on which weekday is not something we can state reliably, so the day numbers that used to appear here have been removed.** Read the Duel screen at the start of the week and plan against what it actually shows. The pairings above hold whatever order the themes arrive in.

Doing big tasks **during a VS day that aligns with the matching Arms Race phase** is the highest-yield play in the game. Land a Tech Center upgrade during a VS Research Day in a Tech Research Arms Race phase = triple-dip points.

## Spending Arms Race Badges

Arms Race Badges are spent in the Arms Race shop on:

* **Speed-ups** (construction, research, training)
* **Hero shards** for heroes featured that week
* **Decoration upgrade materials** — see the [Decorations Tier List](/guides/general/decorations-tier-list/)
* **Skill medals**
* **Resources** (Food, Iron, Coins) — generally low-value spending

The shop rotates; check daily.

## Common Mistakes

* **Burning stamina outside Drone Boost** — biggest single Arms Race leak. Save stamina for the phase
* **Speeding up construction at random times** — speed-ups are most valuable when they cause a completion to land inside the matching phase
* **Forgetting to claim Phase Chests** — chest rewards don't auto-claim. Open them at the end of each phase or you lose the badges
* **Splitting big projects across phases** — a building upgrade started 30 minutes before a phase ends finishes during a different phase. Time starts and speed-ups to maximise overlap
* **Burning the slot swap proactively** — save it for emergencies (missed phase, specific event)
* **Hitting only the first Phase Chest** — the second and third chests give 5 of the 6 badges per phase. Push harder
* **Ignoring matchup ranking** — every additional badge over a same-bracket opponent improves your daily ranking, which compounds rewards
