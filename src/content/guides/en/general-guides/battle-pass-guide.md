# Battle Pass Guide — Free vs Premium Tracks and When to Buy

Economy · 2026-05-03

## What Is the Battle Pass?

The Battle Pass is a season-long progression track. Every season offers a Battle Pass with two paid tiers — **Advanced** (500 Gold Bricks) and **Premium** (2,000 Gold Bricks) — alongside the Free track. You earn rewards by completing daily and seasonal tasks that fill a token meter. The Pass lasts the entire season (49-90 days), and rewards range from gems and speedups to season-specific currency (e.g. Rainforest Mushrooms in Season 6).

The Battle Pass Decision Framework

**Free track is always worth running. Premium is consistently the best per-Gold-Brick season pass.** 2,000 Gold Bricks for a full season of compounding rewards is solid A-tier value in every season. The Free track on its own delivers significant cumulative gems and speedups — engage with it regardless of whether you buy Premium.

> **Note (May 2026):** Earlier versions of this guide framed the Season 6 Battle Pass as "S-tier" because it bundled 70 named Awakening Shards. **The Awakening Battle Pass was removed in the 2026 monetisation update.** Awakening Shards are now obtained exclusively through the Hero Awakening Trial and Global Expedition (both free). The Seasonal Battle Pass no longer contains Awakening Shards. See the [Season Passes Guide](/guides/general/season-passes-guide/) for the full updated catalogue and the [Hero Awakening Guide](/guides/general/hero-awakening-guide/) for current shard sources.

## Free vs Premium Track Structure

Both tracks share the same task list. Completing tasks awards Battle Pass tokens. Tokens fill a tier meter, and each tier you reach unlocks rewards on whichever tracks you have.

TrackWhat You Get

Free TrackModest rewards at every tier — gems, speedups, basic resources, some hero shards

Premium Track (paid)Substantially better rewards at every tier — more gems, more speedups, hero shards, gear, and season-specific items like Awakening Shards

You don't need to choose between them. Premium track simply adds rewards on top of the Free track for tiers you reach. Buying Premium late in the season retroactively unlocks the rewards from tiers you already passed.

## Token Earning

Tokens come from a daily task list and seasonal milestones. Typical sources:

SourceNotes

Daily loginFree baseline tokens for showing up

Daily questsTrain troops, gather resources, complete builds, etc. Most are routine play

Weekly questsLarger token rewards for bigger milestones

Event participationMarshal's Guard, Sky Predator, VS, SvS — tokens at thresholds

Season milestonesHQ pushes, hero star upgrades, gear progression

Premium token boostSome Premium passes accelerate token earning by 20-50%

A reasonably active player can complete the full Battle Pass tier track within a season without rushing. F2P players who play daily typically reach 80-100% of tiers; players who skip days fall short.

## Awakening Battle Pass (Removed)

> **Removed in the 2026 monetisation update.** Earlier in Season 6 there were three sequential Awakening Battle Passes (Kimberly Weeks 1-3, DVA Weeks 3-5, Tesla Weeks 6-8) that each rewarded ~70 named Awakening Shards on the Premium tier. **These passes have been removed entirely.** Awakening Shards are now F2P-only:
>
> 1. **Hero Awakening Trial** — up to 10 shards per hero
> 2. **Global Expedition** (Weeks 2, 4, 6 of an Awakening season) — primary shard source
>
> See the [Hero Awakening Guide](/guides/general/hero-awakening-guide/) for the current shard farming routine.

The Seasonal Battle Pass during Awakening seasons still runs — it just no longer includes named Awakening Shards. Its value reverts to the standard "solid A-tier per-Gold-Brick" framing.

## When to Buy Premium

Decision framework based on the season:

Season 6+ Awakening hero matches your primary squad**Yes** — S-tier value

Season's featured EW hero matches your squad**Yes** — strong A-tier value

Active F2P, no specific carry investmentSkip — gem savings better invested in VIP packs

Whale-tier spender pursuing top server rankYes for every BP — marginal returns add up

Account barely active, missing daily questsSkip — you won't reach enough tiers to justify Premium cost

### Activity Threshold

Premium is only worth it if you can reach the **majority of tiers**. A rough heuristic:

* **Daily login + daily quests**: ~70-80% of tiers reachable. Premium pays off
* **Weekly login only**: ~30-40% of tiers. Premium probably not worth it
* **Heavy daily play + event participation**: 100% of tiers reachable. Premium delivers full value

## Buying Premium Late in the Season

You can buy Premium Battle Pass at any point during the season and **retroactively unlock all rewards from tiers you already cleared**. This means:

* **Mid-season Premium purchases are valid** — no point penalty for buying late
* **Wait if uncertain** — buy at week 4-5 once you know whether you'll engage enough
* **End-of-season buying** is rare but works — you immediately collect the full backfill

The trade-off: buying late means you don't benefit from the **token earning boost** for the early weeks.

## Common Battle Pass Mistakes

1. **Skipping daily quests** — token earning depends on daily activity. Skipped days = skipped tiers
2. **Buying Premium for rewards you can't use** — the hero shards and EW shards on the track are hero-specific, and worth little if the featured hero isn't in your squad
3. **Premium-buying every pass that runs in parallel** — the Seasonal BP and the Hero Growth BP overlap; on a tight budget, pick the one that matches your squad
4. **Skipping Premium in a season whose featured progression matches your squad** — that is the one case where it is clearly worth it
5. **Buying Premium too late to reach high tiers** — buying in week 7 of an 8-week season caps your tier reach
6. **Forgetting that Premium retroactively unlocks** — if you've already cleared tier 30, Premium will deliver tier 1-30 rewards instantly
7. **Comparing Premium price to gem packs** — Battle Pass is closer to a subscription than a pack. Different value math
8. **Letting tokens cap** — claim rewards as you earn them; some seasons have token caps
9. **Skipping the Free track because you didn't buy Premium** — Free is always worth running
10. **Not coordinating with seasonal Hot Deal cycles** — sometimes BP is bundled with other items at a discount
