# Exclusive Weapons Guide

Heroes & Squads · 2026-04-25

## What Are Exclusive Weapons?

An **Exclusive Weapon (EW)** is a hero-specific weapon that **transforms a hero's kit**. Each EW belongs to one specific hero (e.g. Murphy's Mitigation Master, DVA's Energy Master, Tesla's Inductive Current). Equipping and upgrading the EW:

* Boosts the hero's base stats (Attack, HP, Defence)
* Unlocks **new skills** at specific level milestones
* Strengthens the hero's existing skills
* Often introduces new mechanics that change how the hero's squad operates

EWs Are the Skill-Tier Difference Between Heroes

**An Exclusive Weapon is the single biggest power transformation a hero gets.** A 5★ DVA without her Exclusive Weapon hits hard. A 5★ DVA with Energy Master at Level 20 redefines the squad. The same is true for every other EW-eligible hero. If you have a 5★ hero in your main squad and they have an EW available, getting that EW to Level 20 should be one of your top investments.

## The 30 Levels and Skill Unlocks

Exclusive Weapons have **30 levels**. Special skills unlock at four milestones:

LevelWhat Unlocks

Level 1 (initial unlock)First skill effect — usually a baseline stat buff and the EW's signature mechanic

Level 10Second skill milestone — significant scaling improvement

Level 20Third skill milestone — including a 7.5% damage/effect boost. This is the meta sweet spot

Level 30Final skill — the maximum upgrade with the strongest mechanic

Levels between milestones provide stat increases and incremental skill scaling, but **the milestone levels (1/10/20/30) are where the kit-defining skills unlock**.

## Shard Cost Per Level Bracket

Pushing the weapon level requires **named EW shards** (specific to that weapon) and/or **Universal EW shards** (any weapon):

Level RangeShard Cost

Initial unlock (Level 1)50 named shards (5★ hero required)

Level 1 → Level 201,080 additional shards (1,130 cumulative including unlock)

Level 20 → Level 301,750 additional shards

Level 1 → Level 30 total~2,880 shards

The cost curve is steeply back-loaded — most of the shard cost is in the Level 20 → 30 push. Levels 1-20 take about 40% of total cost; Levels 20-30 take 60%.

*The shard totals above are **community estimates** — the exact per-level shard curve isn't published anywhere official, so treat them as planning figures and confirm against your own weapon. The **7.5% Attack/Defense/HP boost at Level 20** is confirmed exact in-game.*

## The Level 20 Sweet Spot

Community consensus across all serious guides: **Level 20 is the right initial target**, not Level 30.

### Why Level 20 Specifically

* **The 7.5% boost** unlocks at Level 20 — significant power gain that compounds across every fight
* **It's the Hero Awakening prerequisite** — see the [Hero Awakening Guide](/guides/general/hero-awakening-guide/). You cannot Awaken Kimberly, DVA, or Tesla without their EW at Level 20+ first
* **Diminishing returns above Level 20** — going to 30 takes 1,750 more shards for incremental gains; that same shard pool covers most of a second hero's path to Level 20
* **Spreading horizontally beats stacking vertically** — five heroes at Level 20 outperform one hero at Level 30 in 90% of practical squad situations

### When to Push Past Level 20

Only push a single EW past Level 20 when:

* You are a heavy spender with the resources to do so without delaying other heroes
* The hero is your absolute primary carry and you have already brought your supporting heroes to Level 20
* Your alliance is in late-late-game cross-server PvP where every percentage point matters

For everyone else: Level 20 your main hero, then start the Level 1 → 20 push on the next hero, and so on.

## Release Schedule Per Season

Each season (Season 1 through Season 5) released **three new Exclusive Weapons** — one per troop type, on the Week 1 / Week 3 / Week 6 schedule:

WeekType

Week 1Tank EW released

Week 3Aircraft EW released

Week 6Missile EW released

This is the same release cadence Season 6's Hero Awakenings now follow — because Awakening **replaces** EWs as the season's hero progression mechanic.

### Season 6 Note: No New EWs

**Season 6 does not introduce new Exclusive Weapons.** Awakening takes that slot. However:

* Existing EWs from Seasons 1-5 still apply and matter
* Awakening **requires** the hero's existing EW at Level 20+ as a prerequisite
* So **Season 6 is a great time to push existing EWs to Level 20+** if they aren't there yet

The realistic F2P path: prioritise Black Market boxes between seasons, ride seasonal Hot Deals carefully, and accept that you will run **one EW season behind** spenders who buy each Battle Pass.

## EW Investment Order

The investment-order question is "**which EWs do I push first?**" given a fixed shard pool.

### Rule 1: Match Your Primary Squad

Always lead with your primary squad's main carry's EW:

Primary SquadFirst EW Priority

Tank squadKimberly's EW (her squad's carry — also unlocks her Awakening prerequisite for S6)

Aircraft squadDVA's Energy Master (carry + unlocks her Awakening)

Missile squadTesla's Inductive Current (carry + unlocks his Awakening)

### Rule 2: Tank/Defensive EW Second

After your DPS carry's EW:

* **Murphy's Mitigation Master** is the second-priority EW for almost everyone — enables the Murphy Exception in Aircraft squads, dramatic durability boost
* **Williams's EW** if running Tank into long defensive fights
* **Adam's EW** if running Missile and you defend a lot

### Rule 3: Support EWs Last

Support and utility hero EWs (Schuyler, Marshall, Morrison, Swift, Sarah) are real value but **not as kit-defining as carry/tank EWs**. Push these only after your carry and tank EWs are at Level 20.

### Rule 4: Don't Spread Below Level 20

A common mistake is putting 800 shards into one EW (just past Level 10) and 800 into another. **Better**: 1,130 into one EW to reach Level 20, then start the next. The Level 10 milestone is real but the Level 20 milestone is dramatically more impactful.

## Hero-Specific EW Notes

### Murphy: Mitigation Master

* Enables the **Murphy Exception** — Murphy can replace Carlie in Aircraft squads at high investment
* Drops mono-type bonus from 20% to 15%, but Murphy's mitigation more than compensates
* High value across all three squad compositions when defending or rally-leading
* Required at Level 20+ if you want to use the Murphy Exception in Aircraft squads, which the weapon makes possible from Season 2 onward

### DVA: Energy Master

* Transforms DVA from "strong burst" to "strongest single-target burst in the game"
* Adds **Vortex Overload** — damage booster that stacks when multiple Aircraft heroes are on the team
* The defining EW of the Aircraft meta from Season 1 onward
* Required at Level 20+ to Awaken DVA (Week 4 of Season 6)

### Tesla: Inductive Current

* Adds the Inductive stack mechanic to Tesla's energy attacks
* Scales his AoE damage significantly
* Pairs with Tesla's existing Electric Grid Lockdown for back-row deletion
* Required at Level 20+ to Awaken Tesla (Week 7 of Season 6)

### Kimberly's EW

* Adds the **Energy Amplification** mechanic that her Awakening Skill scales off
* One of the foundational EWs for any Tank squad
* Required at Level 20+ to Awaken Kimberly (Week 1 of Season 6)

### Other notable EWs

* **Carlie**: front-line Aircraft tank, debuff mechanics
* **Lucius**: Aircraft defensive support, EW released Week 3 of Season 4
* **Adam**: Missile tank with counter-attack scaling
* **Williams**: Tank defensive anchor, EW from Season 4
* **Schuyler**: Aircraft crowd control, EW from Season 3 Battle Pass
* **Morrison**: Aircraft defence-shred support
* **Fiona**: Missile AoE DPS, EW from Season 5
* **Stetmann**: Tank secondary DPS, EW from Season 5

The full EW roster as of Season 5 is: Kimberly, DVA, Tesla, Murphy, Carlie, Swift, Marshall, Adam, Lucius, Stetmann, Williams, Schuyler, McGregor, Morrison, Fiona — **15 heroes** with EWs, all of which still apply going into Season 6.

## What If You Skipped a Season's EW?

If you missed a Battle Pass cycle and don't have one of the seasonal EWs:

1. **Black Market shard boxes between seasons** — your fastest free recovery route
2. **Hot Deal pack purchases targeting that hero** — when the right pack comes around
3. **Universal EW shards** (any source) can be applied to any unlocked weapon at any level — useful for late-game catch-up

You will be **at least one season behind** Battle Pass buyers, but you will get there eventually.

## Common Mistakes

* **Pushing a single EW to Level 30 before others reach Level 20** — diminishing returns vs your second hero's first 20 levels. Spread to Level 20 first
* **Spending shards on support hero EWs before your carry's** — your carry hero is the squad's win condition. The support EW is wasted if your carry isn't already at Level 20
* **Forgetting EW Lv20 is the Awakening prerequisite** — if you plan to Awaken Kimberly/DVA/Tesla in Season 6, your shard plan must include their EW at Level 20+ first
* **Buying every Battle Pass without focus** — a Battle Pass is roughly $20. Buying all three per season is $60 / season. F2P route via Black Market reaches the same destination eventually
* **Saving Universal shards "for the perfect EW"** — Universal shards are valuable but they don't appreciate over time. Use them when they're useful, don't hoard them indefinitely
* **Ignoring older-season EWs** — heroes from Season 2-4 EWs still matter into Season 6. They are not obsolete. Don't skip past them just because newer EWs exist
