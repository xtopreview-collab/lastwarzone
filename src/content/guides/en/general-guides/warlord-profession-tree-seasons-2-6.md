# War Leader (Warlord) Profession Tree — Seasons 2 to 6 (Lv 40→100 + Every Seasonal Column)

Profession · 2026-08-11

## What This Guide Covers

War Leader = the war-phase pick — not for early season

War Leader is for the **war phase**: once your base is built and your alliance is actually fighting (Desert Storm, Capitol Conquest, SvS, Faction Duel). **If a new season just started and you're still building, switch back to [Engineer](/guides/general/engineer-profession-tree-seasons-2-6/) for now** and come back here when the wars begin — a War Leader with nothing to fight is just a slower-building Engineer. Switching costs a scarce Profession Change Certificate and resets your points, so budget one clean switch per season. Full stage-by-stage decision table in the [Profession System Guide](/guides/general/profession-system-guide/).

Season 1 has its own tier-by-tier walkthrough — [Warlord Profession Tree (Season 1)](/guides/season-1/warlord-profession-tree-guide/). This guide covers **everything from Season 2 onward** for the **War Leader** profession (the in-game name for what the community calls "Warlord").

The core tree is the same every season — only the cap and the right column change

Your **permanent War Leader skills (left + middle columns) do not change from season to season.** What changes is (1) the **Profession Level cap**, which rises and unlocks **more tiers of the same core tree**, and (2) the **right-column seasonal skills**, swapped out completely every season and **refunded when the season ends**. There is no separate "Season 4 core build" — one War Leader core tree that reaches higher, plus a new seasonal column each season. The seasonal column is **identical to the Engineer's** — the professions differ only in the permanent core.

Confidence note

The **Lv 1–70 skill names, tiers and max levels are captured in-game and verified** (see the [S2 allocation page](/guides/general/season-2-profession-point-allocation/)); the **Lv 75–100 tiers are projected and flagged as such below**. Exact effect percentages, the per-season point budget and the precise spend order are recommendations — confirm the live numbers and your current cap in your own client. (The real garrison/reinforce skills are **Tactical Garrison, Garrison Boost, Garrison Haste, Rally Guard** and **Hold the Base** — not "Tactical Reinforce" or "Reinforce Strengthen", which are not real skills.)

## How to Spend Your Points (Every Season)

The order is the **same every season** — only how far you get down it depends on your cap and points:

1. **Seasonal EXP-snowball first (right column).** Max the recurring EXP skills before anything else — they multiply your point income. These four are in *every* season's column: **Combat Experience, Building Inspiration I, Double Exchange, Professional Insights** (see the note under the seasonal section below).
2. **The permanent War Leader core.** Max the combat package first: **Team Strike** (rally damage), the garrison wall — **Tactical Garrison / Garrison Boost / Garrison Haste (Lv 25–35)** — and capacity/sustain — **Hospital Prep / Intensive Training / Warfare Supplies** (all reached by Lv 40, covered in the S1 guide).
3. **The higher core tiers (Lv 45–70), as your cap allows.** Best value first: **Hold the Base + Rally Guard (Lv 45, max 5 each)** — the defensive payoff, take them the moment S2 opens Lv 45; then the offensive core **Base Strike (Lv 50)**, **Combat Raider (Lv 65)** and **Assault Squad (Lv 70)**; keep the army big through a long war with **Efficient Training (Lv 60)**. *(Season 3 adds Lv 75–100 tiers — not yet in-game verified, see the note above.)*
4. **This season's signature seasonal skills** (the themed right-column picks — listed per season below).
5. **Skip / defer, then reset at cap.** Deprioritise the mine node (**Destruction Mine**) unless you're on the offensive, and skip the low-value fillers (**Disguise, Decay Ray, Charge Banner, Banner Upgrade**). Once you're *at* the cap, the EXP-snowball skills have done their job — **reset** them (Skill Reset Book / season-end refund) into permanent combat value.

## The Exact Point-Spend — Every Level (Lv 1 → 100)

You earn **about one skill point per Profession Level** (level-up milestones add a few extra — Season 1 totals **45 points** at the Lv 40 cap, and later seasons scale up similarly). These tables show which skill to put each level's point into, to the Lv 100 cap, with **cap lines** marking where each season stops; the handful of bonus points let you finish the last skill in each band.

Best practice: level with seasonal, finish without it

The right-column seasonal points are **refunded at season end**, so they are **not part of your permanent build** — they're a temporary levelling booster. So there are two tables, and you use **both, in sequence**:

* **While levelling → Version B** (seasonal EXP skills first) — the fastest way to the cap.
* **At max level → Version A** (core only) — once seasonal refunds, this is your **permanent end-state build**. For War Leader that is a **PvP / combat build**: rally damage (Team Strike), the garrison wall (Tactical Garrison, Garrison Boost, Hold the Base, Rally Guard) and attack/rally (Base Strike, Combat Raider, Assault Squad), with capacity skills to keep your army big through a war.

## The Seasonal Right Column — Season by Season

The right column is **shared with Engineer**, **different every season**, and **fully refunded at season end** — spend it boldly. In *every* season the first four picks are the same **EXP-snowball skills**:

Always spend these seasonal points first (every season)

**Combat Experience** (more Profession EXP from World-Map monster kills), **Building Inspiration I** (more EXP from upgrading season buildings), **Double Exchange** (1:2 season-resource conversion) and **Professional Insights** (an instant chunk of your current level's EXP). Max each as it unlocks so the rest of the season's points — and the higher core tiers — arrive faster.

The seasonal column is the **same set the Engineer sees** (see the [Engineer S2–S6 guide](/guides/general/engineer-profession-tree-seasons-2-6/) for the full per-season descriptions). The short version for a fighter:

Season (theme)Seasonal picks a War Leader cares about

S2 — Polar World (frost)EXP snowball → **Frost Mine / Frostbomb / Firebomb** for map control on the ice battlefield

S3 — Golden Realm (desert)EXP snowball → **Sand's Protection** (defenders) + **Fragile / Shrinking Spell** (weaken enemy bases) + **Protector's Oath**

S4 — Blood Night (Oni)EXP snowball → **Hunting Inspiration** + the **Blood Night Hunter** line for the event fights

S5 — Wild WestEXP snowball → **Gunslinger Wager** (High Noon duels) + the **Fortune Train** trio; economy otherwise

S6 — Rainforest (territory)EXP snowball → **Homeland Attack + Defense** or **Invasion Attack + Defense** (match where your base sits) — the column's direct combat buffs

## When to Be War Leader (Recap)

The meta doesn't change season to season: play **Engineer early** to rush your seasonal buildings, then spend a **Profession Change Certificate** to switch to **War Leader** for the mid/late-season wars. The difference from S2 onward is that the payoff tiers (Hold the Base, Rally Guard at Lv 45; Base Strike at Lv 50) are higher up the tree — so the switch is most worthwhile once your profession level is high enough to actually reach them (Lv 45+ in S2). Season 3 adds more payoff tiers higher still (Lv 75–100), projected until confirmed in-game.
