# Rampage Bosses Guide

Events · 2026-03-22

## What Are Rampage Bosses?

Rampage Bosses are powerful zombie bosses that periodically awaken from tombstones around your Capitol. Three bosses spawn during each event, each with different strengths and elemental weaknesses. Every commander on the server attacks them individually — this is a **solo attack event**, not a rally. The entire server's combined damage determines whether each boss is defeated.

Solo Attacks, Server-Wide Effort

**You attack Rampage Bosses individually with your own squad — there are no rallies.** However, each boss has a massive health pool that requires the collective damage of all commanders on the server to deplete. Your individual damage contribution earns you personal rewards and ranking position, while the server works together to bring each boss down.

## Schedule

* The Rampage Boss event runs **every 2 weeks on Sunday**
* **3 bosses awaken** throughout the day at **00:00, 06:00, and 12:00 server time**
* Each boss remains active until defeated by the server or until **24:00 server time**, when any surviving bosses escape
* Plan your attacks around these spawn times so you do not miss any bosses

## How It Works

### Attacking

* Commanders with **Base Level 8 or higher** can attack Rampage Bosses
* You get a **maximum of 3 attacks per boss** — use them wisely
* Each boss has a **type weakness** — attacking with the correct troop type (tanks, aircraft, or missiles) deals significantly more damage
* Your damage is tracked individually for ranking rewards

### Defeating a Boss

* When a Rampage Boss loses **100% of its HP** from the server's combined attacks, it becomes **Vulnerable**

### Boss HP by level

Three bosses spawn at each level and they are not identical — the spread is widest early and narrows as levels climb. Verified in-game:

Pick the weak one early; it stops mattering later

At low levels the three bosses are genuinely different targets — at level 7 the strongest has **nearly double** the HP of the weakest (1.1T against 600B), and at level 5 the gap is 78%. If your server is struggling to bring one down, the choice of which to focus is worth real time.

From level 9 upward that stops being true: the three converge steadily - about 11% apart at level 10, 8% at level 12, and roughly 5% by level 15, and the only thing that matters is total server damage. The curve itself is brutal — a level 15 boss has **over a hundred times** the HP of a level 1.

- A Vulnerable boss can no longer be attacked normally — instead, use the **Eliminate** command to finish it off for substantial bonus rewards
- If all 3 bosses are successfully killed during the event, **stronger bosses with better rewards** will appear at the next event

### Boss Weaknesses

Each of the 3 bosses is weak to a different troop type. Check the boss info screen before attacking and send the correct counter squad.

Boss WeaknessWhat to Send

Weak to TanksSend your strongest tank-type squad with tank heroes

Weak to AircraftSend your strongest aircraft-type squad with aircraft heroes

Weak to MissilesSend your strongest missile-type squad with missile heroes

Match the Weakness

**Hitting a boss's weakness is the single biggest damage multiplier you have.** A moderately strong squad of the correct type will outdamage a much stronger squad of the wrong type. Before each attack, check which type the boss is weak to and adjust your formation accordingly.

## Attack Strategy

### Maximising Your Damage

Since you only get 3 attacks per boss, every hit needs to count:

1. **Match the boss weakness** — always send the troop type the boss is weak to
2. **Use your strongest heroes for that type** — if the boss is weak to aircraft, load your best aircraft heroes
3. **Equip attack-oriented gear** — swap to offensive gear before attacking. Defence matters less since this is a damage race
4. **Activate buff items** — attack potions, War Fever, and critical hit buffs all apply to your attacks
5. **Invest in the right squad before the event** — knowing the event is coming, level and gear your heroes for each troop type

### Formation Recommendations

Build a strong squad for each troop type so you can counter any boss weakness. Below are the recommended layouts — refer to the full squad guides for detailed hero explanations and investment priorities.

#### Boss Weak to Tanks — Send Tank Squad

Recommended Tank Formation

Front Row — Damage Sponges

Left

Murphy

Main Tank

Absorbs damage, and his damage reduction is what keeps the front line standing. Note the scope: both of his defensive skills cover the **front row** until 4 stars, where his Tactics extends the physical damage reduction to the whole squad. Left position so he draws the bulk of incoming fire.

Right

Williams

Defensive Anchor

Damage reduction on himself, plus a Defence buff on two front-row heroes (squad-wide only from 3 stars). The best pure tank in the game. Right position to split incoming damage with Murphy — two front-row bodies means neither falls too quickly.

Back Row — Damage Dealers

Left

Kimberly

Main DPS

Highest AoE damage in early game. Your most important hero. Left position directly behind Murphy. Note that his damage reduction covers the *front row*, so it does not reach her until his 4-star bonus extends it to the squad.

Centre

Stetmann

Secondary DPS

AoE energy damage. Effective against grouped enemies. Centre position gives him coverage from both front-row tanks.

Right

Marshall

Support

Critical rate buffs that boost the entire squad's damage output. Right position behind Williams, whose Defense buff keeps Marshall alive to maintain his buffs.

Adapt to the Boss

**Do not use the same squad for every boss.** Swap your entire formation to match each boss's weakness. A moderately strong squad of the correct type will outdamage a much stronger squad of the wrong type. Before each attack, check which type the boss is weak to and load the matching layout above.

## Rewards

Rampage Boss rewards come from multiple sources — individual damage, the Eliminate command, and server-wide milestones.

Reward TypeDetails

Damage rewardsAll commanders who deal damage to a boss receive rewards by mail, scaling with the amount of damage dealt

Eliminate rewardsUsing the Eliminate command on a Vulnerable boss grants substantial bonus rewards

Ranking rewardsTop 100 commanders by total damage dealt across all bosses receive additional ranking rewards

Escalation bonusIf all 3 bosses are killed, the next event spawns stronger bosses with better reward pools

Do Not Miss the Eliminate

**When a boss becomes Vulnerable, use the Eliminate command immediately.** The Eliminate rewards are some of the best in the event. Keep an eye on boss HP — when it is close to zero, stay online so you can Eliminate as soon as it becomes available.

## Maximising Event Efficiency

### Use All 9 Attacks

You get 3 attacks per boss and there are 3 bosses — that is 9 total attacks per event. Do not waste any:

* Set alarms for the spawn times (00:00, 06:00, 12:00 server time) so you do not miss a boss
* Attack as soon as a boss spawns to maximise your contribution before others deplete its HP
* If you cannot be online for a spawn, prioritise the spawn windows where you can use all 3 attacks

### Coordinate with Your Alliance

While this is not a rally event, alliance coordination still helps:

* Share boss weakness information in alliance chat so members know which squads to prepare
* Remind members of spawn times, especially the 00:00 and 06:00 spawns that are easy to miss
* Stronger alliances help the server kill all 3 bosses, unlocking harder bosses with better rewards next time

### Prepare for Each Troop Type

Since each boss has a different weakness, you need viable squads for tanks, aircraft, and missiles. Before the event:

* Level and gear heroes across all three troop types
* Have gear sets ready to swap for each type
* Stock up on attack buff items

## Common Mistakes

1. **Ignoring boss weaknesses** — sending the wrong troop type dramatically reduces your damage. Always check the weakness first
2. **Missing spawn windows** — bosses spawn at 00:00, 06:00, and 12:00 server time. Set reminders or you will waste attacks
3. **Not using all 3 attacks** — every unused attack is wasted damage and lost rewards
4. **Forgetting buff items** — attack potions and War Fever significantly boost your damage output. Use them before every attack
5. **Neglecting weaker troop types** — if all your investment is in aircraft but the boss is weak to tanks, your damage suffers. Build across all types
6. **Missing the Eliminate** — when a boss becomes Vulnerable, Eliminate it immediately for the best rewards
7. **Skipping the event** — even moderate damage earns rewards by mail. Any participation is better than none
