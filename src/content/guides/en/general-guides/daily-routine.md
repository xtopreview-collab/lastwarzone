# Daily Routine

Beginner · 2026-03-11

## Why a Routine Matters

Players who log in daily and complete key tasks will always outpace those who play in bursts. Last War rewards consistency — most timers, events, and resets are designed around daily play.

## Morning Check-In (5 Minutes)

1. **Collect idle rewards** — hero EXP and other passive rewards cap out. Collect regularly
2. **Start a build or upgrade** — never let your builder sit idle
3. **Queue research** — same principle, always have something researching
4. **Queue troop training** — keep training running
5. **Claim daily login rewards**
6. **Tap alliance help** — help every alliance member who has a pending request

## Midday Session (10 Minutes)

1. **Check for active events** — participate in anything running (Arms Race, alliance events)
2. **Complete daily missions** — most are simple tasks you are doing anyway
3. **Upgrade your drone** — if you have parts and resources available
4. **Check the Honor Shop** — buy Legendary Gear Blueprints if available
5. **Send gathering marches** — if resource tiles are available near your alliance hive

## Evening Session (15-20 Minutes)

This is typically when alliance events run and when you have more time.

1. **Participate in alliance events** — boss fights, Arms Race activities, etc.
2. **Progress campaign stages** — push for hero EXP and materials
3. **Hunt zombies** — resources and drone parts
4. **Coordinate with alliance** — rallies, defence, or planned attacks
5. **Spend excess resources** — queue upgrades, training, or research before logging off
6. **Shield up** — if you have resources and will be offline overnight

## Weekly Planning

### Arms Race Days

Plan around the Arms Race schedule:

* **Building Day** — save your biggest building upgrades for this day
* **Training Day** — stockpile training speedups and queue everything on this day
* **Research Day** — start your most expensive research projects
* **Power Day** — use everything you have been saving

### Weekly Tasks

* **Alliance research donations** — contribute daily
* **Review your squad** — are you investing in the right heroes? Is your gear on the right people?
* **Check hero shards** — can you star up any heroes?
* **Review alliance mail** — stay informed on alliance plans and strategy

## What NOT to Do

* **Do not spend gems on short speedups** — a 5-minute speedup is not worth gems. Save for VIP
* **Do not leave resources unprotected overnight** — spend them or shield
* **Do not skip alliance help** — it is free and costs nothing
* **Do not ignore events** — even partial participation gives rewards that add up
* **Do not forget to collect idle rewards** — capped rewards are wasted progress
* **Do not open resource chests unless you are about to spend** — keep them safe in your inventory
