# Season 5 Timeline & Week-by-Week Roadmap

Season Overview · 2026-04-25

## How Season 5 Plays Out

Season 5 is **economy season**. Bank Strongholds turn CrystalGold into compound interest. The Trade Train starts in Week 2 and feeds the banking economy with Whiskey. The Caffeine Institute replaces Furnaces and Lighthouses with temporary buff coffee. City captures only happen on **Wednesdays and Saturdays**. Sundays are **Truce Day**. The financial discipline matters as much as the military strategy. Season 5 runs roughly **8-10 weeks**.

The Whiskey-to-Wealth Pipeline

**Trade Train → Whiskey → CrystalGold → Bank Strongholds → Compound Interest → Final Ranking.** Every link feeds the next. Players who run efficient trade routes, protect deliveries, and reinvest interest pull ahead exponentially. Players who treat S5 like S4 — just capturing territory — fall behind regardless of military strength.
