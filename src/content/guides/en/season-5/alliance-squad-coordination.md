# Alliance Squad Coordination (Season 5)

Alliance · 2026-03-24

## Why Coordination Is Everything in Season 5

Season 5 demands more alliance coordination than any previous season. Bank Strongholds require daily PvP defence. City captures happen only on Wednesdays and Saturdays, meaning concentrated effort on those days. The Trade Train needs escorts and raiders. Sundays are Truce Day for planning. And the CrystalGold economy means every decision has compound consequences.

Season 5 Coordination Reality

**An uncoordinated alliance will lose its banks, fail city captures, and fall hopelessly behind in CrystalGold rankings.** The compound interest from Bank Strongholds means that every day you fail to defend a bank or miss a deposit is a loss that compounds over the entire season. Season 5 is won by alliances that coordinate daily, not just during events.
