# Missile Vehicle Squad Guide (Season 5)

Heroes & Squads · 2026-03-24

## Missiles in Season 5: The Revolution

Season 5 is the season where Missile squads stop being the counter-pick and become a primary squad. Two massive changes land simultaneously: **Fiona's Exclusive Weapon** transforms her into the strongest Missile carry in the game, and **Venom's UR promotion** adds a powerful new hero to the roster. For the first time in the game's history, Missiles are genuinely competitive with Aircraft as a primary squad.

This Is Your Season

**If you invested in Fiona shards since Season 1 (as recommended in previous guides), Season 5 is your payoff.** Fiona's Exclusive Weapon makes her Radiation DoT the most devastating sustained damage mechanic in the game. Combined with Venom UR and T11 troops, Missile squads can now go toe-to-toe with Aircraft in open PvP. The combat triangle is more balanced than ever.
