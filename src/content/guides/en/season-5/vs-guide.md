# Alliance Duel VS (Season 5)

Alliance · 2026-03-24

## VS in Season 5

Alliance Duel VS follows the same 6-day structure, but Season 5 creates the hardest resource trade-offs VS has ever demanded. You are now splitting resources between **Bank deposits** (compound interest), **Trade Train investment** (Whiskey conversion), **VS saving** (Day 5 speedups and Day 4 hero items), and your general progression. The CrystalGold economy adds a financial dimension to every VS decision.

For the full day-by-day mechanics, see the [Alliance Duel VS Guide](/guides/general/vs-guide/).

The Season 5 VS Dilemma

**Every resource you save for VS is a resource not earning compound interest in your banks.** Speedups saved for Friday cannot be used to accelerate Trade Train deliveries. Hero items saved for Thursday cannot boost your Exclusive Weapons mid-week. Season 5 forces you to make trade-offs that previous seasons never demanded. The answer is not to skip VS — the rewards are too good. The answer is to be more disciplined about exactly what you save and what you spend.
