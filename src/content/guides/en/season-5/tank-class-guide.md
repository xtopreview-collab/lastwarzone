# Tank Squad Guide (Season 5)

Heroes & Squads · 2026-03-24

## Tanks in Season 5: Mature and Loaded

Tank squads arrive in Season 5 with the most complete toolkit in the game's history. Stetmann gets his Exclusive Weapon this season, joining Kimberly, Murphy, and Marshall who already have theirs. Scarlett has been UR since Season 3. T11 troops are standard from Season 4. The result is a squad with multiple viable formations and weapon options for every situation.

Season 5 Reality

**Tanks are no longer the dominant squad, but they are the most versatile.** With four Exclusive Weapons, Scarlett UR, and T11 troops, Tank squads excel at Bank Stronghold defence, rally leadership, garrison duty, and counter-picking Missile opponents. The Wild West economy rewards the durability Tanks provide — you need to defend your banks every day except Sunday.
