# Hero Guide (Season 5)

Heroes & Squads · 2026-03-24

## What Changes in Season 5

Season 5 brings the most hero changes since Season 1. Four major updates land simultaneously:

1. **Venom UR Promotion** — a new Missile hero enters the competitive roster
2. **Fiona's Exclusive Weapon** — transforms her into the strongest Missile carry in the game
3. **Stetmann's Exclusive Weapon** — brings him back into contention for Tank squads
4. **Morrison's Exclusive Weapon** — amplifies his defence shred, making the DVA-Morrison pipeline even deadlier

For the core hero mechanics (rarities, skills, star upgrades, Tactics cards), see the [Pre-Season 1 Hero Guide](/guides/pre-season-1/hero-guide/). This guide focuses on what changes in Season 5.

The Season of Missiles

**Two of the four hero changes directly benefit Missile squads.** Fiona's Exclusive Weapon and Venom UR make Missiles genuinely competitive with Aircraft for the first time. Players who invested in Missile heroes early are rewarded massively this season. The tier list shifts accordingly — Fiona rises to the top of S-tier for Missile players.
