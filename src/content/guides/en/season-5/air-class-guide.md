# Aircraft Squad Guide (Season 5)

Heroes & Squads · 2026-03-24

## Aircraft in Season 5: Defence Shred Evolved

Aircraft squads have dominated the meta since Season 1 and remain the strongest PvP squad in Season 5. Morrison's Exclusive Weapon this season pushes his defence shred capability even further, making DVA's burst damage even more devastating. Combined with T11 troops, the Murphy Exception, and Caffeine buffs, Aircraft squads are at their peak.

Season 5 Aircraft

**Aircraft remains the PvP king, but the gap is closing.** Fiona's Exclusive Weapon and Venom's UR promotion make Missile squads genuinely competitive for the first time. Aircraft players must now respect Missile opponents more than ever. The combat triangle has never been more balanced, which means type-matching is more critical than ever for Bank raids and city captures.
