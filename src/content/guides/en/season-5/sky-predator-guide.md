# Sky Predator (Season 5)

Alliance · 2026-03-24

## Sky Battlefront in Season 5

Season 5 Sky Battlefront benefits from the full Exclusive Weapon lineup, T11 troops, and Caffeine buffs that can be timed for maximum rally damage. Your alliance has never been stronger for airship battles. The combination of four new weapons plus all previous weapons means your rally compositions hit harder than any previous season.

For the full event mechanics, see the [Sky Predator Guide](/guides/general/sky-predator-guide/).

Season 5 Sky Power

**If your alliance has been timing Caffeine buffs for Bank raids, apply the same discipline to Sky Predator.** Brew Skill Damage coffee before rallies and ensure your class-specific Bar is upgraded. The stat difference between coffee-buffed and unbuffed rallies is the difference between clean shield-breaks and failed attempts. Season 5 gives you the tools to dominate Sky Battlefront — use them.
