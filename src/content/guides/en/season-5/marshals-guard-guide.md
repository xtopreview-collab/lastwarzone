# Marshal's Guard (Season 5)

Alliance · 2026-03-24

## Marshal's Guard in Season 5

Marshal's Guard in Season 5 features the highest damage output in the game's history. T11 troops, four new Exclusive Weapons on top of all previous ones, and Caffeine buffs from the [Caffeine Institute](/guides/season-5/caffeine-guide/) combine to create rallies that would have been unimaginable in pre-season. If your alliance is not pushing the highest available difficulty, you are leaving rewards on the table.

For the full event mechanics, see the [Marshal's Guard Guide](/guides/general/marshals-guard-guide/).

Push Maximum Difficulty

**If your alliance was clearing Level 11-12 in Season 4, you should be targeting Level 13-15+ in Season 5.** Four new Exclusive Weapons (Fiona, Morrison, Stetmann, Venom UR), T11 troops, and Caffeine buffs make your rallies dramatically stronger. Every difficulty level increase improves rewards.
