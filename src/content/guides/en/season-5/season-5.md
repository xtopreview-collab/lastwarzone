# F2P Season 5 Guide — Free CrystalGold and Banking Without Getting Robbed

Free 2 Play · 2026-06-21

## What Changes for F2P in Season 5

Season 5 (Wild West) drops you into the Golden Wasteland with a frontier economy built around **CrystalGold**, **Bank Strongholds**, and the **Caffeine Institute**. The single most important fact for a free-to-play player is this: **everything seasonal here is temporary.** The Coffee Factories, the Bars, the seasonal resources — they all vanish when the season ends. That makes spending real money to rush them a straight waste, and it makes free farming the only sensible approach.

Never Pay for Temporary Buildings

**Every seasonal building and resource in Season 5 is removed at season end.** Coffee Factories, Bars, CrystalGold, Coffee, Whiskey — none of it carries over. So build it all with farmed resources, never with money. A free player who farms steadily ends the season in the same seasonal position as someone who paid to rush — the spending simply evaporates.
