## 1. What Is Last War?
Last War: Survival Game is a base-building strategy game where you grow your Headquarters, train heroes, and team up with alliances. It’s a marathon, not a sprint—your goal is to survive, grow, and conquer the server with your team!

## 2. Your First Hour
- **Account Setup:** The very first thing you need to do? Change your name! Don't stay "Commander123456" or alliances will think you're inactive.
- **Server Selection:** If you have friends playing, make sure you join their server immediately, as transferring later can be difficult.
- **Early HQ Upgrades:** Just follow the tutorial and keep building. Your Builders should never be resting.

## 3. Understanding the Basics
- **Resources:**
  - **Iron & Oil:** Basic building materials.
  - **Coins:** Used for research and higher-level upgrades.
  - **Gems:** Premium currency. (Don't waste these on speeding up 5-minute timers!)
  - **Gold Bricks:** The real bottleneck later in the game. Mine it whenever you can!
- **Headquarters (HQ):** Your HQ is your most important building. The higher the HQ, the higher the max level of everything else.
- **Tech Centre:** Where you research buffs for construction, economy, and military.

## 4. Your First Squad
Why go Mono-type Tank? Tank heroes are the easiest to get and upgrade early on. A full team of Tanks gives you massive synergy bonuses.
> [!TIP]
> Put two defenders in the front row, and three damage dealers/supports in the back!

**Great Early Heroes:**
- **Murphy (UR Defender):** Excellent front-line tank. 
- **Kimberly (UR Attacker):** Incredible damage dealer.

## 5. The 10 Biggest Mistakes New Players Make
1. **"Even" Upgrading:** Upgrading every building evenly. (Just rush the ones required for HQ!).
2. **Ignoring the Tech Centre:** Research is permanent power.
3. **Playing Solo:** Not joining an active top-7 alliance. You miss out on huge buffs and gifts!
4. **Wasting Speedups:** Using speedups randomly instead of during Arms Race or Duel VS events.
5. **Splitting Resources:** Leveling up 15 different heroes. Focus everything on ONE main squad of 5 heroes!
6. **Opening Resource Boxes Early:** Keep them in your inventory until you need them, so they can't be stolen.
7. **Ignoring the Drone:** Your drone provides massive power boosts. Upgrade its chips!
8. **Staying "Commander123456":** Top alliances will reject you.
9. **Wasting Stamina:** Not hitting the right targets on the map.
10. **Panic Spending:** Using universal shards on the wrong heroes.

## 6. Day-by-Day Roadmap (Days 1-14)
- **Days 1-3:** Rush HQ levels. Keep builders and tech center busy 24/7. Unlock your second builder queue.
- **Days 4-7:** Join a top alliance. Start focusing heavily on your main 5-hero Tank squad. Participate in early server events.
- **Days 8-14:** Start syncing your upgrades and speedups with daily events (like Arms Race). Get your drone upgrades rolling.

## 7. F2P Tips — Getting the Most Without Spending
- **Free Dailies:** Always check the shop for free daily claims.
- **VIP Level:** Use gems to get VIP levels for quality of life (like Auto-Battle).
- **Stamina:** Never let your stamina hit the cap! Always be killing zombies.
- **Events:** The best way to get ahead is to score high in events. Hoard resources and spend them when the event gives you points for it.

## 8. When to Start Spending (If You Want To)
- **Best Value:** The second building queue, Kimberly (if available in an early pack), and monthly passes.
- **Worst Value:** Raw resource packs or speedups.
