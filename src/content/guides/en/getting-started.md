# Getting Started — The Complete Beginner's Guide to Last War

> **Key Takeaways:** Rush your Headquarters above everything else. Build one mono-type squad — not three mediocre ones. Save your gems for VIP 6. Never use speedups outside events. Join an active alliance on Day 1. Read the full [Hero Tier List](/en/guides/hero-tier-list/) and [Combat Mechanics guide](/en/guides/combat-mechanics/) after this.

## What Is Last War?

Last War: Survival Game is a base-building, hero-collecting strategy game with real-time PvP, alliance wars, and seasonal competitive events. With over 114 million downloads and 3.8 million monthly active users, it's one of the biggest mobile strategy games in the world.

This guide covers everything you need to dominate your first two weeks — without wasting a single resource.

## Your First Hour — Account Setup

The decisions you make in your first 60 minutes matter more than you think.

### Server Selection

Pick a **newer server** if possible. Older servers have established players with months of head start — you'll be cannon fodder. Look for servers that launched within the last 7-14 days for the most balanced experience.

### Name Your Commander

This sounds trivial, but **change your default name immediately.** The default "Commander123456" format signals to alliance leaders that you're inactive or a bot. Top alliances routinely reject players with default names. Pick something memorable.

### Tutorial Walkthrough

The tutorial walks you through basic building and your first battle. Don't skip it — it gives free resources and speedups. But after the tutorial ends, resist the urge to upgrade everything you see. Follow the HQ Rush strategy below instead.

## Understanding Resources

Last War has six main currencies. Learning how each one works — and when NOT to spend them — is the single most important skill for new players.

| Resource | Icon | What It's For | Key Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Oil** | 🛢️ | Building upgrades, troop training | Produce constantly; protect with shield |
| **Iron** | ⚙️ | Research, gear crafting | Keep Tech Centre always researching |
| **Coins** | 🪙 | Hero leveling, skill upgrades | Farm via [Coin Farming Guide](/en/guides/coin-farming/) |
| **Gems (Diamonds)** | 💎 | Premium purchases, VIP | **Save for VIP 6** — details in [Gem Economy](/en/guides/gem-economy/) |
| **Gold Bricks** | 🧱 | Premium shop, exclusive items | Worth real money (~100 bricks ≈ $1 USD) |
| **Speedups** | ⏩ | Reduce upgrade/research timers | **NEVER use outside events** |

### The Golden Rule: Keep Resource Chests Unopened

Your inventory is **safe from plunder.** If you open a resource chest and dump oil into your reserves, enemy players can raid your base and steal up to 30% of it. Keep chests sealed until you're ready to spend them immediately on an upgrade.

## The HQ Rush Strategy

Your Headquarters is the master gate for **everything** in Last War. Hero level caps, troop tiers, building unlocks, research branches — all gated behind HQ level.

### The Rule Is Simple

> 💡 **When your main builder is free, upgrade HQ.** Everything else is secondary.

The buildings you "need" to upgrade before each HQ level are listed in the upgrade requirements panel. Focus ONLY on those prerequisite buildings, then immediately push HQ again.

### Why This Works

- Higher HQ → higher hero level cap → exponentially more damage
- Higher HQ → better troop tiers → stronger in PvP and events
- Higher HQ → more buildings → more production

### The Second Builder

Getting **VIP 6** unlocks a permanent second construction queue. This is the most impactful purchase in the entire game for progression speed. Save your gems for this.

## Building Your First Squad

This is where 90% of new players mess up. They recruit 15 heroes and spread resources across all of them. **Don't do this.**

### The Mono-Squad Rule

Running 5 heroes of the **same troop type** gives a massive +20% bonus to HP, Attack, and Defense. That's roughly +44% total combat effectiveness compared to a mixed squad.

| Composition | Bonus |
|---|---|
| 5 same type (mono) | **+20% HP/ATK/DEF** |
| 4 same type | +15% |
| 3 same + 2 same | +10% |
| 3 same type | +5% |
| Mixed rainbow | +0% |

### Recommended Starter Squad: Tank

For beginners, **Tank** is the easiest and most forgiving type to build. Here's your ideal first lineup:

| Position | Hero | Role | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Front-Left (P1) | ![Murphy](/images/heroes/murphy-portrait.webp) **Murphy** | Main Tank | Absorbs 60% of incoming damage, squad-wide physical DR |
| Front-Right (P2) | ![Carlie](/images/heroes/carlie-portrait.webp) **Carlie** | Off-Tank | Healing + shield for survivability |
| Back-Left (P3) | ![Marshall](/images/heroes/marshall-portrait.webp) **Marshall** | Buffer | ATK/Crit buff for entire squad |
| Back-Mid (P4) | ![Kimberly](/images/heroes/kimberly-portrait.webp) **Kimberly** | Main DPS | #1 damage dealer in the game, safest position |
| Back-Right (P5) | ![Lucius](/images/heroes/lucius-portrait.webp) **Lucius** | Support | Energy DR + sustain, protects against burst |

> 💡 **Position 4 (Back-Middle) is targeted LAST** — always put your main damage dealer here. Kimberly in P4 is the standard across every tier list.

## Combat 101 — How Battles Actually Work

You don't need to understand every formula on Day 1. But knowing these three concepts will immediately make you better than 80% of players.

### 1. The Type Triangle

![Type Counter Triangle](/images/combat/combat-troop-type-counter-triangle.png)

- **Aircraft beats Tank** (+20% damage)
- **Tank beats Missile** (+20% damage)
- **Missile beats Aircraft** (+20% damage)

The counter bonus swings fights by ~40% total. This is why mono-squads matter — you know exactly what you're strong and weak against.

### 2. The Damage Formula (Simplified)

$$\text{Damage} = \text{Base ATK} \times \frac{4000}{\text{DEF} + 4000}$$

**What this means:** At 4,000 Defense, you take exactly 50% damage. Every point of Defense makes your hero proportionally tankier, even at high values.

### 3. Formation Positioning

Your front-row heroes absorb the first wave of damage. Position 1 (Front-Left) takes approximately 60% of all opening fire. Your best tank always goes there.

![Formation Positions](/images/ui/ui-lineup-preset-formation.webp)

## Joining an Alliance — Non-Negotiable

Playing Last War solo is like playing chess with half your pieces removed. Alliances provide:

- **Construction help** — alliance members reduce your upgrade timers
- **Tech bonuses** — alliance-wide research buffs that stack
- **Event access** — alliance events give massive rewards
- **Protection** — enemies think twice about hitting alliance members

### How to Pick the Right Alliance

1. **Target the Top 7** alliances on your server
2. **Check Gift Level** — higher = more active members
3. **Look for 90+ members** — dead alliances with 30 members won't help you
4. **Read their rules** — organized alliances with Discord/LINE groups tend to win SvS

> ⚠️ **Don't settle for any alliance.** If you can't get into a Top 7, join the highest-ranked one that accepts you, contribute actively, and transfer up once you've proven yourself.

## Your First 14 Days — Day-by-Day Roadmap

Here's exactly what to focus on each day. Don't stress about perfection — just follow this framework:

| Day | Primary Focus | Target Milestone |
|---|---|---|
| **Day 1** | Complete tutorial, rename, join alliance | HQ 5, alliance joined |
| **Day 2** | HQ rush, start Tech Centre research | HQ 7-8 |
| **Day 3** | Build mono-squad, level main 5 heroes | Squad CP 300K+ |
| **Day 4** | Continue HQ, equip best available gear | HQ 9-10 |
| **Day 5** | Arms Race prep — start hoarding speedups | Resources saved |
| **Day 6-7** | **Arms Race event** — spend speedups now! | Hit event milestones for max rewards |
| **Day 8** | Unlock Gear Factory, craft purple gear | All 5 heroes geared |
| **Day 9-10** | Start Drone upgrades, do radar missions daily | Drone Lv 8-10 |
| **Day 11-12** | Push HQ, upgrade hero skills, alliance tech | HQ 13-15 |
| **Day 13-14** | Prep for first SvS or VS Duel event | Squad CP 800K+ |

### What's "Arms Race"?

Arms Race is a recurring event where every resource you spend (speedups, upgrades, research) earns points toward milestone rewards. **This is why you hoard speedups** — spending them during Arms Race effectively doubles their value since you get the construction/research progress PLUS event rewards.

## F2P Tips — Getting the Most Without Spending

You absolutely can be competitive as a free-to-play player. It just takes patience and efficiency.

### Daily Free Resources Checklist

Do these every single day without exception:

- ✅ Complete all daily tasks
- ✅ Use all stamina on campaign
- ✅ Complete 5 radar missions
- ✅ Collect alliance rewards
- ✅ Check the free daily shop refresh
- ✅ Hit the World Boss (when available)
- ✅ Collect Secret Tasks rewards

### F2P Hero Strategy

Don't chase UR (Ultra Rare) heroes early. Strong SSR heroes like Murphy, Marshall, and Carlie are much easier to upgrade and will carry you through the first few months. Read the [SSR→UR Upgrade Guide](/en/guides/ssr-ur-upgrade/) for when it's worth making the leap.

### Resource Efficiency

- **Stamina:** Never let it cap (wastes regeneration). Use it even if you auto-battle.
- **Coins:** Farm daily via [Coin Farming](/en/guides/coin-farming/) routes
- **Honor Points:** Spend wisely at the [Honor Shop](/en/guides/honor-shop/) — don't buy EW shards here

If you decide to spend money, here's the value ranking:

### Best First Purchase: VIP 6
