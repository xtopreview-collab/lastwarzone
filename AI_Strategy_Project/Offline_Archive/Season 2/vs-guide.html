Alliance Duel VS (Season 2)

Alliance · 2026-03-24

VS in Season 2

Alliance Duel VS follows the same 6-day structure in Season 2. The format has not changed, but the context around it has. Your alliance is managing furnace upgrades, Coal stockpiles, and Rare Soil production simultaneously. Saving resources for VS competes with Season 2's constant resource demands. Day 6 combat features even stronger squads with Carlie's Exclusive Weapon and maxed Season 1 weapons.

For the full day-by-day mechanics, see the Alliance Duel VS Guide.

The Season 2 VS Tension

Season 2 creates a genuine tension between VS preparation and season resource management. Saving speedups for Day 5 is harder when furnace upgrades demand those same speedups. Coal gathering takes march slots. Titanium Alloy production competes for building queues. The discipline to save for VS must coexist with the urgency of furnace progression. Both matter — plan for both.
