Season 2 Engineer & War Leader Skill Tree — Point Allocation

Profession · 2026-08-11

What This Is

A point-by-point allocation reference for Season 2 (level cap Lv 70, roughly 75 skill points — confirm your exact total in-game). Each skill shows the recommended Assign / Max and a Priority. Fill ⭐ Max-first skills before anything else — on their own they already run to about your whole Lv 70 budget; Then is where extra Skill-Point consumables go; Optional is off-role or situational, safe to skip on a focused build.

For which profession to run and when to switch, see the Profession System Guide. Short version:

Which profession, right now?

Season just started → run Engineer (growth phase). Spend one Profession Change Certificate to switch to War Leader when the war phase begins (Desert Storm, Capitol Conquest, SvS, Faction Duel). Switching resets your points, so plan one clean switch per season. The permanent tree is the same every season — only the seasonal column below swaps — so this allocation reuses beyond S2 with a different seasonal set.

Season 2 Seasonal Column (shared — "Polar World")

The right-column skills are identical for Engineer and War Leader and refund at season end. Unlike the permanent trees (free-select, level-gated), the seasonal column is a single linear chain — each skill gates the next, so you cannot skip any of them. You must take Winter Wonderland (even though it's just a cosmetic base skin) to reach Double Exchange and everything after. Because it all refunds at season end, this costs you nothing long-term — just spend down the chain in order, putting real value into the EXP-snowball skills (Combat Experience, Building Inspiration I, Double Exchange, Professional Insights — they multiply your profession EXP) and the frost combat skills, and only the minimum 1 point into the two gate nodes (Winter Wonderland, Gather for Warmth).

It's a chain — you can't skip a node

That's fine, because seasonal points refund at season end. So the only real decision is how many points to sink into each: max the EXP and frost skills, and drop just the 1 required point into Winter Wonderland / Gather for Warmth to pass through.

Point-Spend Order — Tier by Tier

Tiers unlock purely by profession level (two skills every 5 levels), so you spend top-to-bottom as you climb. The permanent order is the same whether or not you have this season's refundable skills — what changes is where your early and "skip"-tier points go. Two routes, same end build:

Two routes — same end build

Route A · No seasonal items (pure permanent). Every point goes into the permanent tree in the order below. On the "— skip —" tiers, hold your points until the next priority tier unlocks — don't sink them into the off-role skills.

Route B · With seasonal items (recommended while levelling). Spend **down the seasonal chain in order** (it's linear — see above), maxing the EXP-snowball skills as they unlock (Combat Experience Lv 1 → Building Inspiration I Lv 10 → Double Exchange Lv 25 → Professional Insights Lv 35) to reach the cap faster, and put every "— skip —" tier point into that chain instead of holding it. It all refunds at season end, so you respec into exactly Route A's build at the cap.

The permanent order below is shared by both routes. "Core" = running total of the permanent priority build — it fills in over the whole climb and lands ≈ 70 of your ~75 Lv 70 budget.

FAQ

Should I run Engineer or War Leader?

Run Engineer during the build-up (growth) phase, then spend one Profession Change Certificate to switch to War Leader for the war phase (Desert Storm, Capitol Conquest, SvS, Faction Duel).

Can I skip skills in the seasonal column?

No — the seasonal column is a single linear chain, so you must take each skill to unlock the next (even the cosmetic ones). It all refunds at season end, so spend down the chain freely.

Do I need an Exclusive Weapon to raise skills?

Skills cap at level 30 without an Exclusive Weapon; levels 31–40 require EW (the cap is 30 + floor(EW/3)).

Does the permanent tree change each season?

No — the permanent Engineer and War Leader trees are the same every season (30 skills each, two per five levels up to Lv 70). Only the level cap and the seasonal column change.

Notes

  • Assign levels are a recommended priority build, not a hard rule — tune to your alliance's war schedule and your spending. Max-first ≈ your Lv 70 budget; the rest needs Skill-Point consumables.
  • Combat Experience and Professional Insights are seasonal-only — they are not in the permanent tree; they appear only while the season is active.
  • Source: both permanent trees were captured directly in-game (S2, Lv 70) and every skill, level and max double-checked in-game — game-accurate (30 skills each, two per 5 levels). Assign levels and priorities are a recommended build — confirm point totals in your client.
