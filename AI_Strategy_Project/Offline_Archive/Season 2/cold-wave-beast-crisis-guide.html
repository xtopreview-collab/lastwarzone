Cold Wave & Beast Crisis Guide (Season 2)

Season Events · 2026-03-24

Two Events, One Survival Test

Cold Wave and Beast Crisis are Season 2's mandatory survival events. Cold Wave drops server-wide temperatures and threatens to push your base below the -20°C lockout threshold. Beast Crisis sends waves of enemies to destroy your Alliance Furnace — one of your most important heat sources. When they happen simultaneously, unprepared alliances face cascading failures that can knock them out of competition for days.

These events are not optional side content. They are direct threats to your ability to play the game.

The Cascade Failure

Cold Wave drops the temperature. Beast Crisis destroys your heat source. Lose the Alliance Furnace during a Cold Wave and your entire alliance can fall below -20°C simultaneously. Below -20°C, you cannot rally, cannot teleport, and cannot fight back. This is the worst-case scenario in Season 2 — a cascading lockout that takes days to recover from. Everything in this guide exists to prevent it.
