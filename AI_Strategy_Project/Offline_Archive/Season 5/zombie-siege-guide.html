Zombie Siege (Season 5)

Alliance · 2026-03-24

Zombie Siege in Season 5

Check Whether It Is Running At All

The game has carried a notice that Zombie Siege is temporarily suspended during Season 5, returning in the S5 off-season with reworked rewards. If that applies on your server, treat everything below as preparation for its return rather than a plan for the season itself — and check the in-game event calendar before building a schedule around it.

Season 5 Zombie Siege features the strongest defensive squads in the game's history. T11 troops, the full Exclusive Weapon lineup, and Caffeine VR buffs combine to make your alliance dramatically more capable. If your alliance is not pushing the highest available difficulty, you are leaving significant rewards on the table.

For the full event mechanics, see the Zombie Siege Guide.

Push Maximum Difficulty

Season 5 gives you the strongest defensive tools ever. Murphy with Mitigation Master, T11 troops, Caffeine VR buffs, Scarlett UR, and four new Exclusive Weapons mean your garrison defence is at its peak. If you cleared high difficulty in Season 4, push 2-3 levels higher in Season 5. The additional rewards compound with the CrystalGold economy.
