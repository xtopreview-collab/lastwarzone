Alliance Train (Season 5)

Alliance · 2026-03-24

Alliance Train in Season 5

Season 5 introduces the Trade Train as a major map mechanic, but the regular Alliance Train still runs as a daily alliance event. These are two separate systems and it is important not to confuse them.

For the full Alliance Train mechanics, see the Alliance Train Guide.

Alliance Train vs Trade Train — They Are Different

The Alliance Train is the daily alliance event you have been running since pre-season. You board wagons, set guards, complete deliveries, and earn Alliance Gift rewards. The Trade Train is a Season 5 map mechanic where you load goods (Whiskey) onto trains that travel map routes and can be raided by other players. Both run simultaneously. Both are important. Do not skip one thinking it is the other.
