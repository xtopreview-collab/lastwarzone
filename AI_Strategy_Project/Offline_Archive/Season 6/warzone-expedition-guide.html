Warzone Expedition Guide

Season Mechanics · 2026-05-16

What Warzone Expedition Is

Warzone Expedition is Season 6's cross-warzone PvP event. Starting in Week 3, your alliance can freely teleport between other warzones in the same season zone to invade their land and capture their strongholds — while simultaneously defending your own warzone against the same incursions.

It is fundamentally outposts-then-fortresses-then-cities: build a forward outpost in enemy territory, use it as a staging ground to capture fortresses, then declare war on cities. Everything else is execution discipline around those three steps.

This Is Not Global Expedition

Warzone Expedition is a completely different event from Global Expedition despite the similar name:

  • Warzone Expedition (this guide) — PvP, cross-warzone, Week 3 start. Outposts and fortresses. Daily caps on captures. Requires alliance coordination and military commitment.
  • Global Expedition ([see guide](/guides/season-6/global-expedition-guide/)) — PvE, solo auto-battle, Weeks 2/4/6. Awakening Shards. No PvP element.

If you came here looking for Awakening Shards, you want the Global Expedition guide. If you came here looking for the PvP outpost-and-fortress mechanic, you're in the right place.
