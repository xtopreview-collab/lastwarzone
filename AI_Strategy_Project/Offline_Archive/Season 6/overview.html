Season 6 Overview: Lost Rainforest

Season Overview · 2026-04-24

What Is Season 6?

Season 6, titled Lost Rainforest (also referred to as Shadow Rainforest in some translations), is the current endgame season in Last War: Survival. It is the biggest strategic shift since Season 1 — introducing faction warfare, a permanent City Destruction mechanic, the Altars strategic system, and the Hero Awakening progression system that replaces Exclusive Weapons as the season's headline feature.

Why Season 6 Is Different

Earlier seasons scaled up your army and added new mechanics on top of existing maps. Season 6 rewrites the strategic layer: your alliance picks a faction, alliance skills now matter as much as hero skills, and enemy cities can be permanently destroyed for the rest of the season. If your alliance is not coordinated, S6 punishes you harder than any prior season.
