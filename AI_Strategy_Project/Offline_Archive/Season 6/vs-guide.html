Alliance Duel VS Guide (Season 6)

Alliance · 2026-04-25
Season 6 Context

The core mechanics in this guide are largely unchanged from prior seasons. Season 6 (Lost Rainforest) layers faction warfare and the new progression systems on top — see the Season 6 Overview, Factions Guide, Altars Guide, and City Destruction Guide for what's new. Hero Awakening for Kimberly (W1), DVA (W4), and Tesla (W7) is covered in the Hero Awakening Guide.

VS in Season 6

Alliance Duel VS follows the same 6-day structure, but Season 6 creates the hardest resource trade-offs VS has ever demanded. You are now splitting resources between EW Lv20+ progression (Awakening prerequisite), Awakening Shard accumulation, VS saving (Day 5 speedups and Day 4 hero items), and your general progression. The War Merit economy adds a financial dimension to every VS decision.

For the full day-by-day mechanics, see the Alliance Duel VS Guide.

The Season 6 VS Dilemma

Every resource you save for VS is a resource not pushing your EW Lv20+ progression or Awakening Shard accumulation. Speedups saved for Friday cannot accelerate your priority hero's EW level mid-week. Hero items saved for Thursday cannot boost your EW progression. Season 6 forces trade-offs previous seasons never demanded. The answer is not to skip VS — the rewards are too good. The answer is to be disciplined about exactly what you save and what you spend.
