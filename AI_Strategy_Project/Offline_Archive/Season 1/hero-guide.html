What Changes in Season 1

Season 1 brings several major hero changes that shift the tier list:

  1. Mason upgrades from SSR to UR — becomes a legitimate A-tier Tank DPS
  2. Exclusive Weapons become widely available — heroes with strong weapons jump in value
  3. Aircraft meta dominance — Aircraft heroes increase in priority
  4. Multi-squad building begins — investment priorities must account for second squads

For the core hero mechanics (rarities, skills, star upgrades, Tactics cards), see the Pre-Season 1 Hero Guide. This guide focuses on what changes in Season 1.
