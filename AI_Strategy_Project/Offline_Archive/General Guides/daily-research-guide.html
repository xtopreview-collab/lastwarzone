Daily Research Routine — Tech Centre + Alliance Tech Priority

Core Mechanics · 2026-04-25

Why Research Routines Matter

Research is the slowest progression system in Last War: Survival. A skipped research day cannot be recovered — every hour your Tech Center sits idle is permanent progress lost. The same applies to alliance tech donations: you have a daily cap, the cap resets, and missed days are gone forever. The difference between a player who runs a daily research routine and one who does not is roughly 10-15% account power per month in compounded gains.

Two Research Systems, One Routine

Personal Tech Center research and alliance tech donations are independent but should run on the same daily check. Tie them together: every time you log in, check both. The five-minute habit compounds across months. This guide gives you the daily and weekly checklist for both.

The Daily Five-Minute Routine

Every login (minimum once per day):

  1. Check Tech Center — is research running? If not, queue the next priority node
  2. Open Alliance Tech — donate 30 times to the Recommended tech (look for the highlighted badge)
  3. Check VS day — adjust your research priority based on the day of the week
  4. Note any urgent alliance request — if officers have asked the alliance to prioritise a specific node, do that

That's it. Five minutes. The compounding return is huge.

Personal Tech Center — Daily Research Priority

Your Tech Center research never stops. Different categories matter at different stages, and certain days of the week reward specific research types.

Always-Running Rule

Never let your Tech Center queue sit empty. If you finish a research and you do not know what to queue next:

  • HQ-blocking research first — anything that gates your next HQ upgrade
  • Troop tier unlocks — always research the next tier when available
  • Construction/Research/Training Speed — the compound speed nodes always pay off
  • Combat stats for your primary squad — Tank/Aircraft/Missile attack and defence

If still unsure, see the Tech Center Research Guide for full priority order by phase.

Day-Specific Research Priorities

The Alliance Duel VS weekly cycle rewards specific research types on specific days. Plan your research queue to land completion (or speedup spend) on the matching day for bonus VS points.

DayResearch Strategy
Monday (VS Day 1)Plan the week's research. Long-burn research started today should land on Wednesday for VS points
Tuesday (VS Day 2 — Building)Research that does not block buildings. Save research speedups for tomorrow
Wednesday (VS Day 3 — Research)Burn research speedups today. Every research speedup spent today counts toward VS points. Bank speedups Mon-Tue for this
Thursday (VS Day 4 — Heroes)Run a long research that does not block hero items. Skill research is fine
Friday (VS Day 5 — Mobilisation)Research running in background. All speedups go to training, not research
Saturday (VS Day 6 — Combat)Combat research running. Healing Speed and ATK/DEF nodes preferred
SundayRecovery day. Long-burn research that completes Mon-Tue is ideal
The Wednesday Research Bank

Save every research speedup Monday and Tuesday for use on Wednesday. VS Day 3 awards points for research speedups burned that day. A speedup spent Mon or Tue is worth 1x. The same speedup on Wed is worth 1x research progress AND VS points. The discipline starts at Monday morning, not Wednesday morning.

Alliance Tech — Daily Donation Order

Every alliance member should complete 30 gold donations to the Recommended technology daily. This is non-negotiable for competitive alliances and high-return for casual ones.

Why 30 Gold Donations Daily

  • Gold donations give the highest contribution per tap — the most efficient currency
  • Recommended tech bonus (20%) stacks on top — every donation worth more
  • 30 donations × 40 members = 1,200 daily contributions — alliance tech progresses fast
  • Personal Alliance Contribution Points — used in the Alliance Shop for hero shards, skill medals, and speedups

If you skip a day, you lose the day's contributions and your alliance loses 30 contributions toward whatever is flagged. There is no makeup.

What If There Is No Recommended Tech?

If your alliance leadership has not flagged a Recommended tech, donate to Construction Speed or Research Speed by default. These compound across every member's progression and rarely cost the alliance value compared to combat tech.

If your alliance frequently has no flagged tech, ask leadership to fix it — donations without the 20% bonus waste contribution efficiency.

See the Alliance Tech Guide for Leaders for the full leader-side strategy.

Personal Research Investment Order — Quick Reference

For active research (not VS-day timing), the priority order across an entire account:

  1. HQ prerequisites — anything blocking your next HQ upgrade
  2. Next troop tier unlock — T8 → T9 → T10 → T11 in sequence
  3. Construction Speed I-V — compound builder savings
  4. Research Speed I-V — compound research savings
  5. March Capacity — every level here improves rally damage
  6. Combat stats for your primary squad — Tank/Aircraft/Missile-specific ATK/DEF
  7. Training Speed I-III — feeds waterfall training and event participation
  8. Healing Speed — late-game priority, especially during competitive seasons
  9. Season-specific research — Virus Resistance (S1), Curse Resistance (S3), the Age of Oil (after S2), etc. when seasons unlock
  10. Economic side nodes — gathering speed, resource production. Lowest priority
The Compound Speed Trap

Speed-of-X research feels boring but compounds across every future upgrade. Construction Speed III at +12% means every building upgrade for the rest of your account's life is 12% faster. Across 100+ HQ upgrades and dozens of side buildings, this is hundreds of hours saved. Players who skip these nodes for "more exciting" combat research lose more progress than they realise.

Common Research Mistakes

  1. Empty Tech Center queue overnight — wasted hours that compound across weeks
  2. Skipping Wednesday VS research speedup spending — VS Day 3 multiplies the value of every research speedup
  3. Burning research speedups Monday or Tuesday — they are worth more on Wednesday
  4. Skipping daily alliance donations — 30 donations/day is non-negotiable for free progression
  5. Donating to non-Recommended tech — loses the 20% bonus on every tap
  6. Asking leadership to flag your favourite tech — flagging is a leadership decision; donate to whatever is flagged
  7. Researching combat nodes before HQ 15 — your combat power early-game is hero levels and troop tiers, not research
  8. Ignoring Construction Speed and Research Speed nodes — they compound across hundreds of future actions
  9. Researching season-specific nodes too early — wait until the season actually opens; you cannot use the bonuses pre-season
  10. Spending gems on research speedups outside VS Day 3 — terrible exchange rate. Wait for Wednesday
