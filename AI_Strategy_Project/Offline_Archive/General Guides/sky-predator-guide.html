Sky Predator Guide

Alliance · 2026-03-13

Sky Battlefront: The Sky Predator Event

Sky Battlefront is a 7-day warzone-wide event where alliances coordinate to build and deploy the Sky Predator airship against an enemy warzone. It first appears on server day 28, and returns on days 49 and 72 during pre-season. In each numbered season it runs once, on day 49 of that season. Each boss challenge window is 10 hours. This is one of the most coordination-heavy events in the game — individual power matters less than alliance organisation.

Why This Event Matters

Sky Battlefront rewards are some of the best in the game and are unavailable elsewhere. The event tests your alliance's coordination, leadership, and discipline across an entire week. Alliances that prepare properly dominate; alliances that improvise lose.

Event Structure: Three Stages

The event runs Monday through Sunday with three distinct stages. Each stage requires different preparation and a different mindset.

StageDaysFocus
Donation StageMon–Thu (4 days)Build the Sky Predator by donating Airship Tokens
Battle StageFri–Sat (2 days)Attack the enemy warzone's airship with coordinated rallies
Calculation StageSun (1 day)Results tallied, rankings finalised, rewards distributed

Stage 1: Donation Phase (Monday–Thursday)

Platform Deployment

The Warzone President chooses a location to build the airship's starting platform at the beginning of the event. If the President does not choose a location within 12 hours, the system assigns one automatically.

Presidents: Deploy Early

Deploy the platform within the first hour. Every hour of delay is an hour your warzone loses for token donations. The location itself matters less than the speed of deployment — pick a central, accessible spot and move on.

Earning Airship Tokens

You can earn up to 50 tokens per day by completing the following tasks:

TaskTokens
Daily Login15
Complete 1 Legendary Secret Task5
Train 100 units5
Defeat 1 Doom Elite5
Complete 5 Radar Tasks5
Make 10 Alliance Tech Donations5
Send 3 Trucks5
Recruit 1 Hero5

Every member should hit 50 tokens every day. The daily login alone gives 15, and the remaining 35 come from activities you should be doing anyway. Missing even one day across your alliance adds up quickly.

Construction Milestones

Global construction is divided into three stages, each requiring 25,000 tokens from the entire warzone:

  1. Stage 1 (25,000 tokens) — Airship frame assembled. Reward chests for all participants
  2. Stage 2 (50,000 tokens total) — Airship systems online. Better reward chests
  3. Stage 3 (75,000 tokens total) — Airship combat-ready. Best reward chests

After construction completes, a Rush Phase begins where additional donations no longer build the ship but enhance its combat stats — HP, Damage, and Defence — for the upcoming battle. Tokens donated during Rush directly affect how strong your airship is in the Battle Stage.

Rush Phase Is Not Optional

Continue donating after construction completes. Rush Phase donations directly buff your airship's combat stats. An airship that barely finished construction is dramatically weaker than one with a full Rush Phase of enhancements. This is where organised warzones pull ahead.

Supply Tiles

During the Donation Phase, special Resource Tiles and Supply Tiles appear on the map with bonus effects for your alliance. Use your Recon Plane to scout and secure these tiles early — they provide free resources and bonus tokens that add up over four days.

The Rush Stage — Where the Airship Actually Gets Strong

Once donations meet the construction goal the airship is complete, and the Rush Stage opens. This is the part alliances stop paying attention to, and it is the part that decides how good your airship is:

The Airship Is Upgraded on Your Rush-Stage Peak

During the Rush Stage the airship is strengthened according to the highest donation progress reached in that stage. Construction being "finished" is not the finish line — it is the point at which further donation starts buying combat power instead of completion. Alliances that stop donating the moment the bar fills fly a materially weaker airship into the battle phase.

Airship Tokens also come from checking resource tiles and gathering, not only from daily tasks — and gathering enough grants bonus tokens on top of the base reward.

Stage 2: Battle Phase (Friday–Saturday)

Launching the Sky Predator

Only the Warzone President can select the enemy warzone target and launch the Sky Predator. The airship travels in Teleport Mode, which takes time. Upon arrival, it automatically begins dealing damage to the enemy warzone.

Simultaneously, the enemy warzone's Sky Predator is heading toward you. Your warzone must both attack the enemy airship and defend against theirs.

Rally Mechanics

Solo Attacks Are Impossible

You cannot attack the Sky Predator solo. All damage must come through Rallies. This is an alliance coordination event — solo players contribute nothing during the Battle Stage. If your alliance cannot field coordinated rallies, you will not deal meaningful damage.

Rally leaders should use their strongest squads. The specific hero compositions will vary by season — see the season-specific Sky Predator guides for recommended rally setups.

The Shield and Berserk System

This is the most important mechanic of the Battle Stage. Understanding it determines whether you win or lose.

When rally damage drops the enemy Sky Predator's HP below certain thresholds, it activates a temporary shield:

  1. Shield activates at HP thresholds (approximately 70%, 30%, and near-death)
  2. Your alliance has a limited time window to break the shield
  3. If you break the shield in time — combat continues normally
  4. If you fail to break the shield — the airship enters Berserk Mode

Berserk Mode Consequences

Berserk Mode is devastating and should be avoided at all costs:

  • Incoming damage to your alliance troops increases dramatically
  • The airship fires stronger, more frequent attacks at your bases
  • Subsequent shields become shorter and harder to break
  • Your final damage score and rewards are significantly reduced
  • Wall Durability on player bases drops rapidly
Avoid Berserk at All Costs

Triggering Berserk Mode is the single biggest mistake alliances make. A clean run without Berserk can achieve up to 20 consecutive victories. A Berserk-triggered run caps at 15 at best. Coordinate your rallies to break shields — or time your damage to skip shield phases entirely.

Rally Coordination: The 5-Second Rule

The key to shield-breaking and maximum damage output is synchronisation:

All rallies must launch within a 5-second window. This concentrates damage into a single burst that either:

  • Skips the shield trigger entirely by pushing HP past the threshold before the shield activates
  • Delivers enough burst to break an active shield within the time window

Rally Timing Strategy

ApproachWhen to Use
Pre-Shield BurstLaunch rallies just before a known HP threshold. If damage is sufficient, you skip the shield phase entirely
Shield-Break RushWhen a shield activates, immediately launch all available rallies within 5 seconds to break it before the timer expires
Staggered DamageOnly if your alliance is significantly stronger than the enemy. Spread rallies to maintain consistent pressure without triggering Berserk

Defence During Battle Stage

While you are attacking the enemy's Sky Predator, their airship is attacking your warzone:

  • The enemy airship fires at random targets, setting player bases on fire
  • Wall Durability decreases with each hit — repair walls between attacks
  • Stock up on teleports before the Battle Stage to reposition defensively
  • Weaker members should shield up if their walls are low

Shields, Rampage Mode and the Win Streak

The airship does not simply lose health until it dies:

MechanicDetail
Damage per rallyEach rally attack reduces airship HP based on the number of enemy queues you defeat, and records each commander's damage individually
Shield thresholdsAt certain HP thresholds the airship raises a shield. Break it within the time limit to carry on
Rampage ModeFail to break the shield in time and the airship enters Rampage Mode, hitting far harder until it subsides
The win streakA streak of 5 winning rounds counts as a triumph — but while the shield is up you need 20 consecutive wins instead
RetreatSky Predator airships have a challenge time limit. Undefeated, they simply retreat
Concentrate Rallies on the Shield Window

The shield turns a 5-win requirement into a 20-win one. That is the moment to have every rally slot filled and every officer leading, not the moment to take a break — a shield left standing costs you both the window and the Rampage punishment that follows.

Stage 3: Calculation and Rewards (Sunday)

On the final day, results are tallied across two ranking systems:

RankingBased On
Donation RankingTotal Airship Tokens donated during the Donation and Rush phases
Damage RankingTotal damage dealt to the enemy Sky Predator during the Battle Stage

Both individual and alliance rewards are distributed based on these rankings. HP Stage Rewards are also granted when the enemy airship's HP is reduced to 70%, 30%, and 0%.

After the enemy airship is destroyed, reward chests with trophies drop on the map. These must be collected quickly — have members ready to grab them as soon as they appear.

Pre-Event Preparation Checklist

Start preparing 2-3 days before the event begins:

  1. Stockpile Airship Token sources — have daily tasks ready to complete immediately on Monday
  2. Stock up on teleports — you will need them for defensive repositioning during the Battle Stage
  3. Identify rally leaders — your strongest players should lead rallies
  4. Establish a coordination channel — real-time communication is essential for the 5-second rally window
  5. Brief your alliance — ensure every member understands the token tasks and daily 50-token target
  6. Heal all troops — maximum army strength before the Battle Stage
  7. Repair walls — full Wall Durability going into Friday

Leadership Responsibilities

R4 and R3 members play a critical role throughout the event:

StageLeadership Action
Pre-EventBrief all members on token tasks, rally expectations, and the schedule
Donation PhaseSend daily reminders for token donations. Track participation and follow up with inactive members
Rush PhasePush for continued donations after construction completes — do not let members assume the job is done
Battle PhaseCall rally timings in real-time. Coordinate the 5-second launch window. Monitor shield HP thresholds
Post-BattleDirect members to collect dropped reward chests immediately

Common Mistakes

  1. President deploys the platform late — every hour of delay costs the entire warzone tokens and Rush Phase time
  2. Members skip daily token tasks — 50 tokens per day is not optional. Missing days means a weaker airship
  3. Stopping donations after construction completes — Rush Phase enhancements are the difference between a strong and weak airship
  4. Launching rallies out of sync — staggered rallies deal less damage and fail to break shields, triggering Berserk Mode
  5. Triggering Berserk Mode — poor rally coordination is the number one reason alliances lose this event
  6. Ignoring defence during the Battle Stage — the enemy airship hits your bases too. Repair walls and shield weak members
  7. Not collecting reward chests — chests drop on the map after the enemy airship is destroyed. If nobody picks them up, the rewards are lost
  8. No real-time communication — this event cannot be played passively. Alliance chat or an external coordination channel is mandatory during the Battle Stage
The Bottom Line

Sky Battlefront is won by the most organised alliance, not the strongest individual players. A disciplined alliance of average-power players will beat a disorganised alliance of whales every time. Preparation, daily token discipline, and rally coordination are everything.
