Presidential Buffs Guide

Progression · 2026-03-18

What Are Presidential Buffs?

When an alliance wins the Capitol Conquest event, their leader becomes the President of the server. The President appoints a Vice President, and together they assign Ministry positions (often called "hats") that grant powerful buffs to individual players.

These buffs are one of the best ways to accelerate your progression — and they are available to everyone on the server, not just the winning alliance.

Key Fact

You do not need to fight in Capitol Conquest to receive Ministry buffs. The queue system is open to all players on the server. You just need HQ Level 16 to apply.

How the System Works

  1. The alliance that wins Capitol Conquest gets to appoint a President
  2. The President selects a Vice President
  3. Both the President and VP can assign players to Ministry positions
  4. Players join a queue for the Ministry they want — assignments last 10 minutes each
  5. The next player in the queue is assigned automatically when the current term ends

Important rules:

  • You can only hold one position at a time
  • There is a 30-minute cooldown between applications
  • Ministries have a 5-minute cooldown between reassignments
  • Buffs only apply to new actions started during your term — ongoing builds, research, or training are not affected

Conqueror Tip

The Administrative Commander position is extremely powerful — +60% to both construction and research. If your server just won Capitol Conquest, this is the best hat to grab for progression.

Applying for a Position — The Rules Nobody Reads

RuleDetail
Base level 16You cannot apply for any position below Base level 16
One at a timeYou may hold only one position, and you cannot apply for another while holding one
Applications expireAfter 24 hours. An application you forgot about is not still in the queue — reapply
Fifty and fullEach position accepts 50 applications, and the auto-appointment list holds 50 commanders. Once either is full, nothing more is accepted
Apply with careThere is a 30-minute wait between applications, so a misfired application costs you half an hour
Wiped by ConquestWhen Capitol Conquest starts, every application is wiped and cannot resume until a new president is in office
Reapply After Every Conquest

Applications do not survive Capitol Conquest. If you queued for a hat before the event and never got it, you are not in the queue any more — you are simply waiting for something that was cleared. Reapply once the new president takes office, and remember the 24-hour expiry on top of that.

Tips for Maximising Your Buffs

Timing Is Everything

Buffs only apply to actions you start during your 10-minute window. Plan ahead:

  1. Queue your resources — have enough resources ready before your turn
  2. Open the upgrade/research screen ahead of time so you can start instantly
  3. Do not start the action early — wait until the hat is assigned, then press start

Stack Your Bonuses

Ministry buffs stack with other speed bonuses:

  • VIP bonuses
  • Hero skills (e.g. construction/research speed heroes)
  • Alliance research buffs
  • Speed-up items (use these on top of the hat for maximum reduction)

Arms Race Strategy

During Arms Race events, time your Ministry hat for the "Focus" phases. Starting a big upgrade with a +50% construction hat during an Arms Race construction phase gives you points and saves time.

Ask for Extended Holds

For major milestones like HQ 25+ or late-game research that takes days, ask your VP for an extended assignment. Many alliances allow longer holds for high-value upgrades — just communicate in alliance chat.

Common Mistakes

  1. Starting a build before the hat is assigned — the buff will not apply retroactively. Wait for confirmation
  2. Forgetting the 30-minute cooldown — plan which hat you need most, you cannot switch quickly
  3. Ignoring the Interior hat — the +100% resource production adds up significantly over time
  4. Not checking the queue — if 50 people are ahead of you, pick a less popular Ministry instead
  5. Opening resource chests while holding Interior — the Interior hat boosts base production, not inventory items
