Heroes & Squads · 2026-03-24

Tanks in Season 3: The Scarlett Era

Season 3 brings the biggest Tank shake-up since Mason's UR promotion. Scarlett upgrades from SSR to UR, giving Tank squads a powerful new defensive hero. Marshall receives his Exclusive Weapon, boosting the entire squad's support capability. Combined with Curse Resistance gating PvE content and Goldust Conquest demanding strong PvP squads, Tank players have a lot to process.

Season 3 Reality

Tanks are back in a strong position. Scarlett's UR promotion gives you a genuine roster decision — she is a viable alternative to Stetmann and potentially even fits alongside Mason. Marshall's Exclusive Weapon elevates your support ceiling. And the Goldust Conquest format rewards durable squads that can hold garrisons. Tanks are no longer just PvE specialists — they are Goldust Conquest defenders.
