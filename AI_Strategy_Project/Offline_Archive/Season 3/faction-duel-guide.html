Faction Duel Guide (Season 3)

Season Events · 2026-03-24

The Climax of Season 3

The Faction Duel is the final event of Season 3 — a seven-day gauntlet that culminates in a winner-take-all battle between the two factions. Everything you have built since Day 1 — Curse Resistance, Desert Protectors, Goldust reserves, alliance coordination — gets tested here. This is not a bonus event. This is the event.

Week 7 Is Not When Preparation Starts

The Faction Duel happens in Week 7, but it is won or lost in Weeks 1-6. Alliances that spent six weeks maximising CR, stockpiling Desert Protectors, practising Goldust Conquest coordination, and building faction relationships will dominate. Alliances that treated the first six weeks as warmup will be eliminated in the qualifying rounds.
