Season Overview · 2026-04-25

How Season 4 Plays Out

Season 4 is the most punishing season for uncoordinated alliances. Darkness penalties bleed your account 24/7, three daily Blood Night windows force schedule discipline, Tactics Cards add a new optimisation layer, and the Copper War starting Week 4 determines your final ranking. Season 4 runs roughly 8-10 weeks with the off-season delivering T11 troops as the closing prize.

Season 4's Three Disciplines

Power, Schedule, and Copper. Your Lighthouse must be powered (zombie capture → Generator → electricity). Your daily plan must respect Blood Night windows (02:30, 10:30, 18:30 server time). Your Week 4+ strategy must commit to Copper War. Skip any one and you lose the season — these three disciplines are not negotiable.

Your climb is gated by what you can beat

Bosses, sieges and events decide how fast you move. Enter the heroes you own and make sure the squad you bring to them is the strongest five you have.
