Sky Predator (Season 4)

Alliance · 2026-03-24

Sky Battlefront in Season 4

By Season 4, your alliance has T11 troops, fully levelled Exclusive Weapons, mature gear sets, and Tactics Cards. This means rally damage is at an all-time high and shield-break coordination can be more aggressive than ever. The main Season 4 challenges for Sky Predator are scheduling around Blood Night and managing alliance attention between Sky Battlefront, Copper War, and Lighthouse duties.

For the full event mechanics, see the Sky Predator Guide.

Season 4 Sky Battlefront

Your rallies hit harder than ever, but your alliance's attention is more divided than ever. T11 Assault Raiders, Tactics Cards, and Exclusive Weapons combine for massive damage output. The challenge is getting everyone online, focused, and coordinated when Blood Night, Copper War, and Generator fuel duties are all competing for time.
