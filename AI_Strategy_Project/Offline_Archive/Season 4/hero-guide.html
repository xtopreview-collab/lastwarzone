Hero Guide (Season 4)

Heroes & Squads · 2026-03-24

What Changes in Season 4

Season 4 is the first season since launch with no new UR promotions and no new Exclusive Weapons. The hero roster is stable. What changes is how existing heroes interact with two new systems:

  1. T11 Troops — Armored Troopers vs Assault Raiders change which heroes benefit most from the stat specialisation
  2. Tactics Cards — additional stat customisation creates new synergies with hero skills and Exclusive Weapons

For core hero mechanics, see the Pre-Season 1 Hero Guide. This guide focuses on Season 4 changes.

Season 4 Hero Reality

No hero becomes stronger or weaker in a vacuum this season. The tier list shifts because Tactics Cards amplify certain hero mechanics more than others, and T11 troop specialisation rewards heroes who lean clearly into offence or defence. Heroes with multiplicative scaling — like DVA and Kimberly — gain more from Tactics Cards than heroes with flat bonuses.
